Could sdd resolve, with the project's configured LLM and without an owning agent, the sync conflicts a git-synced graph repository still meets after the entry merge driver? The driver settles both sides rewriting an entry's title or summary, the conflict the summary-rewrite gap (20261030-083758-s-tac-5ky) named. Left are the rare rewrites of existing files: `sdd rewrite` changing an entry's type and the refs pointing at it, `sdd lint --fix`, schema migration, and edits to config or `.gitattributes`. Each pauses automatic sync until the owning agent merges by hand (20261028-201909-d-tac-fxp).

The question holds only for graph repositories synced by git between local clones. Hosted SDD most likely writes every entry through one server, so its participants never hold diverging copies; this is inferred from its tool surface and the shared application runtime, not verified, since its connector could not be reached.

Open: which conflicts an LLM may resolve (entry bodies are immutable, so likely only frontmatter, refs and config); how a resolution is checked before it is committed; what happens when no LLM is configured; whether these conflicts occur often enough to outweigh the agent-owned fix.
