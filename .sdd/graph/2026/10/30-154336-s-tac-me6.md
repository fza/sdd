A capture can take its body from a staged file: commit `64f4cd91` on branch `pr/feat-staged-body` adds the report field `bodyHandle`, returns the sha256 of the raw staged bytes from `stage_attachment`, and binds the playback confirm to that file.

All eight acceptance criteria of the staged-body directive (20261030-120506-d-tac-l2b) hold, each covered by a procedure test over the real application (`internal/proctest/capture_staged_body_test.go`) or an MCP test (`TestStagedAttachmentHashesRawBytes`). The hash directive (20261030-121523-d-tac-isy) and the field directive (20261030-122829-d-tac-1nq) hold as decided.

Choices made within these decisions:
- Layer abbreviations compare as their full names, and header lists compare regardless of order.
- A header key the report does not state yet is compared once reported.
- A staging whose header contradicts a capture bound to the handle is refused before anything is staged.
- Reporting `body` while a handle is bound is refused unless the handle is cleared with null.
- The engine gained `Session.Update`, a report that serves nothing, so a body written from a staged file stays in the next playback delta instead of being marked unchanged.

Each new check was broken in the code one at a time and failed its own test before being restored. Not yet merged into `pr/core` or `fza-fork`.
