Five defects remain in staged-file capture bodies as of commit `64f4cd91` on `pr/feat-staged-body`, found by the evaluation 20261030-160856-s-tac-dn7 against the staged-body directive 20261030-120506-d-tac-l2b:

1. High: a re-stage after playback was served but before the confirm lets the confirm bind a body nobody was shown. The body written from the re-staged file serves nothing, and the confirm binds whatever the state holds when it arrives, against the directive's rule that a re-stage after playback serves playback again.
2. High: a chooser answer naming `bodyHandle` writes the new body before the engine validates the answer. A refused answer (an option collecting no fields, a chooser not pending) leaves the switched body behind, and the next confirm writes it unseen.
3. Low: a re-stage of a bound handle during an unfinished write replaces the staged file and then reports failure.
4. Low: a byte order mark at the start of a file without a header lands in the entry body.
5. Low: a re-stage after the entry was written still rewrites that capture's state and reports it as bound.
