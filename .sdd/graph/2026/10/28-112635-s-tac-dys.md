An actor signal can now say whether its participant is a human being or a machine, closing 20261028-112404-s-tac-t3m, in commits `373000a5`, `f2ea94ae` and `83d6a78b` on branch `pr/feat-actor-kind`.

The optional frontmatter field `actor_kind` takes `human` or `machine`; absent means unknown, never human. `sdd new --actor-kind` sets it on an actor capture, the MCP capture collects it as `actorKind`, and `sdd show` prints it. The actor kind's authoring fact asks for it.

Pre-flight refuses a supersede that changes an `actor_kind` already declared in the actor's identity chain (the actor entries linked by supersedes), with finding `actor-kind-changed`. Setting it on a chain that never carried it is allowed. When one entry supersedes the heads of several actor chains, the check covers every chain it supersedes.
