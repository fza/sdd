Up to release v0.18.0-alpha.2, an entry written with `sdd new` after its pre-flight findings were settled in a `--dry-run` pass is recorded the same way as one nobody checked, and `sdd show` does not display that record at all.

Expected, as a natural expectation: an entry's frontmatter tells a reader whether a checker reviewed it, and a capture whose findings were already settled in a dry run is distinguishable from one that dodged review. `sdd show`, where a reader meets the entry, displays that record.

Actual, in v0.18.0-alpha.2: after a clean `--dry-run`, the real `sdd new` either runs pre-flight a second time or passes `--skip-preflight`, which stamps `preflight: skipped` and prints a warning. No value says the findings were settled. The `preflight` field is parsed from frontmatter but `sdd show` leaves it out of the entry it prints.
