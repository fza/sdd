A re-run of `sdd init` now commits what an earlier run's failed commit left uncommitted: commit `c11d9312` on branch `pr/fix-init-uncommitted-record`, merged into `pr/core` and `fza-fork`. It closes the directive 20261030-180738-d-tac-f1k and the gap 20261029-143251-s-tac-kic.

## Acceptance criteria

- A re-run after a refused commit commits the first run's files and exits 0: `TestInitCompletesTheCommitAnEarlierRunLeftUndone`.
- A re-run refused again exits with an error and keeps the record: same test.
- A successful commit deletes the record: same test, and `TestInitDropsRecordedPathsCommittedMeanwhile` for a record with nothing left to commit.
- The user's own uncommitted edit to `.gitignore` stays out of the retried commit: `TestInitLeavesTheUsersOwnEditsOutOfARetriedCommit`.
- A refused commit in the project repository of a split setup is completed by a re-run from the work repository: `TestInitCompletesARefusedCommitInTheProjectRepository`.

A recorded path is committed only while `git status` reports a change under it, so paths committed or reverted by hand drop out; `TestUncommitted` covers that query. Six guard breaks (merging the record, writing it, filtering it, deleting it after a commit and when nothing is left, the status check) each failed a test on its own assertion.
