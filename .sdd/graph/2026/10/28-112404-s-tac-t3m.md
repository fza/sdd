Up to release v0.18.0-alpha.2, an sdd actor signal says who a participant is only in prose, so no tool can tell whether the participant is a human being or a machine.

Expected, as a natural expectation: a fact a tool acts on sits in a frontmatter field, as an entry's kind, layer and participants do. Whether a participant is a human being is such a fact for any check that assigns work to a person rather than an assistant.

Actual, in v0.18.0-alpha.2: an actor signal carries the participant's canonical name, its aliases and prose. No frontmatter field classifies the participant, and the actor kind's authoring fact asks for no such classification.
