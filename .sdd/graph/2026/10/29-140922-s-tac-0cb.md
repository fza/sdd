Commits: `cc4a480f` (RepoID, breaking), `061a8f2b` (generic walk), `bc10afb6` (serve's gate onto the walk), `fba7424d` (merge of main) with `5df5b88a` (removes recovery-notice text that remained after main removed recovery), `2d1229f2` (`repos.Cache`), `5e6c5686` (CLI closure and refresh), `40b0313c` (serve horizon and on-demand local composition), `6f2911bb` (lint index drift over the closure) with its test `f727443d`, and `1867f7e8`, `943330e6`, `f97765f6` (second review round).
