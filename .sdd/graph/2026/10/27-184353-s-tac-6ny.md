The response schema sdd sends with every title and summary call describes the title field in its own words, "the entry's subject in a few plain words" (`internal/llmops/schema.go`, the `title` field of both `summaryResponse` and `titleResponse`), instead of following `model.TitleRule`, the one source the single-source title rule delivery (20261027-183637-s-tac-soy) made for title guidance.

Providers that enforce structured output read the field description beside the prompt, so the model meets two statements of what a title is: the full rule in the prompt, and a vaguer "a few plain words" in the schema that names neither the 3 to 10 words nor the code spans. The two do not contradict each other today, but the schema wording is a hand-kept copy that the next change to the rule will not reach. Its evaluation (20261027-184209-s-tac-p7s) found it as the only title wording left outside the rule.
