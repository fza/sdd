`sdd new` now marks a capture whose pre-flight findings were settled in a prior `--dry-run` differently from a capture that bypassed pre-flight, and `sdd show` displays the mark, closing 20261028-114128-s-tac-fip, in commits `570a2e40` and `0adbe0f7` on branch `pr/feat-preflight-bypass-annotation`.

The new `--preflight-verified` flag skips the pre-flight call and stamps `preflight: dry-run-verified`, without the skip warning. `--skip-preflight` keeps stamping `preflight: skipped`; passing both is refused. The flag takes the caller's word: nothing checks that a dry run happened. `sdd show` prints the `preflight` field in the entry's frontmatter when it is set. `0adbe0f7` only reformats the capture command wiring with gofmt.
