Automatic graph sync works in real use: a separate graph repository of a client project, shared by two people on one remote, runs under `sync.mode: auto` with fork releases serving each person's engine sessions over MCP, closing the gap 20261028-004407-s-tac-rr5.

Both participants capture through agent-driven engine sessions, and sdd's own sync carries their entries to each other: the repository's history holds `sdd: merge origin/main` commits made by each of the two, between captures of both, with no conflict left for anyone to resolve. Each participant sees the other's graph movement without pushing or pulling by hand, the outcome the sync directive (20260817-162701-d-cpt-r8k) exists for. Felix Zandanel, one of the two participants, judged the run working and reported no failure from it.

Not covered by this use: timed sync's two-minute lock wait and fetch timeout under load, and a blocked sync reaching both participants at once.
