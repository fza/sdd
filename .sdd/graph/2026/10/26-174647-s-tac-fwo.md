One actor entry superseding the heads of two duplicate actor chains now joins them into one chain, shipped in commit `b5de71a2` and closing 20261026-144315-s-tac-5y1.

Chain grouping now collects every entry linked by supersedes edges in either direction, so a merge joins the chains it supersedes. Procedure chains share the grouping. At Christopher's direction the same commit removes the chain code that actor and procedure handling each kept a copy of. Both chain types embed one model type, `CanonicalChain`. The graph health lint runs one write-once helper for both, and pre-flight's write-once checks call `CanonicalChain.ReusedBy` in place of their own chain lookup.

In the merged shape the health check no longer warns, the role bound to the canonical derives active, and pre-flight raises no `actor-canonical-reused` or `procedure-canonical-reused` finding. Regression tests for actor and procedure merges at model and pre-flight level failed before the change and pass after it, per 20260416-141340-d-prc-8vh. A graph that already holds a merging entry shows one chain once it runs this version.
