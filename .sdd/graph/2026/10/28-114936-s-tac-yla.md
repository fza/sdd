Up to release v0.18.0-alpha.2, the summary prompt and its eval judge still ask for a 50 to 100 word summary with no bound on the first sentence, and brief listings print that first sentence however long it runs.

Expected, from the summary directive (20260901-112834-d-cpt-75a): the first sentence carries the entry's single most important meaning in the shortest sentence that carries it, size is bounded by a ceiling of three sentences, and no numeric length target appears anywhere in the instruction. The activity 20260901-182601-d-tac-yx6 commits to render this into the template and the judge.

Actual, in v0.18.0-alpha.2: `summary.tmpl` asks for "2-3 sentences (50-100 words)" and a first sentence that describes what the entry is, with no instruction to keep it short. The summarize eval's judge asks for "roughly 50-100 words", and its mechanical check rejects a summary under 25 words. `EntryLineBrief`, which renders list, grouped and focus views, writes the first summary sentence in full, so a paragraph-length sentence runs the line far past the terminal width.
