The `openai` provider now sends the system prompt under role `system` to every endpoint other than OpenAI's own API, closing 20261028-113104-s-tac-5ff, in commit `e430ff78` on branch `pr/feat-openai-compatible-system-role`.

The commit repoints the `go.mod` replace directive for `github.com/teilomillet/gollm` from the `networkteam/gollm` fork to `github.com/fza/gollm` at `v0.0.0-20261019164543-de340b31bd11`. That fork keeps the networkteam fork's provider-aware retry and picks the role from the configured endpoint: no `endpoint`, or one whose host is `api.openai.com`, keeps `developer`; any other host gets `system`. No sdd configuration changes. The replace directive stays until the change lands in gollm upstream.
