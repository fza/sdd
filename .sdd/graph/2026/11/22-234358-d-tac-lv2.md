The text question of 20261121-230157-d-tac-ym8 is revised: sdd never asks about a text the agent or the person wrote, asks two questions per response that must both be right to serve short, keeps four question kinds in one hardened form, and counts the compactions it catches one call late. Every other part of that plan stands, and so does the choice of the oldest held text from 20261122-105026-d-tac-wl5.

## What changes

- **No question about written text.** sdd holds the texts the agent or the person wrote and never asks about them: the draft lane, the playback, the person's words, reports and park notes. A phrase found in such a text is never used for a question about another text.
- **Two questions per response.** Each response that starts, resumes or advances a session ends with two questions, from two texts where two yield one. Where the held texts yield only one question, the response asks one and takes one answer. The next call answers both in the one `answer` argument, parted by `;`. Both must be right to serve repeated text short; `lost` or a wrong answer to either serves it in full. This replaces "exactly one question".
- **Four kinds.** The word between two phrases, the number after a phrase, the words after a phrase and the words before a phrase. The three sentence kinds go: the first words of the sentence holding a phrase, its last words, and the first words of the sentence after it.
- **One form.** The phrase and the asked words stand side by side as one single-spaced run on one line. No phrase or answer holds an arrow, a sign, a code span or an entry ID. A question line has at most 90 characters (`maxAskChars` in `internal/model/question.go`). No asked run of words ends on a function word such as "the" (d57ca2f07).
- **Late catches counted.** An answer worked out before a compaction can still be right at the next response. The `lost` or wrong answer that follows such a short serve is counted as a late catch (`engine.LateCatches`), and `sdd sessions` shows the count as `lateCatches`. Only an answer of the caller that got the short serve counts, and a miss with the text still in view counts the same way, so the count bounds the compactions caught late from above. This replaces the acceptance that sdd notices a compaction "one call late at most".

Built on `fza-next` as 7924304ad, 66137e59b, 1c6ffe59b, d57ca2f07 and aacf047b4.

## Why

The [measurement record](./22-234358-d-tac-lv2/text-question-revision-measurement.md) holds the method, every cell and the limits of the three runs below: the hardened re-run, the study of guessing and a comparison with tags. A study of guessing ran 2,040 calls of Haiku, Sonnet and Opus against the questions as first built. With no context no model answered right (0 of 60 in each of 9 cells). Every right answer without the text came from a compaction summary, by two routes: questions about the agent's own draft, which both real summaries reproduce word for word, and the question pending at the compaction, which the summary quotes with its answer (Haiku 7 of 10, Sonnet 8 of 10, Opus 0 of 10). Excluding written text closes the first route. Two questions close the rest: with two questions no response had both answered right from a summary (0 of 60 per model), while one of the two was right in 2 to 5 of 60. In context, wrong answers came where the question's sentence boundary differed from the reader's, at labels before a colon, list items, arrows and entry IDs; dropping the sentence kinds and those characters removes them.

A re-run against the hardened build, 6 real compactions and 192 forks with Sonnet and Opus as main agents:

- After compaction: 0 of 60 false right for each model (Wilson 0 to 6.0%); every one of 240 answer parts was `lost`.
- In context: Opus 92 of 93 responses right (98.9%), Sonnet 89 of 93 (95.7%), up from 74 and 72 of 81 under the first build. The 3 wrong parts were all Sonnet, all at the first answer, two of them a pair ending on "the", which the stop-word rule now avoids.
- The 3 `lost` parts with the text in view are the safe side: a miss costs one full re-serve, never a false pass.

The Haiku rows of the guessing study show the same routes; Haiku is not a main agent, so they do not carry the decision.

## Rejected

- Tags inside the served text in a plain form. In context tags were right 81 of 81 per model, but they cost 80 to 150 input tokens per serving response, and tag stripping returns wherever an agent echoes served text.
- Tags with a text question as fallback: two mechanisms to build and keep.
- One question per response with only a filter for distinctive words: it let draft questions through ("likely", an entry ID) and did not reach zero false rights.

sdd works through MCP alone: no hook, no skill and no proxy sits between agent and server, so every check has to travel in what sdd serves and what the agent sends back.

## Accepted costs

- Two question lines and two answers per call instead of one.
- A question answered from a summary is caught one call late; the count makes it visible instead of bounding it.
- In-context accuracy stays below 100%: about 1 miss in 93 responses for Opus and 4 for Sonnet, each one full re-serve.

## Not measured

- Automatic compaction; the runs used a manual compaction near 98k tokens.
- A question pending at a compaction under the hardened build: the runs paused before compacting.
- Hosts other than Claude Code.

## Settled

The revision is built; the done signal of 20261121-230157-d-tac-ym8 records the build against this revised form.
