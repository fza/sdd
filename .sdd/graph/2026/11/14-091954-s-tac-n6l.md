A deep verifier round that `sdd serve` launched dies unrecorded when the serve process exits, and the capture then waits at its verify step forever instead of failing.

## Actual

`launchDelegate` in `pkg/application/workflow_delegate.go` runs each launched round in a goroutine inside the `sdd serve` process, and the round's timeout (`verify.round_timeout`) runs in the same process. The round's outcome, a launcher session or an error, is recorded only when the round ends. When the serve process exits while a round is in flight, nothing is recorded: the round neither delivers nor fails, and its timeout dies with the process. The next `next` at the capture's waiting step (`deepWait`, or `deepResponding` for a later round) serves the wait again ("check again in 20 seconds") on every call, and the step never leaves for `deepFailed`.

Hosts stop or restart the server routinely: an MCP host reconnecting it (a Claude Code `/mcp` reconnect, for example) or a host that stops it between turns.

Observed in a Codex eval batch on `fza-fork` at 7d4e0dbb, whose harness stopped the app server, and with it `sdd serve`, after every user turn: the verifier rounds stopped after one or two model requests, and all three runs stayed at the wait step. Earlier runs got through only because the agent kept polling within one turn, so the server lived until the round ended.

## Expected

The capture procedure (20260703-094500-d-prc-cap) leaves its wait step for `deepFailed` once the delegate ended without delivering, so the agent can put a retry to the user. The delegate continuity directive 20261109-004924-d-cpt-cxw, since built and closed, accepted that sdd owns the lifecycle of the verifier CLI processes it launches. A round whose owning process is gone is such an ending, and the wait step should fail with that cause named. The follow-up build that moved launched rounds into the background (20261109-111708-s-tac-tuk) shipped them to be picked up on a later call and to fail loud there, and under 20260829-113416-d-cpt-nut a call never leaves the agent visibly retrying until it gives up.

## Candidate remedy, unsettled

The round record could carry its owner, such as the serve process's pid or a heartbeat, so a later call sees a round whose owner is gone and serves `deepFailed` naming the cause.
