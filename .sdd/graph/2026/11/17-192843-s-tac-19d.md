- **A draft larger than a page reached the verifier cut.** The reading's lead (the draft, what pre-flight raised, the changed passages of a revision) was placed whole on page 1, and the page's serve bound cut it, so a verifier missed a large draft's end. The lead now runs on across continued pages that open with `(continued from the page before)`, and a fence a page break splits is closed and reopened. A `Ref fit` section now states, per ref, the target's class, the chosen kind's applicability and the admissible kinds, rendered through pre-flight's own template; a target resolving in neither the graph nor a dependency says so.

Accepted limit: a draft line opening with three backticks that pairs with no other can close the split fence on the wrong page.
