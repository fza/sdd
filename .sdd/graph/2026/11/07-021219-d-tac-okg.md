An entry that already has a summary gets its title from that summary alone. The title call sends the entry's type, layer and kind, its stored summary, and the titles of its direct refs, closes and supersedes entries. It no longer sends the entry's body or the summaries of related entries.

An entry without a summary still gets title and summary from one call that reads the full entry, as it does today.

One constructor, `llmops.NewDescription(entry, graph, language, regenerate)`, decides which of the two calls an entry needs: with no summary, or with `regenerate` set, the paired call with schema `summary.json`; with a summary but no title, the title call with schema `title.json`. It returns a `Description` holding the request and the parser for its answer, so a caller can render under its graph lock and run the call outside it. `llmops.Describe` builds, runs and parses one in a single step. Every caller goes through them: entry capture in `internal/handlers`, `sdd summarize` (named entries and `--force` set `regenerate`), the capture in `pkg/application`, and the background title backfill (20261105-233807-d-tac-831). `Summarize`, `GenerateTitle`, `RenderSummaryPrompt` and `RenderTitlePrompt` are removed, and no caller picks a prompt itself.

Why: a summary's first sentence states what the entry observes, commits to or did (20260901-112834-d-cpt-75a), which is the subject a title names. On this graph's entries from October and November a whole entry averages about 3.4 KB and its summary about 0.9 KB, so the title call sends roughly a quarter to a third of today's input. The backfill runs that call on every untitled entry. With one constructor choosing the call, no caller can send the wrong prompt.

Rejected:
- Title and summary in two calls on every capture: a second round trip before the entry is written and the entry text sent twice, for no gain where the paired call already returns both.
- Only slimming the title prompt and keeping `Summarize` and `GenerateTitle`: same saving, but each caller keeps choosing between them, in three places today and a fourth with the backfill.
- Keeping the full entry in the title call: three to four times the input on every title-only call, multiplied across the backfill.

Accepted costs: a title can no longer name an identifier that appears in the body but not in the summary, and a weak stored summary gives a weak title. Every caller and its tests change together.

Acceptance criteria:
- `llmops.NewDescription` is the only place that chooses between the paired call and the title call; `Summarize`, `GenerateTitle`, `RenderSummaryPrompt` and `RenderTitlePrompt` no longer exist.
- The title-only request carries no entry body and no related summaries; a test asserts both on the rendered request.
- An entry without a summary gets title and summary from one call.
- Entry capture, `sdd summarize`, the application capture and the title backfill all go through `llmops.NewDescription`.
