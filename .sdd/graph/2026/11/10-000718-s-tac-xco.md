- 59b187bd, merged in 1263eca9: a run that changes nothing prints `sdd: everything up to date, nothing changed` and commits nothing, on the plain, `project_dir` and `graph_repo` paths; `--verbose` adds the per-surface detail; a run that writes anything, a `--verify-mode` write included, keeps its report.
