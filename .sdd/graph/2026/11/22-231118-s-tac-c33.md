The open parts of 20261121-205340-d-tac-gyk are built and merged on `fza-next` (73acbcc68, b612b30a2): the far floor's careful form, with the lowest of the three far scores as the line while no proof holds, and the background replay of past entries as whole checks, from which each graph learns its thresholds. The median form came earlier in 20261121-233522-s-tac-qcq.

## Built

- Careful form: without a proof the lowest far score of a call sets its line; the median form applies only once the rule's proof reaches the full step. The floor spares candidates within two reference hops of a referenced entry and candidates two or more reads found.
- Replay: in the background the judge scores past entries as whole checks against the entries that existed before them, the references as positives and unreferenced earlier neighbours as negatives of weight 0.25. The replayed text drops every sentence that names a referenced entry. Picks spread over topic areas and months. The set-aside and serve-in-full thresholds rest on the replayed checks.
- Background replays wait while a live check judges (e08b5035c). `sdd grounding status` names the replay phase (fbd656439); before that fix it printed a zero start time and no stage while replays ran.
- The configuration reference describes both mechanisms.

## Criteria met

All eight: `TestGather_FarFloorTakesTheFormItsStepAllows`, `TestFarFloor_Line` and the far-floor proctests (line, form, a ledger without labels); the existing spare tests (two hops, two reads); `TestAutoCalibrate_ScansWrittenEntriesThenReplaysPastOnes` and the `TestReplayLabels_*` tests (background replay and label weights); the `TestReplayText_*` tests (no naming sentence); `TestPickReplays_SpreadsOverTopicAreasAndMonths` (spread). `TestGroundingStatus_NamesTheReplayPhase` holds the status fix.

## Test run

One `sdd serve` with a scripted MCP client and the real Haiku judge, on 195 entries of this graph (126 live). Calibration labelled 327 reference pairs and reached the 0.98 stage after 140 pairs. 92 replays ran in the background in 21 minutes. A live check before any proof held 84 candidates and set none aside; once the threshold held `narrowed` on its proof (3 of 176 needed entries hidden in 92 replays, bound 0.951), the same check set 30 of 84 aside. The far floor had no proof on this graph: its far candidates held no needed entry. The replays cost $0.60 in logged Haiku calls (205 scoring and 92 hidden-entry scan calls).
