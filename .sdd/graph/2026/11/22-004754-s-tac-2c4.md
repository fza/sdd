The deep verifier's reading carries two parts whose size no decision bounds: the pages headed "Pre-flight's rules", 32 to 37 KB in the readings measured, and a block of about 1 KB for each listed entry the drafter dismissed with a verdict.

The rules text here means those pages, not the `rules` lane of the verify step, which is about 3 KB and is set apart below.

All sizes but one come from the measurement attached to 20261121-223938-d-tac-rqi, its file `shown-today.md`, and the estimates under Derived from its file `predicted-sizes.md`. That plan names both parts and states that no decision covers them. The code references are to `fza-next` at commit `3f694cab`.

## What a reading sends

A reading is the paged text a capture prepares for a fresh deep verifier (`deepPages`, `pkg/application/workflow_deep.go:582`). `model.DeepReadingPages` (`internal/model/verifier_deep.go:786`) orders it: the lead with the draft, the rules pages, then entry blocks and lines.

**The rules pages.** In a project with `verify.mode: deep` the verifier judges in place of pre-flight's model, unless a skip of the deep exchange covers the draft (`deepJudgesPreflight`, `pkg/application/workflow_deep.go:176`). Pre-flight then runs only its mechanical checks and stores the rules its model would have judged by (`pkg/application/capture.go:65-70`, `pkg/application/workflow_capture.go:143`). `preflightRules` (`pkg/application/workflow_deep.go:761`) pages that text under the heading "Pre-flight's rules", and `deepPages` puts it into the reading of every fresh verifier (line 599). `llmops.PreflightRubric` (`internal/llmops/preflight.go:149`) builds the text from pre-flight's system prompt, the check the draft's shape selects, the active contracts quoted as entry blocks (`internal/llmops/preflight.go:503-510`) and the prose verifier's axes.

The closed directive 20261110-164357-d-tac-h1e moved these rules into the verifier's reading and accepted a longer reading as a cost, without a size. 20261110-211909-s-tac-n1p delivered them as these pages. 20261114-000441-s-tac-05p removed the sections only pre-flight's model reads and kept each check's calibration section on purpose.

**The verdict briefs.** `model.PlaceVerifierReading` places every listed entry that carries a drafter's verdict as a brief (`internal/model/verifier_lanes.go:129-133`), as 20261118-173708-d-tac-euf sets it. An entry the drafter read in full stays in full. `llmops.DeepReadingBlock` (`internal/llmops/preflight_light.go:243`) renders a brief as the entry's header and its title. The header holds the ID line, the topics, one line per ref with its description and the whole summary (`writeReadingContext`, `internal/llmops/preflight_light.go:321`), the lines naming who decided or approved the entry and the reason given (`writeAgentMark`, `internal/llmops/preflight_light.go:284`), then the role line with the verdict code (`pkg/application/workflow_deep.go:689`). A closed entry whose closer the reading holds comes as its first line, role line and title only. No limit applies to the number of briefs or to the size of one.

**Not part of this gap.** Each call that serves a page also carries the `rules` lane of the verify procedure 20261109-010000-d-prc-vfy (`internal/baseprocedures/entries/20261109-010000-d-prc-vfy.md:83-105`, about 3 KB) and the report schema of `findings` (`deepFindingSchema`, `internal/engine/schema.go:555`). Both come in full unless the call proves that it holds them (`composeInstructions` and `serveReportSchema`, `pkg/mcpapp/pieces.go:89` and `pkg/mcpapp/pieces.go:207`). They are step text and report schema, whose repeats 20261121-230157-d-tac-ym8 decides. The sizes of the rules pages do not include them.

## Measured

The graph of this repository on 2026-11-21, with 1,044 live entries.

- Three readings of decision drafts, simulated over the exported functions, without scores and without verdicts. The rules pages take 34,953 of 73,862 bytes for a draft on a narrow topic, 34,953 of 82,820 for a draft with few references and 37,044 of 155,355 for a draft on a broad topic.
- Six real readings from four captures of the same day. The rules pages take 35,365 bytes in each, of 69,342 to 159,405 bytes. In three of the six they are larger than the entries served in full.
- One reading of a signal draft with references on 2026-11-22, not part of that measurement. Its rules text is 32,012 bytes before paging, the size the cut notice of the capture's dispatch step names for the stored text.
- The rules pages fill three to four pages of a reading. About 10 KB of them are the active contracts.
- A verdict brief, over all 1,873 loaded entries, has a median of 1,024 bytes, a 90th percentile of 1,748 and a maximum of 5,527. A line with title and first summary sentence has a median of 320 bytes.
- A simulated upper bound with a verdict on every listed line. The briefs take 34,519, 114,280 and 321,306 bytes, and the three readings grow to 94,629, 161,473 and 385,458 bytes. No capture was seen to do this.

## Derived

- The rules pages are 47%, 42% and 24% of the three simulated readings. They are the largest part of the first two.
- 20261121-223938-d-tac-rqi estimates the three readings at about 55, 59 and 83 KB once its decisions are built, with the rules pages unchanged. Their share then is about 64%, 59% and 45%, and they are the largest part of each.
- A verdict on a listed line adds about 0.7 KB to the reading against the same line left unanswered, at the medians.
- Under that plan a line marked as likely bearing owes a verdict or a reference, and an entry with a verdict stays a brief. Each marked line answered with a verdict then costs one brief. The plan's estimates count no verdicts.

## What it costs

The verifier runs on a strong model and must have every page served before sdd accepts its findings (`requireReadingServed`, `pkg/application/workflow_deep.go:1156`). The rules pages are nearly the same size for a draft with 35 candidates and one with 249, 34,953 and 37,044 bytes, so the narrowest reading pays the largest share. 20261121-223938-d-tac-rqi and 20261121-233807-d-tac-4iw change which entries the reading holds and in what form. 20261121-233256-d-tac-mm2 shortens framing, move-end text and repeated findings. None of them changes the rules pages or the briefs, so their share grows as the rest shrinks.

## Not known

- Tokens and money. Every size here is bytes of text. No token count of a verifier run was taken.
- How often one capture sends the rules pages more than once. By the code a revision's reading carries none (`deepRevisionPages`, `pkg/application/workflow_deep_revision.go:123`). The whole reading with its rules pages is served again when the verifier reports `reread` (`internal/baseprocedures/entries/20261109-010000-d-prc-vfy.md:33`), and when a fresh verifier starts after three revisions or after the last verifier's session ended (`deepRevisable`, `pkg/application/workflow_deep_revision.go:46-76`). Neither case is counted in real captures.
- Which hosts cache the pages. The pages reach the verifier as tool results. Whether a host bills them as cached input on the verifier's later turns is not measured for any host. 20260604-164527-d-tac-fah made pre-flight's system prompt byte-stable so that pre-flight's own calls reuse the provider's prompt cache across captures, and 20260606-125155-s-tac-sk1 delivered it with the active contracts inside that cached preamble. Nothing arranges that for the same rules served as page text.
- Which of the rules a verifier applies. 20261110-211909-s-tac-n1p records that no test compares the pages rule by rule, and no run shows which rules lead to findings.
- How many verdicts on listed lines real captures give, today or under marked lines.
- The size by entry kind. Three decision drafts and one signal draft are measured, and the kinds behind the six real readings are not recorded. No other kind is measured.
- Other graphs. The share of the contracts depends on a graph's contracts.

Confidence is medium because the expected side is not written down anywhere: no entry states how large these two parts may be. The actual side is measured for the six real readings and simulated for the three drafts.
