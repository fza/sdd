Repeated full serves seen on the live serve after the token-cost build (20261112-195218-s-tac-day) are fixed where sdd caused them and the proof guidance now covers the rest; four commits on `pr/fix-serve-repeats`, merged into `fza-fork` at f59842f2.

- 1ca9b058: a paged result's first page no longer stops at an early blank line. A gather result's page 1 held only `# to judge (N)` because the page cut at the last paragraph break that fit, and the to-judge lines are separated by single newlines; a page now ends at a blank line only when that keeps half its room, else at the last newline (`TestSplitServedFillsAChunkPastAnEarlyParagraphBreak`, `TestGatherFirstPageCarriesCandidatesToJudge`).
- cd2da7e3: `gather` with `ids` serves an entry's pointer when the call proves the agent holds it, as `show` does and as the pointer's way back already said; the exception that always served requested entries in full predated the anchor proof. A pointed entry still counts as read for grounding. `TestGather_RelevantEntryTheConsumerHoldsPrintsAsItsPointer` and `TestGatherPointsAtAnEntryAlreadyServedInFull` now assert the pointer for a proved entry and full text with its tag left out; both fail on the old code.
- 1dea1558: the type-system overview re-served at every new capture and the framing and junction re-served at every move landing were not server defects: the agent proved only the tags of its last result, so older pieces had no proof. With every earlier tag proved, a completed capture's landing is 1,003 characters of pointers. The proof guide and the `held` field now say proofs never carry over and must cover every earlier result.
- f59842f2: `TestLaunchedDelegateDispatchCarriesNoHandle` flaked under load because it polled `resume_session`, which writes to the child session, every 10 ms, and the background round retries a write conflict only once; it now polls the session store, which only reads, and still asserts once that `resume_session` reports the session ended. Reproduced failing under `-count=30 -race -cpu=1,4` before, passing after.

Each new guard was broken, seen to fail on its own assertion and restored.

Open: whether agents follow the new proof wording is not evaluated; an agent proving only its last result still receives older pieces in full. Holding back such a piece and naming its old tag would avoid that, but it changes the plan's rule that a missing proof gets full text (20261112-075604-d-tac-t4r) and is not decided. Session write conflicts are retried once when a delegate round records its result and when the delegate ends, so heavy concurrent writes can still lose a round record; `internal/proctest/capture_deep_test.go` polls `ResumeWorkflow` the same way the flaky test did.
