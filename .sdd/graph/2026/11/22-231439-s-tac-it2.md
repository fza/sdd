Kept judgements and the verifier's searches of 20261121-233807-d-tac-4iw are built and merged on `fza-next` (d916fb9b5, d398542cc, 1d795516e, 76a974edb, 732ad7661, a72216633): a judgement stays with its capture across draft changes, the judge gets only unjudged candidates and entries due a second look, and the verifier's reading runs the capture's searches against the final draft. The build also carries the later pages and the landed-entry listing of 20261121-223938-d-tac-rqi. Two criteria stay open: the verifier's rejudge and the check of the value "half". The plan requires that check before the change ships, so this entry leaves the plan open until a later done signal records the check's result.

## Built

- Kept judgements (d916fb9b5): every later check and the verifier's reading of a capture use its judgements. A second look comes for a low judgement that a new check ranks among the first 20 of the merged order, whose topic the draft gains, or that sits one reference step from a reference the draft gains. When fewer than half of the 20 entries nearest the judged draft are among the 20 nearest the current draft, every low judgement is judged again and the current draft becomes the judged one. No rule judges again an entry placed as likely bearing.
- Rejudge: the drafter's `gather` takes `rejudge` (CLI `--rejudge`), which judges every candidate of that check again.
- Verifier searches: the reading runs the draft-text search, every phrase and term the capture's checks passed, and the final draft's topic listings, and the merged order places them.
- Without an embedding model a check runs the keyword searches and topic listings on a graph of any size, says that no search by meaning ran, and passes the gate. `verify.brief_all_below` loads, has no effect and shows as ignored; `sdd config set` refuses it (1d795516e).
- Later pages of a check are built when asked and credited per served page (d398542cc).
