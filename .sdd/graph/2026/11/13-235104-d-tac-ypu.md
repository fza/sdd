The deep verifier's reading drops lossless overhead now, and once the prefilter's recall bound holds it shows entries scoring below gather's calibrated set-aside cutoff as one line each; the reranker prefilter becomes the one relevance judge, and gather's similarity stage retires.

## Problem

After the serve token-cost round (20261112-195218-s-tac-day), `gather` and the deep verifier remain the largest serves. One `gather` call served 35.5k characters over 3 pages; 75 percent were one-line "to judge" lines from topic listings. The capture's grounding gate (20261109-004506-d-cpt-udz) never checks those lines: it requires a ref or a verdict only for entries served in full. The verify procedure (20261109-010000-d-prc-vfy) reads the draft and its grounding reading in pages before it judges. One such reading ran to about 27 pages: the draft, a 26.5k-character rubric and 175 entry blocks of about 1,464 characters each, 53 percent of them summary and 27 percent refs. Verifier subagents page through tens of results before they start judging.

## Lossless trims

These are built:

- Pages are packed.
- An entry's header puts ID, kind and status on one line; the ID already carries type, layer and time.
- Briefs carry no role line.
- A done's status is one word.
- A closed entry's brief sits beside its closer as ID, kind, closer and brief.
- The verifier rubric, which 20261110-164357-d-tac-h1e moved into the verifier's brief, renders from pre-flight's templates without the sections only pre-flight uses; contracts stay verbatim.

On three real readings they cut 9 to 12 percent of the characters.

## Sorting the verifier's reading

The verifier sorts its listed entries with `gather`'s configured prefilter judge (`llm_prefilter`; the Cohere reranker in the measured replay), under the set-aside cutoff the prefilter calibrates itself (D33 of 20261109-004506-d-cpt-udz): an entry may be hidden only once the 95 percent Wilson lower bound of recall reaches 0.98. The verifier and `gather` share that calibration. Until the bound holds, the verifier's reading hides no listed entry; only the trims above apply.

Once it holds, these stay full blocks: declared targets, entries read in full, entries the drafter gave verdicts, entries one ref hop from the draft's refs, entries of the draft's kind on its narrowest topic, unscored entries, and entries at or above the set-aside cutoff, including those between the set-aside and the relevant cutoff. Every other listed entry shows as one line with ID and title and still counts as listed. 3 percent of hidden entries return blind as full blocks for audit (D36).

`gather` itself folds nothing: once calibrated, its prefilter already sets aside the entries below the same cutoff.

## One judge

`gather`'s calibrated similarity stage (`pkg/application/cosine.go`, delivered in 20261109-054916-s-cpt-mn7) retires. The reranker prefilter is the one judge everywhere, with one calibration and one label set. Similarity matching is too vague to serve as a judge. The embedder stays for search.

## Stored scores

The relevance ledger already stores each score per pair, judge and direction (20261109-004506-d-cpt-udz). The verifier's sorting and the replay seed read stored scores first and score only pairs the ledger lacks, so a pair is paid for once.

## Labels

- Reader labels gain a source: `live` or `replay`.
- 159 reranker-scored positives replayed from 71 past captures seed the calibration with source `replay`, removable by that source.
- The bases of the verifier's findings become `live` positives.
- A hidden entry the verifier opens counts as a miss in the D36 drift window, under the same rule of 2 percent over the latest 200 audit labels, only when a verifier finding rests on it. An opening without such a finding goes to a separate counter in `sdd grounding status` and has no drift effect.

## Measured basis

A replay of 71 captures, with 123 listed entries per capture on average: at a 98 percent recall target the reranker hides about 24 of 123 entries per capture and draft cosine about 9; at 95 percent, 37 against 15; at 90 percent, 53 against 32. The replay covered 8,735 pairs: 2,500 newly scored, 6,079 already stored in the ledger and 156 over the size limit. The reranker bills per search unit, one query with up to 100 documents: 71 calls took 118 to 140 units, about $0.25, or about 0.35 to 0.4 cent per capture of about 123 entries.

## Rejected alternatives

- A fixed top K. It carries no recall claim.
- A 95 or 90 percent target for the verifier alone. It needs a second calibration and hides up to 5 or 10 percent of relevant entries.
- The relevant cutoff as the verifier's cutoff. It carries no recall claim and breaks the 98 percent target.
- Folding `gather`'s lines between the two cutoffs. That band carries no recall claim.
- Draft cosine as the judge. It hides about 8 percent of listed entries, against about 20 percent for the reranker.
- Cosine first, then the reranker. It needs two calibrations, and entries cosine misses are never reranked.
- Keeping the similarity stage as a free first pass in `gather`, or using both judges in the verifier. Similarity matching is too vague to serve as a judge.
- Every opening of a hidden entry as a miss. Routine checks would widen the cutoff and erode the saving.
- A separate drift window for openings with its own threshold. It is a second rule with no data to set it.
- No seeding. More than 100 captures would pass before any saving.
- Seeding without a source field. The seed could not be undone.
- A shared table of ref lines. 633 of 641 ref lines are distinct, so it saves about 2 percent.
- Trims only. They were estimated at about 20 percent and measured at 9 to 12 percent.

## Accepted costs

- Sorting saves nothing until the calibration holds.
- Every `gather` candidate goes to the paid reranker; calls cost about 0.35 to 0.4 cent per capture.
- A hidden entry shows only its title unless audited or opened.
- Replay labels lean toward entries the structural rules already keep.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The deep reading has packed pages, one-line headers with ID, kind and status, no role line for briefs, one-word done status, closed briefs beside their closer, and a rubric rendered from pre-flight's templates without pre-flight-only sections, with contracts verbatim.
- [ ] Until the shared recall bound holds, the verifier's reading hides no listed entry.
- [ ] Once the bound holds, the verifier's reading shows full blocks for declared targets, full reads, drafter verdicts, one ref hop from the draft's refs, same-kind entries on the narrowest topic, unscored entries and entries at or above the set-aside cutoff, and one ID-title line for every other listed entry, still counted as listed.
- [ ] 3 percent of hidden entries come back blind as full blocks.
- [ ] `gather` runs no similarity stage: every candidate goes to the reranker prefilter, and the embedder still serves search.
- [ ] The verifier's sorting and the replay seed take a pair's stored score for the judge before calling it, and store every new score per pair, judge and direction.
- [ ] Reader labels carry a source; the replay seed is removable by source; the verifier's finding bases count as live positives.
- [ ] A hidden entry the verifier opens counts as a drift miss only when a verifier finding rests on it; other openings show in a separate counter in `sdd grounding status`.
