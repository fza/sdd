The delegate dispatch handoff on `fza-fork` lacks two of its harness-side pieces: no per-harness launch configuration ships, and dispatch does not ride the chooser answer that starts it. The child-session build (20261109-054916-s-cpt-mn7) exposes the launch through the `DelegateLauncher` seam of the application options and through `verifier.launch`, and a harness without a continue mechanism relaunches a fresh delegate each round with the same child handles; no subagent definition with an MCP-only tool allowlist is shipped per harness, and the engine forks the child on a separate call rather than in the answering one.

Expected, from the dispatch handoff directive (20260829-122157-d-cpt-zpe): per-harness launch configuration, a sub-agent definition granting the engine's MCP tools, through which a harness can also continue the same delegate across rounds; and a dispatch costing the parent one engine call beyond its normal walk, the child forked in the call answering the chooser.

Candidate, unsettled: ship the Claude Code and Codex subagent definitions from `sdd init` beside the skill renders.
