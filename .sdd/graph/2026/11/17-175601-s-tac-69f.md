The agent decision marks plan (20261117-131703-d-tac-xyv) is built on `next/feat-agent-authorship`, merged into `fza-next` at 746a2944 and released in v0.18.0-alpha.3.fza.49, so the plan closes; each criterion has a test that failed before its change.

Auto-mode entries carry `decided-by: agent`, `run-by` and, on decisions, the agent's `reason`; their participant is the MCP client's actor (20261106-174926-d-cpt-kx0's open case of agents capturing alone). A new step `agentLimit` parks what an agent may not decide alone, before any model call. A derived mark follows reasoning refs until a person's entry or an `approves:` lifecycle field on a person's settled directive stops it; view, show and the deep verifier show it with its root and distance, and catch-up lists marked chains for review. The configuration reference and the coding-agents guide state the behaviour, and entries without `decided-by` read as a person's, with no migration.

Left for follow-up: participants come from the last-attached client, not the client that started the capture; a decision confirmed in guided playback after a mode switch is not asked for user words.
