A capture parked at a timed-out pre-flight in auto mode and resumed after a switch to guided mode now reopens its playback instead of stopping at a gate nothing can pass: commit 0b9faa97 on `next/fix-mode-switch-capture`, merged into `fza-next` at 13649801 and released in v0.18.0-alpha.3.fza.49. No graph entry tracked the defect; it surfaced on a live capture and was fixed on the spot.

In auto mode with the verifier on, pre-flight runs before any playback confirmation is recorded, so a timeout parks the capture with none on record (the timeout step is 20261112-233840-s-tac-jcg). After the switch to guided, the step's guard required that confirmation and nothing led back to playback. The engine now reopens the step that records the confirmation when a held gate owes one that was never recorded, as it already did when a recorded confirmation had gone stale after a draft change. Reopening exposed a second defect: a timed-out or cut pre-flight (the cut path is 20261112-203435-s-tac-air) counted as current, so a confirmed entry could be written without judgment and without the skip mark; an unjudged pre-flight now counts only after the user's skip.

Tests failed before the fix: a proctest drives the live sequence to a written entry with three pre-flight runs, and an engine test reopens a never-recorded confirmation on a synthetic spec. A decision drafted in auto mode carries no user words, and its reopened guided playback does not ask for them, because that check (20261112-213854-s-tac-1lr) runs only at assemble.
