The fork reaches a project's whole dependency chain on every surface, as stock sdd does, and the resolver for refs beyond the direct dependencies is removed with its miss record.

## Scope

Every repository down the chain of declared dependencies is loaded, searchable and usable as a session's project. This is the horizon of 20261026-194528-d-cpt-gcq again, the transitive declared closure, computed as upstream computes it (`model.DependencyClosure`). It holds for the surfaces 20261113-235859-d-cpt-l3m had narrowed:

- CLI search and view;
- the MCP reads `search` with `repos` or `all_repos`, and `show`, `view`, `read_attachment` and `info` with `project`;
- moves, `start_procedure` with `project`;
- export and lint;
- the dependency refresh and `sdd repo sync`.

What a new entry may reference, and bare-ID resolution, stay bounded to the direct dependencies, as 20261026-194528-d-cpt-gcq keeps them and as stock does.

## What goes

The resolver for refs beyond the direct dependencies (`model.FurtherResolver`) and the miss record (`internal/repos/ref_misses.go`) lose their purpose. With the whole chain loaded, a ref a dependency holds into a repository further out resolves like any other ref. The fetch on a miss goes with the resolver.

The three warning kinds for an unresolved reference (`not-connected`, `missing-after-fetch`, `fetch-deferred`), their exported type in `pkg/application/types/unavailable_ref.go` and the reason on the `[unresolved: repo X]` marker are removed with the resolver. The marker reads as in stock. A missing connection is named by the CLI's warning per read and by the MCP server's refusal of an all-repositories read, as stock does today, until the per-reference rules of 20261029-135448-d-cpt-olh replace that refusal. Rejected: keeping "not connected" as a reason on the marker.

The MCP server instructions, the tool descriptions and the docs that state the direct-only rule change with it.

## Why

The fork is this repository's own line of sdd, built on `fza-next` beside upstream (20261117-092355-d-prc-3vb). Stock sdd is upstream's build. A project that relies on a chain shows less under the fork than under stock. Stock loads a repository two steps down the chain, searches it and opens a session in it. The fork refuses the same repository as no direct dependency, and shows single entries from it only where a ref leads there. The fork stays compatible with stock sdd, and this difference breaks a project that was set up with stock.

Rejected: keeping direct dependencies only, with single entries beyond them fetched on a ref.

Accepted cost: every fork machine clones and indexes every repository down the chain.

## What this replaces

20261113-235859-d-cpt-l3m narrowed the horizon to the direct dependencies and added the resolver, its fetch on a miss, three warning kinds and the recorded misses. 20261114-012455-s-tac-g52 built it. This entry withdraws the narrowing, the resolver, its fetch on a miss, the miss record, the three warning kinds and the reason on the marker, because the narrowing is a difference to stock that a project set up with stock cannot work around.

One case that l3m had removed returns with the chain. A repository further down the chain is again a member a read can select, so the open gap 20261028-155600-s-tac-4mz covers it again as it does in stock, and the per-reference rules of 20261029-135448-d-cpt-olh, still unbuilt, are again its expected side for that member.
