A capture's playback now shows when the draft bypasses the grounding gate, so the user sees before confirming that the entry will be written with confidence low and `grounding: skipped`: commit `9e198dd3` on `pr/fix-capture-eval-defects`, merged into `fza-fork` as `45cd8277`.

The grounding bypass that 20261109-054916-s-cpt-mn7 built forces confidence low at the write, but the playback's draft block left the bypass out and showed the confidence the agent reported. In a Codex eval run the user confirmed a draft reading `confidence: high`, and the entry was written low and skipped, unannounced.

- The capture procedure's playback step adds `skipGrounding` to its draft block, and its guidance tells the agent that a bypass writes the entry low and skipped whatever confidence the block shows, to be named to the user before the confirm.
- `TestCaptureGrounding_BypassWritesLowConfidence` in `internal/proctest/capture_grounding_test.go` now also requires the bypass in the playback block; it failed on that assertion before the change and passes after. The full suite passes on the fix branch, and the procedure tests pass on the merge.
