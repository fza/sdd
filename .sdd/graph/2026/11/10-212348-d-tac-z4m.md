Every LLM pre-flight finding carries a quote of the passage it is about, and sdd checks that the quote locates text in the material pre-flight's user prompt holds: the draft, the entries it carries (referenced, closed and superseded entries) and, where pre-flight runs over one, the grounding reading. A finding whose quote locates nothing is kept, but its observation opens with a note that it is unverified, and its severity is capped below blocking.

This answers the invented findings of 20261110-150803-s-tac-9u1, where a small model returned findings about text that appears nowhere and seven of them blocked a write. Since directive 20261110-164357-d-tac-h1e, deep captures no longer call the pre-flight model, but captures under `prose` or `off` and deep captures whose deep exchange is skipped still do. The check rests on the quote contract of 20261005-170145-d-tac-gch: a quote need only locate its passage, so the match tolerates changed case, quotation marks, dashes and whitespace and a quote shortened with an ellipsis, while provenance, the one thing a quote must keep, becomes a check in code instead of a prompt rule. The writing guide's quotes, which the open gap 20260901-220355-s-tac-owp found pointing outside the draft, stay outside this directive.

Rejected: dropping a finding whose quote locates nothing, since a true finding with a misquoted passage would vanish silently, against failing loud; keeping pre-flight without a guard, since prose- and off-mode projects and skipped deep exchanges would stay open to a cheap model's invented blocking findings. Accepted costs: a `quote` field in the prompt and the response schemas, the check in code and a test guarding it; a true finding whose quote locates only an active contract, which renders in the system prompt outside the check, is flagged and capped too; the drafting agent still reads and judges each flagged finding.
