Every sdd serve lists the earlier pieces the agent context has not yet proved, so an agent that tracks only its last result can still prove the text it holds, under the anchor proof of 20261112-075604-d-tac-t4r.

The token-cost build left the repeat cost for agents that send no proofs open (20261112-195218-s-tac-day). In three Codex capture runs on `fza-fork` after the proof wording said proofs never carry over (20261112-222734-s-tac-wdx), kept in the [run record](./13-121039-d-tac-ca2/proof-eval-record.md), a call proved on average 7 to 11 percent of the tags served in earlier results, about half of all calls proved only the last result's tags, and 29 to 34 percent of all result characters were pieces served in full again. No pointer named text an agent no longer had. The agents lose track of older tags, not of the text, so a reminder targets the cause while the word each proof quotes still comes from text the agent holds.

What a serve does:

- It lists, by tag id and piece name, the pieces that come back in full when unproved (instruction lanes, framing blocks, report schemas and entries read in full) which the agent context has not proved since, at most the 20 newest, with the instruction to prove each one whose text the agent still sees. A piece leaves the list once proved or served in full again. Gather brief lines stay off the list: they always serve in full.
- The list and the pointers write tag ids without the bracket form, so no word follows a tag there. The wrong proofs in the runs came from the pointer form, where a bracketed tag is followed by a dash and agents guessed a word.
- A missing or wrong proof keeps getting the full text.

Rejected:

- Holding an unproved piece back as a pointer naming its old tag, the agent rereading when it lost the text: the largest saving, but it changes the rule of 20261112-075604-d-tac-t4r, and in the spike behind that rule a pointer without proof let an agent judge 43 of 50 entries from its summary.
- Keeping serves as they are: about 30 percent of result characters stay repeats.

A capture eval of the same shape judges the reminder once built: it is met when repeated characters fall under 15 percent of all result characters, the criterion of 20261112-075604-d-tac-t4r, with no more wrong proofs than these runs.
