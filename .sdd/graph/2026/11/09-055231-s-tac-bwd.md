Configured hooks on `fza-fork` are hard to see from both sides: `sdd config` lists the hooks of the top config layer only, though hooks from the user-global and local layers both run, and a hook's `medium` and `low` findings at the capture's `assemble` and `guide` steps reach the step's findings without being served to the agent, so only a `high` one, which blocks, ever shows. The hooks build reported both (20261109-054916-s-cpt-mn7); neither was probed by hand.

Expected, from the configured hooks directive (20261109-005138-d-tac-i61): hooks from both personal layers are the configured set a person inspects, and findings joining a step are weighed like that step's other findings, which the agent is served.
