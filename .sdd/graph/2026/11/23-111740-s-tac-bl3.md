In assisted and guided mode a capture now shows the person the checks' outcome once, at the final playback: the draft as the entry file with its frontmatter, "Changed by the checks" and "Open for you". Commit `413ce0863` on `fza-next` changes the capture procedure and the capture workflow; commit `4af0dcf0d` updates the coding-agents guide and the pages on working modes, configuration and getting started.

Each acceptance criterion of 20261123-100216-d-tac-b49 is met, with no deviation. The tests named below live in `internal/proctest/capture_single_playback_test.go` and run every case in guided and in assisted mode:

- No check finding before the playback or at the summary check: `AFindingSettledWithoutAnEditIsNotShown` asserts the summary check names no such finding; the open-point tests assert the capture does not park before the playback.
- Entry file, changed sentences and open points at the playback: `ThePlaybackShowsTheEntryFileAndWhatTheChecksChanged` and `AConfirmEndsTheChangedList`; the replayed playback step is removed.
- An open point is one line and does not park the capture: `AnOwnerPointIsOpenAtThePlayback` (deep verifier, facts only the person knows), `AnEscalatedPointIsOpenAtThePlayback` (deep verifier, contest held) and `APreflightFindingTheAgentJudgesWrongIsOpenAtThePlayback`.
- Confirm settles open points, the override recorded as the person's: the pre-flight test asserts the written entry records the override with no second pre-flight run; `AnAgentsConfirmNeverSettlesAnOpenPoint` asserts only the person's confirm does.
- Adjust reruns the checks: `AnAdjustRunsTheChecksAgain`.
- Auto mode unchanged: `AutoModeServesTheCaptureAsBefore` asserts a high pre-flight finding still reaches the revise-or-override choice.
- Docs: `website/src/content/docs/guides/working-with-coding-agents.md` describes the playback, its two sections and when to confirm an open point.
