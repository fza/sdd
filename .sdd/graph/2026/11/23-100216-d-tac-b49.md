In assisted and guided mode a capture is to show the person the checks' outcome once, at the final playback: the draft as the entry file sdd writes, what the checks changed in it, and the points only the person can settle. No check finding reaches the person before that playback, and one confirm or adjust settles all of it.

Today the agent relays findings across the capture, as the capture procedure `internal/baseprocedures/entries/20260703-094500-d-prc-cap.md` serves it: deep verifier findings settled without an edit are shown one by one at playback; a point only the person can settle stops the capture for a question of its own, in assisted mode after one unattended revise; in guided mode the agent names each writing-guide finding it dismissed, and asks the person where only they can settle a pre-flight finding. Each relay asks the person to read or answer how the draft came to be, while what they own is the entry as it stands. Assisted mode exists to put one approval gate to the person (20261117-213336-s-tac-hlw); the separate block for findings only the person can settle (20261114-150252-s-tac-2xb) is where those points come from.

## Decision

- Playback shows, in this order: the draft as the entry file, YAML frontmatter first; "Changed by the checks", the sentences revisions added and removed; "Open for you", one line per point only the person can settle, naming what the draft holds and what the checker holds.
- A finding settled without an edit is not shown to the person, neither at playback nor at the summary check after the write. This ends the replayed playback that shows such findings once more before the write (20261113-205441-s-tac-e87), in full as listed (20261113-222213-s-tac-qvt): a guided capture whose checks change nothing and leave no open point writes after the person's confirm.
- A point only the person can settle, from the deep verifier or from pre-flight, no longer stops the capture: it becomes a line under "Open for you". Confirm settles every open point as the draft stands, a pre-flight finding with its override recorded as the person's. The agent routes a pre-flight finding: it folds in every high finding it can, and one it judges wrong goes under "Open for you" in place of the separate revise-or-override question, since only the person may override it. Adjust carries the person's changes back to drafting, and the checks run on them.
- Guided mode: the agent no longer names to the person a writing-guide finding it acts on or dismisses, nor a medium pre-flight or hook finding it sets aside; what the checks changed shows under "Changed by the checks".
- Unchanged: auto mode; in guided mode the person's choice whether the deep verifier runs, and the retry or skip choice after the deep verifier fails or pre-flight's model leaves the draft unjudged, since none is a check finding.

Rejected: a separate question per open point during the capture, which keeps asking the person mid-capture; the agent settling open points and naming them as its inference, since facts only the person knows then get guessed and a quick confirm approves the guess.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] In assisted and guided mode, no step before the playback, and no summary check after the write, puts a check finding to the person.
- [ ] The playback shows the draft as the entry file with frontmatter, the sentences the checks changed and the open points, and no finding settled without an edit.
- [ ] A point only the person can settle, from the deep verifier or from pre-flight, reaches the playback as one line and does not park the capture for the person.
- [ ] Confirm writes the entry with every open point settled as the draft stands, a pre-flight finding's override recorded as the person's.
- [ ] Adjust returns the draft to drafting, and the checks run on the change.
- [ ] Auto mode serves the capture as before.
- [ ] Procedure tests in `internal/proctest` cover the playback in assisted and in guided mode.
- [ ] The coding-agents guide describes the playback and its open points.
