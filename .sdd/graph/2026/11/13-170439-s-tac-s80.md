A headless verifier sdd launches through Codex now reads every page of its reading before it judges: the launch prompt no longer orders it to end its turn, and tells it to end only when a serve says it is waiting for the dispatching move. Commit 6bea4ca2 on `pr/fix-capture-eval-defects`, merged into `fza-fork` at b03c41f0, fixes the headless Codex rounds the token-cost build added (20261112-195218-s-tac-day).

In three Codex capture runs on `fza-fork` at 369cb6fe, the headless verifier ended its turn on the paging step in two of three launches and the capture fell into retry, skip or abort. Its transcripts quoted the prompt's sentence "Step waits on the dispatching move: end turn" as true of the reading step, which waits on the verifier itself. A step that truly waits already says "waiting for the dispatching move to send …: end your turn", so the prompt sentence added only the misreading.

The new prompt says "End your turn only when a serve says it is waiting for the dispatching move. A step with options waits on you." A test of the dispatched prompt failed on the old sentence and passes on the new one. In three new Codex capture runs on the merged build, each headless verifier read all six pages, delivered its findings and ended at the step that waits for the capture's answers; none stopped early.

Open: whether Claude Code's headless verifier reads the prompt the same way was not run.
