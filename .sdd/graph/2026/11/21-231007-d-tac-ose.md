Three small fixes found in another project's use of sdd are to be built: three rewordings in the text the capture procedure serves, a stricter check of a new entry's generated title and summary, and a new setting, `embedding.keep_alive`, under which a local Ollama embedding model stays loaded for 30 minutes after sdd's last request. The three share one source and one small size, so one plan carries them.

## Served wording of the capture

All three rewordings are to be built. They change text of the capture procedure (20260703-094500-d-prc-cap), the refusal of one predicate and one heading of the `gather` result. No check changes what it accepts.

**Inspection scope.** The predicate `refsInspected` passes a reference when the current sdd session served the entry in full. Its read set belongs to the sdd session (`uninspectedRefs` in `internal/engine/predicates.go`), and an entry that session wrote counts as read, as the delivery of the session read log records (20260706-224658-s-tac-0iy). The refusal says "not inspected at full depth this session", and the grounding text of the step `assemble` says "served to the current session in full". Neither says which session. An agent session can span several sdd sessions. In another project's use of sdd an agent took the refusal for a defect, because its agent session had written the refused entry shortly before. The refusal and the step text are to say that only reads of the current sdd session count.

**Intent at the assisted playback.** In assisted mode the step `playback` has the agent name a directive's intent "plainly with its gloss", with the posture names `pending`, `guiding` and `settled`. Assisted mode shipped that way (20261117-213336-s-tac-hlw). In another project's use the person at the playback found the three names confusing. Guided mode avoids them: its text has the agent speak of the intent in the person's terms, whether the directive applies from now on or is work still to be done, and never name the posture. Assisted playback is to speak the same way. For a draft that supersedes a directive, the served text is to have the agent propose that directive's intent as its inference. Code gets no default: the intent stays required (`DirectiveIntentRequirement` in `internal/model/construction.go`), and the proposal is served text.

**The heading "to judge".** `gather` prints its candidate lines under the heading `# to judge (N)` (`renderGather` in `internal/presenters/gather.go`). The grounding text of `assemble` opens its rule with "every entry shown ends referenced, opened, dismissed in `verdicts`" and names the way out, "or else implicitly at publish", last. The gate demands a reference or a verdict only for an entry served in full (`groundingRefusals` in `pkg/application/workflow_grounding.go`). In another project's use an agent gave a verdict for each of the 80 to 99 lines its checks listed, about 15,000 tokens by that session's own estimate. The heading and the rule text are to stop reading as a duty to give a verdict for every line.

The plan for graph checks as marked lines replaces that list and its duty once it is built (20261121-223938-d-tac-rqi). Until then the heading and the rule text stand as reworded here.

Rejected: building the first two only and leaving the heading until that plan is built, which keeps the unneeded verdicts until then. Rejected: leaving the text as it is.

## Generated title and summary

`parseSummaryResponse` in `internal/llmops/summary.go` rejects a generated summary only when it is empty after trimming. In another project's use the generator returned a summary of three dots. sdd stored it, and the capture's summary check replaced it 11 seconds later. Another entry there got a wrong generated title, which the summary check also replaced. The text of the step `verifySummary` asks of a title that it names the entry's subject and follows the title rule. It does not ask whether the title contradicts the entry.

Two changes are to be built:

- A generated summary with no word character, meaning no letter or digit, or with fewer than three words counts as empty and is generated again by one repair call. `Description.Run` already sends one repair call for a first sentence over `MaxFirstSentenceWords`.
- The summary check also asks whether the title claims anything the entry's text denies.

Accepted cost: one more generation call in the rare case of such a summary.

Rejected: the stricter guard alone, because a title that contradicts its entry still passes the check. Rejected: leaving both as they are.

The summary check already names what counts as drift in a summary (20261113-222449-s-tac-2qx). The second change adds a question about the title and answers one more part of the open gap that the summary check lets defects through (20261005-172341-s-prc-xr5). Leaked markup and a doubled summary, that gap's own case, stay uncovered. The generator gap behind that case, a reasoning tag stored inside a summary (20261005-172136-s-tac-c8r), stays open too: the new guard counts words and does not look for markup.

## Local embedding model through Ollama

`ollamaEmbedder.request` in `internal/llm/embed/ollama.go` sends `model` and `input` and nothing else. Ollama unloads a model after five idle minutes by default, so the first embedding request after a pause waits for the model to load. Every `gather` that runs the semantic search begins with a reachability probe (`checkEmbedder` in `pkg/application/gather.go`); a graph below `verify.brief_all_below` live entries runs none. Its only time limit is `embedding.timeout`, 2 minutes by default, and its error names neither the model nor the host. Another project's use of sdd met this cold start between captures.

Three changes are to be built:

- A new setting, `embedding.keep_alive` in the `embedding` block, names how long Ollama keeps the embedding model loaded after sdd's last request. Its default is 30 minutes. sdd sends it with every Ollama embedding request.
- The server loads the model once at its start.
- The reachability probe ends after 30 seconds, and its error names the model and the host.

Ollama takes no instruction to keep a model for as long as the sdd server runs. Each request names how long the model stays loaded after it. A negative duration keeps the model loaded without limit. A shorter one, or `0`, frees the memory sooner.

Accepted costs: with the default the model holds memory until 30 minutes after sdd's last embedding request, and a server idle for longer than that starts cold again. Until upstream has the key, stock sdd warns about it in a project whose committed config sets it.

Rejected: no setting and a fixed 30 minutes, because a machine short on memory could neither shorten the time nor turn the keeping off. Rejected: a setting that stays on the fork with no proposal upstream, because stock would then warn about the key for as long as a project sets it. Rejected: the 30 second limit alone, because the cold wait stays. Rejected: leaving all three as they are.

The branch `next/cut-graph-checks` holds an earlier build of this part, to be reused:

- `36b600be` adds `embedding.keep_alive`, 30 minutes by default, sent with every Ollama request, and has `sdd serve` embed the probe text once at its start.
- `fe2a4083` limits the probe to 30 seconds and names provider, model and host in its error. The reuse takes that part of the commit alone. Its ranking and cutting of the check's list and its `gather` by ids alone do not return with this plan. How the check finds and orders its candidates (20261121-221907-d-tac-4rg) and what it serves of them (20261121-223938-d-tac-rqi) are the subjects of plans of their own.
- `826c3d4a` makes the restart test count the warm-up probe apart from the index embeds.
- `3ba506db` documents `embedding.keep_alive` together with the cuts this plan leaves off. The reuse takes its `embedding.keep_alive` part alone.

The release record says that the cuts to the capture's graph check were merged into `fza-next`, that `6f760d64` took them off again and that the branch remains (20261120-194054-s-prc-3oo). This build was among those cuts. The record does not name it. As built, Ollama unloads the model 30 minutes after the last request, so a server idle for longer starts cold again. The reuse keeps that default.

`embedding.keep_alive` sits in the `embedding` block, where every embedding setting lives today. Until upstream has the key it is fork-only and counts among the fork-only keys of item 17 of the fork's compatibility rule with stock sdd (20261119-013237-d-cpt-fu4). Stock applies no such key and prints `ignoring unknown config key` for it on every command, so a project whose committed config sets `embedding.keep_alive` makes stock warn. A project that leaves the key unset gives stock nothing to warn about. The key is to be proposed upstream, like the outcomes that rule's closing section lists. The pending move of LLM configuration to named provider entries (20261106-162723-d-tac-31y) is not built. It keeps the keys of the `embedding` block readable and maps them to an implicit provider entry, so a build of it has one more key of that block to map.

## Where the pieces live

No part adds a command, a query, a handler or a finder.

- The rewordings are served text: the step texts of the capture procedure, the failure text of the predicate `refsInspected` in `internal/engine`, and the heading the presenter `renderGather` prints.
- The summary guard is a parse rule in `internal/llmops`, beside the repair call of `Description.Run`. The title question is text of the step `verifySummary`.
- The request to keep the model loaded is a field of the Ollama request in the provider adapter `internal/llm/embed`, read from one new field of `model.EmbeddingConfig`.
- The load at server start is one read-side method of `pkg/application` that `sdd serve` calls from `internal/cliapp`. It writes nothing.
- The probe's limit and error text sit in `checkEmbedder`, on the read path of `gather` in `pkg/application`.

## Not measured

- How often the generator returns a summary without words is unknown. One case is on record.
- The load time of a cold embedding model and the memory a loaded one holds are not measured.

## Not covered

- In the first part, no check, gate or predicate changes what it accepts: only wording changes.
- Summary defects other than a summary without words and a title that contradicts its entry.
- Embedding providers other than Ollama.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The refusal of the inspection check names the current sdd session as the scope of the reads that count.
- [ ] The grounding text of the step `assemble` names the same scope.
- [ ] In assisted mode the text of the step `playback` has the agent speak of a directive's intent in the person's terms and never name the posture.
- [ ] For a draft that supersedes a directive, the served text has the agent propose that directive's intent as its inference.
- [ ] No code supplies an intent that a directive's draft leaves out.
- [ ] Neither the heading over the candidate lines of `gather` nor the rule text of `assemble` states a duty to give a verdict for a line that is not served in full.
- [ ] A generated summary with no letter or digit, or with fewer than three words, is not stored. One repair call generates it again.
- [ ] A regression test feeds the summary parser a summary of three dots: it fails before the guard and passes after.
- [ ] The summary check asks whether the title claims anything the entry's text denies.
- [ ] Every embedding request sdd sends to Ollama carries the duration of `embedding.keep_alive`, 30 minutes when the key is unset.
- [ ] `sdd serve` sends one embedding request at its start, so a local model is loaded before the first capture.
- [ ] The reachability probe of a `gather` ends within 30 seconds.
- [ ] The probe's error names the model and the host.
- [ ] The configuration reference describes `embedding.keep_alive` and its default of 30 minutes.
