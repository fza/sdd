An entry without a summary is left out of every chat judge call and stays unscored: the chat judge reads one line per candidate, built around the entry's summary, and no longer falls back to the body's first sentence.

The chat ranking judge of 20261118-162927-s-tac-pq5 sends each candidate as one line of kind, title and summary. An entry without a summary went out with its body's first sentence instead. Now:

- `EntryRankingLine` is empty for an entry without a summary, and the chat judge reads only candidates with a line, through `relevance.Reader`, an optional interface of a scorer. Cohere and systemone read the full text and every candidate.
- Such a candidate goes into no call: capture check, calibration, replay and the scan after writing alike. It is never used as a far entry, one of the entries known to be unrelated that each call carries as a floor. It stays unscored, lands among the doubt lines after the scored ones and is never set aside. It counts as no failed call and shrinks no batch.
- Gather names the count ("N the judge cannot read, left to judge unscored"). `sdd grounding update` and `sdd grounding status` count such pairs apart, and neither the recall stage nor the unscored count suggests an update for them.

Rejected: writing the missing summaries in the background before scoring (one model call per entry, a new setting, commits that change older entries' files); keeping the first sentence (a judgement from one sentence).

Accepted costs: such an entry is never scored and calibration never learns from it. Scores the old first-sentence line produced stay in the ledger and are still read.
