An agent can now report the user's reason and intent words at every capture step that sends the draft back for revision: each revise and recheck option of the capture procedure collects `reasonWords` and `intentWords` beside the draft fields. Commit b5b34b50 on `pr/fix-capture-user-reason`, merged into `fza-fork` at ac953ca0, extends the fields that 20261112-213854-s-tac-1lr added when it closed directive 20261112-212433-d-tac-v7s; that directive stays closed, and this fix widens where its fields are taken.

Users often give the reason late: when asked about a verifier finding, or while editing the draft. Only playback's adjust option accepted the two fields; the nine revise and recheck options in the other steps left them out, so the agent could not record the words at the step where the user gave them. The merge into `fza-fork` gave the pre-flight timeout's revise option, which came from another branch, the same fields.

A guard test in `internal/baseprocedures` requires every revise or recheck option that collects the body to collect both fields; it failed on the spec before the change and passes after it.

Open: the three Codex capture runs on the merged build gave no user words at a revise, so no agent has used the fields there yet.
