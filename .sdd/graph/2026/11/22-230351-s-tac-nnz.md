Graph checks and the verifier's reading as marked lines, per 20261121-223938-d-tac-rqi, are built and merged on `fza-next`: a check serves no entry in full, the agent opens entries by ID, and only a line marked `!` owes a verdict or a reference. The marked lines replace the entries in full, the tiers and the neighbour parts of the tiered reading that 20261118-173708-d-tac-euf settled; that directive is born terminal, so this entry cannot close it and names the replacement here.

## Built

- `model.PlaceLines` (48fc0a03a) places the lines of the drafter's check and of the verifier's reading through one order, one rule for the three classes and one set of limits.
- The check, the gate, the verifier's start and the procedure text serve marked lines (02682661d). The verifier's reading starts with the declared targets in full; lifecycle neighbours of a target and entries the order places as likely bearing are marked lines with their reason.
- Only a marked line owes a verdict; an opened entry without the mark owes nothing (71fce1adb). Opening by ID inside a capture runs nothing else (3c751d0f5, d0cfb34f7).
- Later pages of a check are built when asked, with the scores that arrived by then, and each served page is credited as read (d398542cc). Entries landed while a capture drafts are listed as lines and refuse no report (d916fb9b5). Both come from the build of 20261121-233807-d-tac-4iw.
