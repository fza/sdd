A capture's graph check sends the main agent lines, marks and counts under one set of limits on every path and no entry in full, the agent opens the entries it needs, and the drafter's check and the deep verifier's reading share one ranking and placement. The main agent runs on a costly model and the ranking judge on a cheap one, so the limits bound what the main agent reads, not what the judge scores. With entries served in full, sdd fixes what the agent reads. With lines, the agent chooses, and a rule a project gives its agent can limit that reading. Nine decisions make up the plan. Each names what was rejected, and the accepted cost where one exists.

The plan 20261121-221907-d-tac-4rg decides how a check finds, orders and judges its candidates. This plan decides what the agent and the verifier receive of them.

A graph check is one `gather` call inside a capture. It places each candidate in one of three classes: likely bearing on the draft, in doubt, or set aside. A line is one entry listed with its marks, ID, status and title. A summary line adds the entry's first summary sentence. A title line holds the ID and the title only. To open an entry is to have it served in full by its ID.

## 1. No entry in full from a graph check

A graph check sends lines, marks and counts. With them it sends this instruction, word for word:

> Open every entry you would reference and every entry that could contradict the draft. Then open those that look like bearing on the topic.

- The agent opens entries by ID, several in one call.
- A line that sdd marks as likely bearing owes an answer: a verdict, or a reference. A reference needs the entry opened.
- The first sentence of the instruction is a duty, which "every" states. The second carries no "every". A rule a project gives its agent, such as a maximum of entries to read, can narrow it without contradicting sdd's text. sdd names no number.
- One test run with real agents comes before the change ships.

Unchanged: sdd refuses a reference to an entry it has not served the capture's session in full.

Today a check serves in full every entry whose score reaches the judge's relevant threshold, up to 12 per call (`query.GatherFullCap`). Only an entry served in full owes a reference or a verdict (`GroundingEvidence.OpenFullReads` in `internal/model/grounding_gate.go`), and a line owes nothing. Serving relevant entries in full and that duty come from the grounding design 20261109-004506-d-cpt-udz. Under them the agent receives the first entries whether it needs them or not.

This decision replaces:

- entries that surely bear served in full,
- the cap of 12 entries in full per call,
- 5 entries in full in a check the agent judges itself (decision 5),
- every entry in full in the verifier's reading beyond the entries the draft references (decision 9).

Rejected:

- Lines without the duty on marked lines. An agent or a verifier that opens nothing then passes unnoticed.
- Entries that surely bear served in full, as before. sdd then fixes what the agent reads.

Accepted cost:

- A line needs the summary sentence to be judged by, about 320 characters against 180 for a line with the title. The estimated sizes therefore differ little from a check that serves 3 to 5 entries in full.
- Each open is a call.
- Whether agents open enough entries is not measured. The test run measures it.

## 2. The list and its marks

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `!` | likely bearing: the line owes an answer, a verdict or a reference |
| `.87` | the judge's score |
| `?` | not judged |
| `~ref` | one reference step from an entry the draft references |
| `{open}` | status |
| `→ refined by <id> (newer)` | a newer entry refines this one |

A check prints these groups:

- Header: the candidates found, how many are judged, how many are shown and how many set aside.
- Likely bearing: every line with its title and first summary sentence. Each owes an answer.
- In doubt: under the limit of decision 3.
- Set aside: one count per reason, without IDs.

sdd marks a line as likely bearing where the check serves the entry in full today: its score reaches the judge's relevant threshold.

A line does not name the searches that found its entry, as 20261121-221907-d-tac-4rg decides. That information orders the candidates before judging, and it keeps an entry found by two or more reads from the far floor's set-aside (`model.FarFloor`). Shown to the agent, it would act as a second ranking beside the judge's score and weigh against the entries that only a topic listing finds. Today it takes 21 to 22% of the bytes of the list. The mark for a newer refinement comes from that plan too.

That plan also removes the list of every live brief that a graph below `verify.brief_all_below` live entries gets today. The list of this decision is therefore the only form of a check, whatever the size of the graph.

The [list format](./21-223938-d-tac-rqi/list-format.md) shows the groups and marks in five scenarios: a narrow draft, a broad draft, a check where the judge's time ran out, a check where the judge failed, and the verifier's start.

## 3. One limit on entries in doubt, on every path

- Lines marked as likely bearing are all shown.
- Entries in doubt: at most 100 lines, the first 30 with a summary sentence and the rest with the title. Then at most 300 title lines. Then one count. For a judge weak on its seeded labels the lines double to 200, as today.
- A candidate without a score, because the judge failed or is off, falls under the same limit. Unscored candidates stand in the order in which candidates go to the judge, the merged order of 20261121-221907-d-tac-4rg.

Today `model.PlacePrefiltered` in `internal/model/prefilter.go` applies `GatherJudgeRanked` (100) and `GatherTitleListed` (300) only while the judge holds no recall stage. These are the tiers 20261119-105440-s-tac-5et delivered. Once thresholds hold, every candidate that scores between the set-aside threshold and the relevant threshold stays a line, without limit. An unscored candidate stays a line on both paths. Without a limit, 764 candidates come to about 134,000 characters of lines.

The plan 20261121-210755-d-tac-d5e lets a rule hide entries only on a proof. It counts on this limit holding with or without such a proof.

Rejected:

- A limit that shrinks as evidence grows. It needs a record of ranks per check and about 35 clean checks per size class before it can act. It stays a later item.
- No change. The band between the thresholds and the unscored candidates stay without limit.

Accepted cost: when the judge fails, an entry the draft needs can show as a title only.

## 4. The reading's size follows what bears

No fixed budget bounds a whole check. Lines marked as likely bearing are shown in whatever number, in the drafter's check and in the verifier's reading. The limits apply to entries in doubt and to entries set aside.

Rejected: a fixed budget of one page for a whole check. Reading more pages is right when the entries on them bear.

Accepted cost: the main agent's reading has no upper bound when many entries bear.

## 5. A check the agent judges itself

This applies when the judge failed or is off, or when it scored fewer candidates than the required minimum and the check goes on without it. The minimum is a configured count of judged candidates. When the judge's time runs out below it, sdd reports how many candidates are judged, and the person working with the agent chooses between waiting for the judge and letting the agent judge. The minimum, the time limit and that choice are decided in 20261121-221907-d-tac-4rg. So is the rule for a session with no person: sdd waits one further time limit, and if the judge is still below the minimum, the agent judges from the lines. The check serves the same lines with or without a person.

- An entry the judge did score keeps its normal place.
- The other entries carry `?` and follow the order in which candidates go to the judge. They fall under the limit of decision 3, and the first 50 of their lines carry a summary sentence.
- The first 5 of them carry `!` and owe an answer, so the duty of decision 1 does not end when the judge fails.
- A later page shows an entry scored in the meantime with its score (decision 6).

No graph entry gets a new field or mark for how its check went, as that plan decides: not the count of judged candidates, not that the agent judged, not the state of the far floor. The session log and the server log hold these facts.

Today a line carries the title and no summary sentence (`model.EntryBrief` in `internal/model/grounding.go`).

Rejected:

- The first 5 unscored entries in full. sdd then fixes what the agent reads, which decision 1 ends.
- 12 entries in full and 100 summary lines. At about 3,100 characters per entry and 300 per summary line, both estimates, that is about 67,000 characters, close to what a failed judge costs today.
- Title lines only. An agent that opens few entries then judges from titles.

Accepted cost: a needed entry past place 50 shows without its summary sentence, and past the limit as a title or in the count.

## 6. Pages built when asked for

- sdd builds a page when the agent asks for it, from the entries not shown yet and every score that has arrived by then.
- When scores are missing, sdd waits a short fixed time of a few seconds. Then it serves those entries as in doubt, marked `?`.
- Nothing appears twice and nothing is lost. A shown entry stays shown. An entry whose late score makes it likely bearing is added on the next page as a marked line under its own heading. A late set-aside applies only to entries not shown yet.
- A read is credited per page served.

Today `pagedText` in `pkg/mcpapp/result_bound.go` splits the finished result into pages and holds them for the session. `WorkflowSession.Gather` in `pkg/application/workflow_gather.go` credits the read before the result is paged, so the lines on a page the agent never fetched count as shown.

Rejected:

- Pages frozen at the first result, as today. Every entry the judge has not scored by then is served unjudged.
- A page that blocks until its entries are judged. The agent waits up to the judge's time limit per page.

Accepted cost: a page asked for twice can differ, and the build carries more logic and tests.

## 7. A check that only opens named entries

A check that passes only entry IDs serves those entries in full and a short list of the entries landed since the last check. It runs no search, no topic listing and no judge call. This is the call by which the agent opens entries under decision 1.

Today such a call inside a capture runs the whole check on a graph at or above `verify.brief_all_below`. `WorkflowSession.Gather` fills the draft from the capture's stored fields, so `gatherOn` in `pkg/application/gather.go` takes the search branch: the query writer, the semantic search, the keyword searches, every topic listing and the judge.

Rejected:

- Judging the opened entries too. That is a judge call and a wait per open, and the background run can score them.
- No change. Every open repeats all searches and listings.

Accepted cost: an open does not search again for a changed draft. A full check does.

Commit fe2a4083 built an open without search or judge, and 6f760d64 took it off the fork line again. The build can reuse it.

## 8. Entries that land while a capture drafts

Where the capture's check refuses today, sdd lists the landed entries itself as lines and counts them as read. The next report is not refused for them. A landed entry that is likely bearing is listed as a marked line and owes its answer like any other.

Today `groundingRefusals` in `pkg/application/workflow_grounding.go` refuses the report with "entries landed since the reading began" and names a `gather` call, which runs a full check. The write step already lists late entries itself, through `WorkflowSession.lateEntries` in the same file.

Rejected:

- A refusal that names the exact call to open the landed entries. It is still a refused report and one more call.
- No change. Every landed entry costs a full graph check.

## 9. One shared ranking and placement

The drafter's check and the verifier's reading use one order, one rule for the three classes and one set of limits. They differ only where the role needs it:

- The verifier's reading starts with the entries the draft references in full, and its marked lines name why sdd marked them.
- The graph check leaves out what the draft already answers.
- Only the graph check sets candidates aside by the far floor of 20261121-205340-d-tac-gyk, because the verifier's ranking calls carry no far entries.

### The verifier's start

- In full: the declared targets only. These are the entries the draft names in its refs and the entries it closes, supersedes or approves.
- Everything else is lines with marks, in the groups of decision 2. The verifier decides what to open.
- The verifier runs no search. Its candidates are the entries the capture's checks listed, with the scores and the place in the order those checks gave them. A candidate no check placed, such as an entry one reference from a target, is ordered by its similarity to the draft, as 20261121-221907-d-tac-4rg orders an entry that only a topic listing found.
- sdd marks three kinds of entries for the verifier: an entry that closes or supersedes a target, an entry a target closes or supersedes, and an entry the shared order places as likely bearing. A marked line names the reason and what the drafter did with the entry: read it, dismissed it under a verdict code, or did not read it.
- The verifier's report states for each marked entry whether the verifier opened it.

### What this changes in the verifier's directive

Directive 20261118-173708-d-tac-euf serves four groups in full: the declared targets, the entries the drafter read in full, the lifecycle neighbours of a target in both directions, and the top 5 of the ranked rest. Under this plan only the declared targets come in full. The other three groups become lines, the lifecycle neighbours and the top-ranked entries as marked lines. The up to 50 entries one reference from a target join the shared order like any other candidate and carry `~ref`. The directive's tiers of 100 summary lines and 300 titles give way to the limit of decision 3. The directive's rule that a done entry never serves in full has nothing left to act on, and 20261121-221907-d-tac-4rg decides how done entries rank. Unchanged: entries with a drafter's verdict stay briefs. Until the build lands, the directive stays in force.

### The two readings today

Both use the same judge (`rankingJudge` in `pkg/application/ranking.go`), delivered by 20261118-162927-s-tac-pq5, and the same score store, keyed by judge and by a hash of the draft. They differ in these points:

- Sources. The check reads this call's searches, every draft topic's listing without limit, the entries landed since the last check and the opened entries. The searches are one semantic search and one keyword search per term, each with its top 20 live hits. The verifier reads the union of every credited read of the capture and runs no search.
- Dropped entries. The check drops answered, opened and non-live entries. The verifier keeps them.
- Judge start. The check calls the judge only from 30 candidates on (`verify.prefilter_from`). The verifier has no such floor.
- Done entries. Only the verifier ranks them last (`model.PlaceVerifierReading` in `internal/model/verifier_lanes.go`).
- Unscored entries. The check keeps them all as lines, ordered by the number of reads that found them, then by ID. The verifier ranks them by similarity to the draft.
- Limits below the first recall stage. The check keeps 100 lines and 300 titles. The verifier keeps the tiers of 20261118-173708-d-tac-euf: the top 5 in full, the next 100 by title and first summary sentence, then 300 titles.
- Limits once thresholds hold. The check serves every entry at or above the relevant threshold in full, up to 12 per call (`query.GatherFullCap`), and keeps the band above the set-aside threshold as lines without limit. The verifier ignores the relevant threshold. It lists every ranked entry past the top 5 with its summary sentence and hides those below the set-aside threshold as titles.
- Neighbours. Only the verifier adds the reference neighbours and lifecycle neighbours of the draft's targets.
- Background scores. Only the verifier reads the scores of a background ranking pass (`rankedScores` in `pkg/application/workflow_ranking.go`).
- Far floor. Only the check sets candidates aside by the far floor (`model.FarFloor`), the rule of 20261121-205340-d-tac-gyk. That plan leaves the verifier's ranking calls out.

The constants already state the intent. `GatherJudgeRanked` and `GatherTitleListed` equal the verifier's `DeepSummaryRanked` and `DeepTitleListed`, with the comment "so drafter and verifier read the same cut of one ranking". The placement itself is two functions, `PlacePrefiltered` and `PlaceVerifierReading`.

Rejected:

- One order and one set of scores with separate tiers per reading. Two sets of limits must then be kept in step.
- No change. The differences listed above stay.
- Keeping the verifier's entries in full as 20261118-173708-d-tac-euf sets them. sdd then fixes what the verifier reads.
- Extra entries in full for the verifier in one lifecycle direction only, the entries a declared target replaces or closes. An entry that closes or supersedes a target shows that the target is outdated, and the marked lines list both directions.

Accepted cost: the verifier's reading changes shape, its size must be measured again, and the change is large. A verifier that opens little judges from lines, and its report shows which marked entries it left unopened. The verifier may miss an entry one reference from a target that the judge rates low.

## Measured today and estimated after

The [measurement of what sdd shows today](./21-223938-d-tac-rqi/shown-today.md) ran three real drafts of this graph, which held 1,044 live entries: one on a narrow topic, one with few references and one on a broad topic. The [predicted sizes](./21-223938-d-tac-rqi/predicted-sizes.md) estimate the same three drafts after the change.

| Draft | Candidates | Check today, one call | Check today, one capture | Check after, before opens | Check after, with 3 to 4 opens | Verifier's reading today | Verifier's reading after, before its opens |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| narrow topic | 35 | 7 KB | 21 KB | about 8 KB | about 14 KB | 74 KB | about 55 KB |
| few references | 93 | 19 KB | 58 KB | about 14 KB | about 22 KB | 83 KB | about 59 KB |
| broad topic | 249 | 51 KB | 154 KB | about 24 KB | about 33 KB | 155 KB | about 83 KB |

- One call of today's check is measured. The capture column is three times one call: a capture calls the check about three times, and each call prints the whole list.
- The verifier's readings of today are simulated over the exported functions, without scores and without verdicts. Six real readings of the same day ran to 69 to 159 KB on 7 to 16 pages.
- The columns "after" are estimates. They assume that the 30 best lines in doubt carry a summary sentence and the next 70 the title, and they include the set-asides of the far floor plan 20261121-205340-d-tac-gyk.
- No judged check was measured. On the measuring machine the judge scored none of the 377 pairs before its deadline of 45 seconds, so every candidate stayed a line and no check served an entry in full. How a working judge splits a check today is read from the code.
- Median sizes today: an entry in full 2,169 bytes in the check and 2,586 in the verifier's reading, a line of the check 143 without the searches that found it, a summary line 320, a title line 61.

The predicted sizes also hold the estimates for a variant with 3 to 5 entries in full, and for everything sdd sends in one recorded dialogue of 140 calls: 1,157,000 characters today and about 490,000 after all planned changes. Most rows of that total belong to changes outside this plan. The row of this plan, the entries from graph checks, reads and searches, falls from 196,000 to about 115,000.

Three levers stand out in the measurement:

- The verifier's rules text is about 35 KB of every reading. No decision covers it.
- A brief of an entry with a drafter's verdict is about 1 KB, without a cap. No decision covers it.
- A judge with valid thresholds lifts the limits of 100 lines and 300 titles in both readings today. Decisions 3 and 9 end that.

## Layers

The build follows the CQRS layering of this repository.

- `internal/model`: the shared order, the rule for the three classes, the marks and the limits, as pure functions without I/O. The duty of a marked line at the capture's gate is a rule here too, beside `GroundingEvidence.OpenFullReads`. The check's `PlacePrefiltered` and the verifier's `PlaceVerifierReading` both rest on them. Which entries a page holds, given the scores known and the entries already shown, is computed here too.
- `pkg/application`: the check (`gatherOn`) and the verifier's reading (`planRanking`) call the model placement. The session (`WorkflowSession`) keeps which entries a check has shown, waits the short time for missing scores, reads the scores of the background pass and credits the read per page served. The path that only opens named entries and the lines for landed entries at the capture's check live here.
- `internal/presenters`: renders the header, marked lines, summary lines, lines, title lines and counts.
- `pkg/mcpapp`: transport only. The `gather` tool passes the page asked for to the application and no longer holds a check's pages through `pagedText`.

The check stays one read request. The plan needs no new CLI action and no new command.

## Not part of this plan

- The order in which candidates go to the judge, the judge's time limit, the minimum of judged candidates, the choice when the judge misses it and the rank of entries of kind done. The plan 20261121-221907-d-tac-4rg decides them.
- Which lines a judge without valid thresholds marks as likely bearing. Today such a judge serves no entry in full by its score.
- The limit that shrinks with evidence, named under decision 3.
- The verifier's rules text and the size of a brief, named under the measurement.
- Far entries in the verifier's ranking calls.

## Not measured

- Whether a drafting agent and a verifier open enough entries when none arrives in full. The test run of decision 1 measures it.
- A graph check with a working judge, today or after the change.
- Every size after the change. The numbers are estimates.
- How often the limit on entries in doubt leaves a needed entry as a title when the judge fails.
