The fork released v0.18.0-alpha.3.fza.53 on 2026-11-20 as a prerelease of `fza/sdd` (https://github.com/fza/sdd/releases/tag/v0.18.0-alpha.3.fza.53), built from `fza-next` at `d8b6bdb3` on upstream v0.18.0-alpha.3: binaries for macOS and Linux (amd64, arm64), checksums and `install.sh` from a release workflow run that succeeded, with the Homebrew cask `fza/tap/sdd` at the same version. It follows v0.18.0-alpha.3.fza.52 (20261119-120806-s-prc-oan).

The release ships seven changes:

- The refusal of a misplaced key beside `graph_repo` names the file the key belongs in (20261119-193028-d-tac-2ap).
- Contribution mode is removed from the fork (20261119-192155-d-tac-zwv).
- A capture's publish no longer fails after an ended verifier child, and a retry writes no second entry (20261119-221932-d-tac-c0y).
- Assisted mode serves a spent capture step to the person (20261120-013119-d-tac-ddj).
- A revision delivered by any call launches the verifier's round (20261120-192504-d-tac-s5l).
- Accept merges the drafter's own fixes into the deep proposal (20261120-192559-d-tac-adf).
- `sdd serve` kills its `claude` processes at shutdown and keeps ranking scores across a restart (20261120-192705-d-tac-os2).

Its notes list three breaking changes:

- Contribution mode is removed, and a config that still sets `contribution` gets a warning.
- fza.52 and older cannot read a session that wrote an entry under this release.
- The fork-only `WorkflowSession.StageAttachments` returns `StageResult`.

The notes carry the chapter on the fork beside stock sdd unchanged from fza.52.

Two sets of token-cost changes are not in the release. Cuts to repeated serve output and cuts to the capture's graph check were merged into `fza-next`, and `d8b6bdb3` and `6f760d64` took them off again before the tag. Their branches `next/cut-serve-repeats` and `next/cut-graph-checks` remain.
