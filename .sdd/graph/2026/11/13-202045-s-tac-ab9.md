A decision captured with a user present now gets the user's real reason: the capture tells the agent that the reason states why, never what, and sdd refuses a reason that only restates the directive's intent. Commit ce4e0643 on `pr/fix-capture-user-reason`, merged into `fza-fork` at ac953ca0, builds on the reason field of 20261112-213854-s-tac-1lr, which 20261112-212433-d-tac-v7s committed to.

In three Codex capture runs on `fza-fork` at 369cb6fe, with the decision stated and its reason held back, no agent asked why. Each filled the reason field with words from the opening message: in one run the decision sentence itself, which the intent field held almost word for word; in another a fragment of it ("instead of returning an empty result"). The field was only checked for being non-empty.

What changed:

- The assemble guidance says the reason states why, never what: words stating the decision itself never fill `reasonWords`, and when the user's words give no why the agent asks.
- `model.ReasonRepeatsIntent` reports a reason that contains the intent words or sits inside them, once case, spacing and closing punctuation are set aside. The `userWordsHeld` gate counts such a reason as missing and serves "reasonWords repeats intentWords, the decision itself: ask the user why they decided it".

Tests: a table test of `ReasonRepeatsIntent` and two proctests, a directive whose reason restates its intent held at assemble and advancing with a real reason, and the new guidance served at assemble; both failed before the change. Breaking the gate's overlap check made the proctest fail; the file was restored byte for byte.

In three new Codex capture runs on the merged build, every agent asked why before reporting any field, and each reason field held the answer verbatim ("Empty input hid upstream bugs twice."), which answers the open eval of 20261112-213854-s-tac-1lr.

Open: a reason that paraphrases the decision without containing the intent words passes the check. In all three runs the agents rewrote the quoted intent words when a wording change was requested, and in one an agent wrote a user message for an adjust, so invented user words remain possible.
