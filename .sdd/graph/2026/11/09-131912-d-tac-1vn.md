The working-principles lane of the session shell keeps its default 12 KB cap: active facts under `principles/interactive` arrive whole while they fit, and a set past the cap arrives as named pointers, each with its ID, brief and pull, never cut partway. The shell declares no larger cap and no larger serve total.

The opening serve then stays within its 36,000-byte total and the host floor of about 10K tokens the serve-cap plan (20260829-173342-d-tac-rzi) sizes it against. The shipped principles set is about 4.3 KB, so only a project that adds its own principles past 12 KB meets the pointer, and the posture directive (20260810-123226-d-cpt-ei0) asks for principles primed at open, which keeping the set small already serves.

Rejected: raising the lane cap to 24 KB, because the opening serve's worst case then passes its total, so other lanes shrink or the shell declares a larger total closer to the host cut. Rejected: no cap on principles, because it brings back the unbounded opening serve the serve-cap plan exists to prevent, and a host that cuts the middle of a serve loses more than the principles.

Settled: the whole-entry cut with named pointers is built and merged into `fza-fork` (ebb14567), recorded in the follow-up done (20261109-111708-s-tac-tuk), which left this cap question open; nothing remains to build. Accepted cost: a project with more than 12 KB of principles gets them as pointers the agent must pull, not primed.
