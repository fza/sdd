The deep verifier's grounding reading (verify procedure 20261109-010000-d-prc-vfy) serves the entries a verdict must read in full with no page cap, and tiers the rest by rank under ceilings, so it fits a verifier's context on a large graph.

Served whole, one reading on a board engine on a large graph ran to 84 pages, about 980k characters, past any verifier's context; no verdict could rest on all of it (20261118-162919-s-tac-2t0).

## The reading

- **In full, no cap:** the declared targets (refs, closes, supersedes, approves), the entries the drafter read in full, and lifecycle neighbours: entries closing or superseding a target, or closed or superseded by one.
- **Briefs:** entries the drafter gave a verdict.
- **Title and first summary sentence:** other entries one ref from a target, at most 50 by rank.
- **The ranked rest:** the top 5 in full, the next 100 by title and first summary sentence, then up to 300 by title; beyond that, one count line.
- Done entries rank last and never serve in full.
- Once the prefilter is calibrated, its set-aside threshold (20261114-003118-s-tac-0vl) replaces the count of 100.
- The rank comes from the one ranking judge of 20261118-162927-s-tac-pq5.
- A judge weak on its seeded labels (ranking recall below 0.9, measured from 20 positives on) widens the reading: the top served in full and the summary lines double.

## Measured sizes

The refs of a reading run to a median of 4k to 7k tokens. With top 5, 100 lines and 300 titles, the reading stays about 46k tokens at the large graph's median, before any widening.

## Rejected

- A fixed page budget: it cuts entries a verdict must read.
- One hop in full: about 56k tokens at the median, up to about 280k on the large graph.
- No ceilings: the reading grows with the graph; at twice the graph it reaches about 64k tokens.
- Titles only for the overflow: too little to judge from.

## What this overrides

The closed directive 20261113-235104-d-tac-ypu hid no listed entry before calibration and rejected a fixed top K. On a large graph that uncalibrated reading is the one that overran, so the tiers apply from the start, before calibration.
