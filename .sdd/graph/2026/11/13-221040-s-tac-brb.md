The capture now accepts `reasonWords` or `intentWords` in an answer relaying the user's message only when they are quoted from that message; a wording change to the draft goes into the body. `model.QuotedFrom` decides whether the words are quoted, setting aside case, spacing, quotation marks and closing punctuation. In Codex capture eval runs on `fza-fork` (two batches of three runs, builds `369cb6fe` with fixes and `719cfd12`), agents rewrote the user's intent words after a wording request in 3 of 3 runs, showed changed intent words that were never stored, and replaced them with words about the directive's posture. The capture guidance now adds "A wording change to the draft changes the body, never these quotes".

The check guards the reason and intent fields of 20261112-213854-s-tac-1lr and takes up the rewritten intent words that 20261113-202045-s-tac-ab9 left open. Commit `f3314779` on branch `pr/fix-eval-defects-2`, merged into `fza-fork` at `1b04d01e`. Tests `TestQuotedFrom` and `TestCapture_UserWordsChangeOnlyToWhatTheUserSays` failed before the change and pass after; breaking the guard made them fail, and the file was restored.

Open: no real-agent run has shown it yet. The eval login hit its usage limit until 2026-12-09, so no Codex rerun was possible.
