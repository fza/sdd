No capture is refused or held for a missing reason on any kind, and a directive's intent is no longer demanded in the person's own words; a directive still needs an intent value, and a reason or an intent that is given is stored.

## The rule

- No kind owes a reason at capture. A directive, a plan and every other decision are captured with or without one. A signal carries none.
- A directive needs an intent value: `pending`, `guiding` or `settled`. This is the rule stock sdd has (`model.DirectiveIntentRequirement`), unchanged.
- The person's own words for a directive's intent (`intentWords`) are no longer demanded in guided mode.
- A reason and an intent are stored when given. In every mode the agent may propose both and shows them in the playback. In guided mode a reason or an intent the person gives is stored with the entry; no question asks for one and no step holds for one, so the dialogue runs as in stock sdd.
- Wherever a reason or an intent is stored, `reason-by` and `intent-by` name who gave it. In guided mode they name the person who gave the reason or the intent. A guided reason or directive intent the agent proposed and the person confirmed at the playback names the person too, as an approval in assisted mode does. An approval adopts a proposed reason and a directive's intent as the person's, so `reason-by` and `intent-by` name the person who approved, whether the words are their own or a proposal they approved. An entry an agent decided alone in auto mode carries `decided-by: agent` and no `reason-by` or `intent-by`. The value `agent` in those marks appears only in the checks an assisted draft runs before the person approves it, never in an entry sdd writes.
- An entry without a reason carries no `reason-by`.

The rule holds in guided, assisted and auto mode alike.

## Why

The fork is this repository's own line of sdd, built on `fza-next` beside upstream (20261117-092355-d-prc-3vb). Stock sdd is upstream's build. Stock has no `reason` field, no `reasonWords` and no `intentWords`. It enforces only the intent value. An agent or a person who knows stock meets a hold in the fork that stock does not have. The fork stays compatible with stock sdd (20261119-013237-d-cpt-fu4), and the reason requirement is one of the differences it gives up; item 40 of that directive ends the per-kind reason rule through a decision of its own, which is this entry. The owner's instruction, verbatim: "remove the reason dor directive / plan enforcement".

Three other capture checks stay in every project: reading before writing (`verify.mode`, default `prose`), the hold until the agent's client matches an actor in auto mode, and the delegate check under `verify.mode: deep`.

Rejected: keeping the reason requirement for directives and plans. Rejected too: skipping all of the fork's capture checks in a project that sets none of the fork's keys.

## What this replaces

20261119-013345-d-tac-adk stated this rule with the marks misstated: it held `agent` in `reason-by` and `intent-by` for an approved proposal. This entry restates the rule with the marks of an approved entry as `markApproved` in `pkg/application/capture.go` sets them, and adds the marks of a guided entry, which the capture does not write yet.

20261118-173349-d-tac-8jh narrowed the reason requirement to two kinds: a directive, with its intent, and a plan owed a reason at capture, and every other decision stored one when given. This entry removes the requirement for those two kinds as well, so the per-kind split has nothing left to separate. What 8jh said about storing a given reason and about showing an inferred reason and intent in the playback stands and is restated in the rule above.

20261118-162948-s-tac-kd7 built the per-kind rule. Three parts of that build change: `model.UserWordsOwed`, the `reason` field requirement on a directive or plan an agent decided in auto mode or a person approved in assisted mode, and the reason steps of the capture procedure in guided and assisted mode, with the `userWordsHeld` gate that holds assemble until the words arrive. In assisted mode the playback of 20261117-213336-s-tac-hlw keeps showing a proposed reason and intent. The agent marks of auto mode from 20261117-175601-s-tac-69f, `decided-by: agent` and the `agentLimit` step, stay as they are, except that a person taking on a draft parked at `agentLimit` is no longer asked for a reason in `reasonWords` or an intent in `intentWords`. The model check and the capture change so a guided entry may carry a reason and an intent the person gave, with their name in `reason-by` and `intent-by`. The model check still asks for `reason-by` only where a reason is stored.

## Also proposed upstream

The upstream proposal that carries the fork's capture checks drops the same two demands, so the proposal and the fork ask a person for the same things. The intent value stays required there as in stock.
