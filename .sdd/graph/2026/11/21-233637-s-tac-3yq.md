A capture that stuck on `session_conflict` after a round of the deep verifier is fixed on `fza-next`: a background ranking pass no longer writes to the session log, a log write catches up with other writers up to eight times, and a verifier round builds its reading once (a8b28c48, fb75881e, 9e09ce7e, 2f408aaf, 9f331133). The defect shipped in v0.18.0-alpha.3.fza.53 (20261120-194054-s-prc-3oo). No release holds the fix yet.

## The defect

The released build showed it under `sdd serve`, on one long session with many captures run one after the other.

- A capture went through a round of the deep verifier and returned to its draft step, through an accepted proposal or an adjustment at the playback.
- The next report at that step failed with `session_conflict`. The message held "session log append failed earlier" and "session binding is stale", on later calls "session version changed".
- Every later call on that capture failed the same way. `resume_session` still served the capture. Only abandoning the capture cleared it.

Four captures of that session stuck this way. A fifth ran the same steps without the failure, so the defect is intermittent.

## Cause

Two things met.

- A second writer on the capture's own log. 20261120-192705-d-tac-os2 had each background ranking pass append what it scored to the session log as a `workflow_ranking_scores` record when the pass ended. A pass ends outside the call that drives its capture, so its record lands at any time between that call's own writes.
- A log write that survived one such append and not two. A write that found another writer ahead caught up once and tried again (`workflowSink.Append` in `pkg/application/workflow.go`). A second foreign append before that second try made the write fail as a conflict.

A round that hands a revision to a waiting verifier built the verifier's reading twice, once for the whole reading and once for the revision's pages (`prepareDeep` in `pkg/application/workflow_deep.go`). Each build started a ranking pass over the entries the judge had left unscored. Two passes ended, two records landed, and the second met the second try of the capture's write.

A later call built the reading again and started passes again while entries stayed unscored, so their records landed on that call's writes too.

## One stuck log

One session log of an installation that showed the defect supports the cause. The reading took record kinds, instance ids, versions and times only.

- Where a capture stuck, three ranking records follow its last write in a row, 22 and 47 seconds apart, with no write of the capture between them.
- After a resume the capture wrote one read record. A ranking record followed 0.8 seconds later and another 46 seconds after that. No result record of the capture followed the read.
- Where captures ran clean, single ranking records lie between the capture's own writes.

The ranking records come in twos and threes exactly where the capture's own write is missing.

## The fix

- Scores off the log (fb75881e). A pass keeps what it scored in the staged area of its session, one file per pass beside the staged attachments (`RankingStore` in `pkg/application/blobstore.go`, the files in `pkg/local/ranking_store.go`). The files survive a restart of the server and go when the staged area of the session is removed. A staged store without this part keeps the records in memory, and a server started anew scores those entries again.
- Catch-up up to eight times (9e09ce7e). A log write catches up with each writer that got ahead and tries again, at most eight times (`sessionCatchUps` and `appendCaughtUp` in `pkg/application/workflow.go`). A caught-up event that changes the instance the write belongs to still ends the tries as a conflict. Past eight tries the conflict surfaces, so a writer that never stops cannot hold a call forever.
- One reading per round (2f408aaf). `prepareDeep` places the verifier's reading once and renders the whole reading and the revision's pages from it. Entries a pass left unscored go to the judge again with the next round, not within the same one.
- Logs of the released build stay readable. A log that holds `workflow_ranking_scores` records loads as before, and `rankedScores` in `pkg/application/workflow_ranking.go` still counts their scores. No call writes such a record any more.
- The configuration reference says where a pass's scores are kept and that a round ranks once (9f331133).

Each change removes one condition of the failure. Scores off the log removes the second writer. The catch-up covers the writers that remain: a CLI call with `--session` beside a server, a delegate that delivers, another client's resume. One reading per round stops a round from sending the same entries to the judge twice.

## What changes against earlier entries

The fix replaces one part of 20261120-192705-d-tac-os2, the place the scores are kept. Its aim stands: a restart does not pay again for a pass already scored. Its kill of `claude` processes at shutdown is untouched.

That directive put the scores into the log because 20260914-113737-d-cpt-qst makes the log the one source of session state. The scores of a pass are no session state: a lost record costs a second scoring and changes no step of a capture. They become session state when the capture's read event credits them, and the call that drives the capture writes that event. The log keeps one writer while a capture advances. Eight tries stay within the bounded automatic attempts the same directive asks for.

`abandon` keeps its single retry on an append conflict (20261114-085447-s-tac-aqy). The eight catch-ups hold for the writes of a running call: the engine's events and the records beside them.

## Tests and limits

- `TestCaptureDeep_ARevisionAdvancesWhileItsRankingPassesEnd` and `TestCaptureDeep_ARevisionRanksItsReadingOnce` in `internal/proctest/capture_deep_ranking_race_test.go` drive a capture through a verifier round and an adjustment, then report a revision under a judge that leaves entries unscored. They hold that the report advances, that the scores stay off the log and that the round asks the judge once per entry.
- The reproduction on the unfixed code (a8b28c48) needs a helper. A session store wrapper held the two ranking appends and landed one as the capture's write started and the other behind the catch-up. Without that forced order the window is as short as the writes themselves, and the scenario does not fail reliably. The wrapper left with fb75881e, because no ranking append is left to order.
- The catch-up tests in `pkg/application/session_fold_test.go` use a helper of the same kind: it lets another writer append between a holder's load and its append.
- Eight is a judgment, not a measurement.
- No test reproduces the stretch of three ranking records in a row.
- Why every later call failed is derived from the code and from one stretch of one log. It is not reproduced.
- The log is no part of the repository, so its figures cannot be checked from here. Its reading did not show which step a transition went to, so the step is inferred from the order of the records.
- This entry rests on the commits and the code as read. No test ran again for it.
