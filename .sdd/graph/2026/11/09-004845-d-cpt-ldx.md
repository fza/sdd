A task procedure may declare interim deliveries, so a delegate child session can exchange several rounds with the instance that dispatched it instead of ending after its first result. This refines 20260827-232430-d-cpt-voa, where the child's final transition delivers declared values and completes the child, so a delegate could answer once and then was gone. The deep verifier (20261109-004729-d-cpt-xs5) needs findings out, the drafter's answers in and the verifier's responses out; the back-and-forth is the point of that design.

- **Interim delivery.** A step delivers values into a declared field of the dispatching instance without completing the child. The parent's step does not move, as for the final delivery.
- **Parent-filled gate.** The child then waits at a gate on a field the parent fills; the engine delivers the parent's value (for the verifier, the drafter's answers) into the child, and the child's next step is served from it.
- **Lifetime.** Only the final delivery completes the child. The child concludes when the dispatching instance ends, its entry written or abandoned, or at its declared round cap; an abandoned parent abandons the child.
- Everything else in 20260827-232430-d-cpt-voa stands.

The procedure-spec reference (20260814-100000-s-prc-psr) describes interim deliveries and parent-filled gates when they ship.

Rejected, with the reasoning: a fresh child per round (the verifier loses its reading and its earlier findings each round); collecting rounds through the host's text return (the host-carried results shape voa set aside, without engine provenance); letting the child drive the parent's step (the delegate would steer the dialogue).

Accepted costs: a child can outlive one round and must be cleaned up with its parent; procedure authors declare a round cap.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A task procedure can declare an interim delivery that writes into the parent's declared field without completing the child or moving the parent's step.
- [ ] A child waiting on a parent-filled gate is served its next step once the parent fills the field.
- [ ] A child completes only at its final delivery, and concludes when its parent ends or at its round cap; abandoning the parent abandons the child.
