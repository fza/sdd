The deep verifier's reading is now sorted by the reranker prefilter under the calibration `gather` shares, and `gather`'s similarity stage is retired; built on `pr/feat-verifier-sorting` and merged into `fza-fork` at 36aad85d, this completes 20261113-235104-d-tac-ypu, whose lossless trims 20261114-000441-s-tac-05p delivered.

## Built

- 3 percent blind audit: met (2c3ee2c1), with deviation 1 below.
- No similarity stage in `gather`: met (62b83ba5).
- Stored scores read first, new scores stored: met (958740f9), with deviation 3 below.
- Label source, removable seed, finding bases as live positives: met (c69fb7bb, 4f955d96, 9b74bfcb, bd5b1851, 65e0c274).
- Hidden openings as misses only under a finding: met (65e0c274).

No live reading has passed the bound yet, so the hiding, the one-line block and the blind audit are shown by tests over a calibrated ledger (`internal/proctest/capture_deep_lanes_test.go`), not by use.

## Deviations from the decision

1. The decision names full blocks; a kept or audited listed entry shows as its brief block, not its body.
2. The one-hop rule keeps every entry one ref, close or supersede away from a declared target, in either direction; the topic rule also keeps same-kind entries on subtopics of the draft's narrowest topic.
3. Scores for the unpublished draft live in the capture's reading and reach the relevance ledger at publish, not when scored.

## Choices the decision left open

- Basis labels are written for every finding, conceded and dropped ones included.
- A scorer failure during the verifier's split leaves the affected entries inspected.

## Applied

On this machine, the 178 replayed pairs were seeded; 167 were already labelled. `sdd grounding rescore` scored 8 more pairs; 28 are too long to score. `sdd grounding status` then shows 169 referenced labels, 10 replayed labels and a recall bound of 0.9778 at 0 misses against the 0.98 floor. The thresholds are not valid yet, so nothing is hidden or set aside; about 20 more referenced labels without a miss make them valid.
