Waiting instruction lanes of a split result now drain however a follow-up call proves or rereads, fixed in 328c373a on `fza-next`. An agent sending wrong held proofs or rereads looped 101 calls without a report at capture assemble: lanes the previous result had sent served in full again, filled the result bound and crowded out the waiting lanes. Now, while lanes wait, the lanes the previous result sent serve as pointers whatever the call proves, matched by name, so the waiting lanes drain within as many calls as lanes wait. A call carrying a report with fields always went through.

This narrows the reread rule of 20261117-134258-s-tac-x4u, under which a sent piece serves in full again on a reread or a failed proof, to calls where no lane waits. It limits the cost of the wrong and skipped proofs of 20261114-185019-s-tac-8aa without closing that gap: agents still do not send proofs reliably.
