sdd now titles untitled entries in the background on every session start, engine-call sync round and timer round, and after an explicit `sdd sync`, closing 20261105-233807-d-tac-831 in commits `458da1eb` and `79af2c5a` on `pr/core`, merged into `fza-fork` as `7d4f85a0`.

One run titles at most 10 untitled entries, newest first, through the title call of 20261107-021219-d-tac-okg, and commits them as one `sdd: summarize N entries` commit through the `sdd summarize --all` path. Each entry is re-read under the graph lock and skipped if it gained a title meanwhile; a per-checkout try-lock under `.sdd-runtime` turns a second start into a no-op. The engine-call trigger obeys the per-checkout cooldown, so a failing LLM is not retried on every call. A failure is recorded as a blocked-sync record of kind `titles-failed` with fixed-phrase detail, served as the sync notice; under `sync.mode: manual` the notice serves only that record. A titles failure never replaces another blocked-sync record, and a successful sync round no longer clears it; a successful backfill does. The CLI runs the backfill before an explicit `sdd sync` or `sdd sync --pull` returns; when it cannot build its LLM runner it records "no LLM is configured" and the sync still succeeds. The backfill does not push; under `sync.mode: auto` the next round does.

Evidence: each of the nine acceptance criteria has its test, among them `TestSyncRoundPullsUntitledEntriesAndTheBackfillTitlesThem`, `TestBackfillTitlesTitlesTheNewestTenInOneCommit`, `TestSessionStartBackfillsTitlesWithoutWaiting`, `TestBackfillAndCaptureInOneCheckoutBothCommit`, `TestBackfillTitlesKeepsATitleWrittenMeanwhile`, `TestBackfillTitlesFailureReachesTheNoticeAndIsRetried`, `TestBackfillTitlesCommitsTheOthersPastAFailedCall` and `TestBackfilledTitlesFromTwoClonesMerge`. A live `sdd sync --pull` with the built binary against the configured LLM titled an untitled entry and committed `sdd: summarize 1 entries`.
