A generated summary whose first sentence still runs over 25 words after its repair call gets one fresh generation inside the same publish before the publish fails, and the final error says that publishing again generates a new summary.

Seen twice on one day on the fork build, as the agents running those captures reported; no other entry records the incidents. In a capture in this repository the repair left 26 words, in another project of the owner's 27. Each time the next publish generated a new summary that passed. The limit guards the short first sentence the summary contract (20260901-112834-d-cpt-75a) asks for. The limit is `MaxFirstSentenceWords`, and `Description.Run` makes the one repair call, both in `internal/llmops/summary.go`. A repair still over the limit fails the publish with `generating summary: summary's first sentence is too long: <n> words after the repair call, limit 25` (`pkg/application/capture.go`).

The failure directive (20260829-113416-d-cpt-nut) decides it: it counts a malformed model response as a failure time alone can clear, and a first sentence over the limit is read here as one, where the engine tries again briefly and leaves a trace of the retry, and every failure tells the agent whether trying again helps.

Rejected:

- Failing after the one repair call with no hint, as now. It runs against the failure directive, and the agent learns that a retry can pass only by trying.
- A hint in the error alone. The agent still pays a failed publish and a full retry each time.

Accepted cost: up to two more model calls in a publish whose repair falls short, and a slower publish then.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A repair still over the limit starts one fresh generation, with its own repair call, before the publish fails.
- [ ] The fresh generation is logged, naming the word count that triggered it.
- [ ] A publish whose fresh generation also falls short fails with an error that names the word count, the limit, and that publishing again generates a new summary.
- [ ] A summary within the limit makes no extra call.
