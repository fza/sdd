Decisions an agent captures in `dialogue.mode: auto` carry a mark of their author, the mark passes down the reasoning chain until a person approves it, and a fixed limit by layer and kind decides what an agent may decide alone. Until now sdd let an agent in auto mode write a decision with a reason of its own that looked exactly like a person's: the user-words gate is off, the reason is not stored, and the local participant is listed by default. Agent decisions stay active, so other agents can act on them; a provisional status that held them back until review was rejected for that reason.

Rejected as well: a mandate or role entry a person writes in advance to scope agent decisions, a separate entry kind for agents, expiry by age, and a hash of the user's message as proof (the agent supplies that message itself).

## Decomposition

- `internal/model`: the `decided-by`, `run-by` and `approves` fields on the entry, the limit table as a pure function of kind, layer, intent and lifecycle targets, and the chain mark derived on the graph.
- `internal/command` and `internal/handlers`: the capture write stamps the fields from the dialogue mode and refuses them from the report; the capture procedure parks a draft the limit refuses.
- `internal/query`, `internal/finders` and `internal/presenters`: show, view and catch-up read the derived mark and list marked chains; the deep verifier's reading states it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An entry written in auto mode carries `decided-by: agent`, set by sdd from the dialogue mode; a report trying to set it is refused.
- [ ] An auto-mode decision stores the agent's own stated reason, and the capture refuses a decision without one.
- [ ] Its participants name the MCP client's actor (`Claude`, `Codex`) instead of the local participant; a client without an actor entry is named by its client name until its actor is captured.
- [ ] Its `run-by` names the participant sdd runs as, filled by sdd, left out when none is configured.
- [ ] In auto mode an agent writes alone only signals, plans and activities on the tactical and operational layers, and directives with intent pending on those layers; every other decision, and an agent decision replacing or closing a person's decision, parks for a person with the rule named. A done may close any entry.
- [ ] A derived mark, never stored, starts at each agent-decided entry no approval covers and passes along grounded-in, builds-on, refines and depends-on refs; an entry without `decided-by` stops it.
- [ ] A settled directive carries a lifecycle field `approves:` beside `closes` and `supersedes`, so refs stay pointers without effect (20260628-130356-d-cpt-gm1); it clears the mark of the approved entries and of those marked only through them. Dismissals are settled directives that close the dismissed entries. Both are refused in auto mode.
- [ ] `sdd view`, `sdd show`, the catch-up briefing and the deep verifier's reading show the mark with its root and distance, and catch-up lists marked chains for review.
- [ ] The configuration reference and the coding-agents guide state this behaviour for `dialogue.mode: auto`.
- [ ] Entries without `decided-by` read as a person's decision; no migration.
