Proof-gated hiding of 20261121-210755-d-tac-d5e is built and merged on `fza-next` (73acbcc68, e9b471375, b612b30a2): a hiding rule hides only at the step its replay proof allows, misses are counted by four detectors, and no blind sample reaches the main agent's lines to judge. Criteria 1 to 9 are met. The tenth, the committed team proof record, is postponed by 20261122-232332-d-tac-aoi, so this entry closes the plan.

## Built

- Steps: each rule holds full, narrowed or compute-only per judge. Without a valid proof the threshold hides nothing and the far floor holds its careful form; `sdd grounding status` shows what each would hide. A rule goes one step down on misses from three distinct captures or a fallen bound, and up only by a fresh replay. The far floor takes its median form only at full.
- Proofs name judge, prompt revision, graph size and counts, and expire on a changed prompt or a graph grown by a quarter. Proofs are kept per judge key, so a changed judge model starts its own. Proof records live in machine state.
- Replays read a past entry's text without its naming sentences, against earlier entries only, and plant misses; the status prints the detection rate beside each miss count. The margin above the cut is recorded and shown and moves no rule.
- Detectors: rescue, verifier, the post-write scan and the person's words (e9b471375).
- The replays and the careful far floor they rest on are recorded in 20261122-231118-s-tac-c33.

## Test run

One `sdd serve` with the real Haiku judge on 195 entries of this graph (126 live). Before any proof a live check of 84 candidates set none aside. After 92 replays the threshold held `narrowed` (3 of 176 needed entries hidden, bound 0.951) and the same check set 30 of 84 aside. `sdd grounding status` showed each rule's step, proof counts and a planted-miss detection rate of 35% of 26 planted. The far floor reached no proof on this graph. While replays ran, the status printed a zero start time; fbd656439 fixes that.
