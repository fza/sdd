One ranking judge now serves both the capture's grounding reading and the deep verifier's reading, through a chat protocol any chat model can run, shipped on `fza-next` (b7d01a69, fee9f0fc, c7639589, cd61ad1b, b036e9df, 87a223ca, 44a69aca).

- `llm_prefilter.protocol` takes `cohere`, `systemone` or `chat`; `generate`, `listwise` and `cohere-rerank` read as aliases.
- `chat` ranks up to 100 candidates per call. A broken or incomplete answer, or a timeout, halves the batch, and the working size is remembered per model.
- The judge is the configured `llm_prefilter`; else, for a Claude client, `claude-haiku-5-5` through the `claude` CLI at `--effort low` (for CLI commands: when `claude` is on `PATH`); else `chat` on the `llm` provider.
- Ranking starts in the background once assemble passes. After `verify.ranking_timeout` (45s), what it left unscored ranks by similarity to the draft. The reading names its ranking source.
- The ref-seeded calibration doubles as a health check: a judge weak on the seeded labels widens the reading's tiers.
- `sdd grounding status` prints a compact view (labels, audit, drift, judge); `--verbose` prints each judge's full calibration.

Measured on Haiku 5.5: 100 candidates per call took 11.7s at p50 and 16.6s at p90; recall of real refs in the top 5 was 0.93, against 0.53 for similarity order. Thinking cannot be disabled for Haiku 5.5; low effort is the floor.

This replaces the reranker-only sorting of 20261114-003118-s-tac-0vl with one judge any configured provider can serve, and ranks the reading the verify procedure 20261109-010000-d-prc-vfy reads.
