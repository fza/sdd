- Not available without a checkout: `TestReadGit_IsNotAvailableWithoutACheckout`.
- File source with `rev`: the revision cases of `TestCheckDeepFindings` and `TestCheckDeepFindings_CitationsThatResolveStand`.
- Finding that asks for a source: `TestCheckDeepFindings_ASourceRequestQuotingTheClaimStands` and the source-request cases of `TestCheckDeepFindings`.

- Verify procedure and coding-agents guide name `read_git` and the source request.
