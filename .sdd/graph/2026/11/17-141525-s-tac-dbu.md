Two defects in captures that cross graphs are fixed on `next/fix-dependency-capture` (ff768ee4, c2b4421f) and merged into `fza-next` at 06a25077; the full test suite and lint pass.

- A capture started in a direct dependency no longer loops at the late-entries step: the write's last late-entry check read the session's home graph instead of the dependency's, so every home entry looked late. A test drives such a capture to the write; before the fix it stopped at the late-entries step.
- The served capture schema admits a ref in the `<repo-id>:<entry-id>` form the cross-repo design allows (20260606-003344-d-cpt-s6q); before, the advertised pattern refused it although the application resolved and stored such refs. A test checks that the served pattern admits that form and that the written entry keeps the ref; before the fix it refused it. `closes` and `supersedes` stay same-graph.
