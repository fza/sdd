`verify.kinds` sets the verify mode per entry kind on `fza-fork`, closing 20261110-224950-d-tac-7mr: commit a08a563c adds the per-kind modes, the default `prose` cap while no kind is named, the local-may-only-raise rule per kind and the kind-fit pre-flight check, and commit 6bfaab83, merged as aa841ca7, widens that check to fire whenever another kind is checked more strictly than the draft's kind.

`VerifyConfig.ModeFor` in `internal/model/verifier.go` resolves each kind's mode, and `StricterKinds` lists every kind whose mode is stricter than the draft kind's, which the kind-fit check asks about. In `pkg/application/verifier.go`, `verifierFor` resolves the verifier policy and `enforcesGrounding` the grounding requirement from the draft kind's mode, so the deep exchange, the verifier chosen, the grounding reading and the deep capture's pre-flight skip follow it; `TestCaptureKinds_AKindBelowDeepSkipsTheDeepExchange` and `TestCaptureKinds_AnOffKindOwesNoGroundingReading` in `internal/proctest/capture_kinds_test.go` cover this. The configuration reference, `website/src/content/docs/reference/configuration.md`, documents `verify.kinds` and the trigger. `TestVerifyConfig_StricterKindsForAKindAtTheModeBelowAConfiguredKind` in `internal/model/verify_kinds_test.go` covers the widened trigger with `verify.mode: prose` and `verify.kinds.directive: deep`, and fails on the code before 6bfaab83.
