Entry titles for entries that already hold a summary now come from that summary alone, chosen by one constructor every caller goes through, closing 20261107-021219-d-tac-okg in commit `6eba83ba` on `pr/core`, merged into `fza-fork` as `7d4f85a0`.

`llmops.NewDescription(entry, graph, language, regenerate)` returns a `Description` holding the request, its parser and `TitleOnly()`; `llmops.Describe` builds, runs and parses it in one step. With no summary or with `regenerate` set it is the paired call with schema `summary.json`; with a summary and no title it is the title call with schema `title.json`, carrying the entry's type, layer and kind, its stored summary and the titles of its direct refs, closes and supersedes entries. Related entries without a title are left out. `Summarize`, `GenerateTitle`, `RenderSummaryPrompt` and `RenderTitlePrompt` are gone. Entry capture in `internal/handlers`, `sdd summarize` (named entries and `--force` set `regenerate`; the request renders under the graph read lock and runs outside it) and the capture in `pkg/application` go through them.

Beyond the decision: `NewDescription` returns `ErrDescribed` for an entry that already holds title and summary without `regenerate`, so a caller cannot spend a call on nothing.

Evidence: `TestNewDescriptionChoosesTheCall`, `TestNewDescriptionRefusesADescribedEntry`, `TestTitleDescriptionSendsOnlyWhatATitleNames` (no body, no related summaries in the title request) and `TestDescribeGivesTitleAndSummaryFromOneCall`. A live `sdd sync --pull` with the built binary titled an untitled entry from its summary.
