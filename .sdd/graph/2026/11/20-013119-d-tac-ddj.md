In assisted mode, a capture step whose unattended answers the agent has spent is held for the person and served as in guided mode, so the person's answer relayed verbatim in `userWords` settles it without a switch to guided.

That follows from what assisted mode is for (20261117-213336-s-tac-hlw): the person is present, and their answer relayed verbatim settles what is put to them. An unattended step such as the capture's deepUser allows the agent a limited number of answers of its own (one revise); once they are spent, the move waits for a person. The engine refused every answer at a spent step in any session where the agent answers, assisted included. A consumer project's capture therefore stayed parked after the person had confirmed the draft, with a refusal demanding a switch to guided.

In an assisted session the spent step is still held: its parked event is logged, the session lists it, and it survives a restart. The serve opens with a lane telling the agent to put the choice to the person and relay the answer verbatim as `userWords`, and the step is served in guided mode with its unit text, all options and `userWords` required. The answer is accepted directly or after resume_session, with any option. An answer carrying the agent's own reason is refused there, and so is abandon by the agent, since the person drops the move by answering abort. Auto mode still refuses, and its messages name guided or assisted as the way out.

Rejected: requiring a switch to guided to relay the answer, because the person is present in assisted mode and can answer there; letting the agent answer the spent step itself, because the unattended limit exists to put the choice to a person.

Settled: built as `24c8226b` with docs `85eef539` on `next/fix-assisted-parked-user`, merged into `fza-next`. Process tests reproduced the refusal before the fix and pass after it, and the full isolated suite and lint pass.
