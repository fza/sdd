The far floor of the ranking judge is built and merged on `fza-next`: every judge call of a gather carries three entries far from the draft, and a candidate the judge rates at or below their median in its own call is set aside in a group of its own. It builds the first mechanism of 20261121-205340-d-tac-gyk in the median form (8f34d10a, cf84d38d, 7fcc9721, 319da99a, 477f70b5, d45b6021, cb24ae7f, f277b2ed, 53de0edd). The plan stays open, because its second mechanism, the replay of past entries as whole checks, is not built.

## What is built

- The far entries. `model.NewFarFloor` in `internal/model/far_floor.go` picks three entries per draft. An entry is far when it is live, is no candidate of the gather, is not referenced or answered by the draft, is not shipped in the binary, shares no topic area with the draft and lies more than two reference hops from every entry the draft references. A topic area is the first component of a topic path. A hop is a ref, a close or a supersede, followed in either direction. The pick is a hash of the draft text's hash and the entry ID, so every gather of one draft text picks the same three.
- The judge call. `relevance.ScoreWithFar` in `pkg/llm/relevance/far.go` puts the three into each judge call in the form of every other line. A call sent again in halves after a failure carries them in every half. The call returns the candidates' scores and, as its floor, the median of the three far scores. The far scores are not kept.
- The rule. `FarFloor.Below` puts a candidate below the floor when its score is at or below the floor of the call that scored it. `gather` lists these candidates in a set-aside group of their own, named "set aside by the far floor", with and without a calibrated threshold.
- The exceptions. The rule never sets aside a candidate within two reference hops of an entry the draft references, nor one that two or more reads of the gather found.
- No floor. A gather has no floor when fewer than three entries are far from the draft, when the draft has neither a topic nor a reference, or when a judge call has no room for the three far entries and one candidate.

The floor rests on no calibration label, the record of how an earlier capture answered a pair of entries, so it needs no ledger. It sets aside from the first gather on a machine and for every judge protocol, rerankers included.

## The verdict is stored with the score

The read event of a gather stores which of its newly scored candidates the floor put below, in `PrefilterPayload.Below` beside the scores. A later gather of the same draft text scores no candidate again that the capture's reading holds a score for, and keeps each one where its first verdict put it (`model.StandingBelowFloor`). A held verdict gives way in three cases (f277b2ed):

- The draft now references an entry within two reference hops of the candidate.
- A second read of the gather finds the candidate.
- The floor is off for the judge.

In the first two cases the candidate returns to the lines to judge. A changed draft text has its candidates scored again, so their verdicts are new.

## Stored format

The build adds three fields. What the released build v0.18.0-alpha.3.fza.53 does with each is read from its source and was not run.

- `below` in the prefilter part of a session log's read event. The released build ignores the field and reads the scores without the verdicts.
- Bit 16 in the flags byte of a reader record in the relevance ledger. It marks an audit sample of a floor set-aside. The released build ignores the bit, and a compaction by the released build writes the record without it.
- `floor` in a reader line of the ledger's JSON export. The import of the released build decodes strictly and refuses a line that holds `"floor": true`.

## How the build checks the rule

The blind audit covers the floor's set-asides together with the threshold's: 3 percent of both (`model.AuditShare`) return unmarked among the lines to judge. A referenced sample of a floor set-aside counts against the floor and not against the threshold. `model.JudgeFloorHealth` turns the floor off for a judge once the latest 200 audit labels of its set-asides, at least 50, hold a weighted share of referenced samples above 2 percent. An off floor sets nothing aside, so it gets no new sample and stays off for that judge. `sdd grounding status` shows the floor's state and these counts.

## Not built, and what later plans change

- The careful form is not built. 20261121-205340-d-tac-gyk starts the rule with the lowest of the three far scores as the line and moves to the median once a proof is in. The build runs the median form from the first gather. The plan records this state itself: it names 8f34d10a and cf84d38d as holding the median form and the careful form as not built. Until the careful form is built, the floor hides more on a first gather than either plan allows without a proof.
- The steps of 20261121-210755-d-tac-d5e are not built: full, narrowed and compute-only, the replay proof behind them, the miss detectors and the proof record in the repository. That plan removes the blind samples and replaces the switch-off: a rule goes one step down on three misses from three captures, and no rule is off for good. Until it is built, the audit above is the only check of the floor, and a floor that went off stays off.
- The deep verifier's reading does not carry the rule. Its ranking calls carry no far entries, and an entry the floor set aside in the gather ranks in the verifier's reading by its score.

## Against the plan's acceptance criteria

20261121-205340-d-tac-gyk lists eight criteria.

- Met: a gather on a machine whose ledger holds no label sets aside the candidates the judge rates at or below the line of their call.
- Met: the rule sets aside no candidate within two reference hops of an entry the draft references and none that two or more reads found.
- Not met: the lowest of the three far scores as the line while the graph holds no proof. The build takes the median.
- Not met: the four criteria on the replay.
- Met in part: the configuration reference describes the far floor as built, in `website/src/content/docs/reference/configuration.md`. It describes no replay.
