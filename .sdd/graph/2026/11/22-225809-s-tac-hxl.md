The capture rewordings, the summary guard and the Ollama keep-alive of 20261121-231007-d-tac-ose are built and merged on `fza-next` (1550e01c0, f12e1daee, 37b07a640). The correction of fork point 17, 20261121-234844-d-tac-156, takes effect with it: `embedding.keep_alive` is a fork-only key until upstream has it.

## Built

- Capture wording (1550e01c0): the refusal of `refsInspected` and the grounding text of `assemble` name the current sdd session as the scope of the reads that count. Assisted playback speaks of a directive's intent in the person's terms and never names the posture; for a draft that supersedes a directive, the agent proposes that directive's intent as its inference. Neither the `gather` heading nor the `assemble` rule text states a verdict duty for a line not served in full. The summary check asks whether the title claims anything the entry's text denies.
- Summary guard (f12e1daee): a generated summary with no letter or digit, or with fewer than three words, is not stored, and one repair call generates it again.
- Ollama (37b07a640): every embedding request carries `embedding.keep_alive`, 30 minutes when unset; `sdd serve` embeds the probe text once at its start; the reachability probe of a `gather` ends within 30 seconds, and its error names the model and the host. The configuration reference describes the key.

## Criteria met

All fourteen, under these tests: `TestCapture_LifecycleTargetMustBeInspected` (refusal and `assemble` scope); `TestAssisted_ThePlaybackShowsTheDraftedReason` and `TestCapture_IntentIsShownInTheUsersTerms` (intent in the person's terms, proposed intent on a supersede); the existing directive-intent tests of `capture_reason_kinds_test` (no code default for intent); `TestRenderGather_CandidateLinesOweNoVerdict` and `TestCaptureDismissals_AssembleOwesAVerdictOnlyForEntriesServedInFull` (no verdict duty per line); `TestDescribeRegeneratesAnEmptySummary`, `TestDescribeRefusesASummaryTheRepairLeavesEmpty` and the three-dots case of `TestPairedDescriptionParsesTheAnswer` (summary guard and regression test); `TestCapture_SummaryCheckPairsEachSentenceWithTheBody` (title question); `TestOllamaEmbedder_KeepAlive`, `TestWarmEmbedder_EmbedsTheProbeOnce`, `TestServeReusesPersistentIndexAcrossRestarts` and the stalled and unreachable cases of `TestGather_SearchModeChecksTheEmbedderBeforeAnyLLMCall` (keep-alive, warm-up, probe limit and error).

## Open

- Stock sdd warns `ignoring unknown config key` for `embedding.keep_alive` in a project whose config sets it, until upstream has the key.
- The cold-load time and the memory a loaded model holds are not measured.
