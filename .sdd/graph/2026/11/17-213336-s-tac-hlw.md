`dialogue.mode: assisted` ships on `fza-next`: the agent answers every choice as in auto mode, each entry's sealed draft, with every check already run, is played back to the person once at one approval gate, and the person's approval, relayed verbatim, writes the entry as theirs. Commits fd0b3921, 2a746bbb, d0cb9e61, 4273ceec and c63e9bd5 on `next/feat-assisted-mode`, merged at 73f6f6aa, then 21396ccb, 3d60d947, de9262c2, 38847944, 920e78fb and e118c7c7.

The mode exists because a person present in an auto-mode checkout had entries written as the agent's, marked so by 20261117-175601-s-tac-69f, while guided mode puts too many questions to them.

- An approved entry carries `approved-by` naming the person, and `reason-by` and `intent-by` naming the person as the source of its reason and intent, as the participant rules of 20261106-174926-d-cpt-kx0 count a person who agrees to a decision.
- A bare approval of a decision brings a reason question: for one entry, the agent's drafted reason or the person's own words; for a batch, one reason for all, one per entry, or the person's words. No approved entry is marked `reason-by: agent`.
- A directive's intent is named plainly at playback, and approval adopts it.
- One answer can approve several sealed drafts, with words and exclusions per draft; an excluded draft goes back to drafting and holds there until revised.
- An assisted session opens naming the drafts waiting at the gate; a draft without approval stays parked there.
- Every choice sdd puts to a person, in guided and assisted mode, names the host's question tool: `AskUserQuestion` for Claude hosts including Claude Desktop, `request_user_input` in Codex where listed, else one plain-text question.
- `sdd view`, `sdd show`, catch-up and the deep verifier show `approved-by` and `reason-by`. `sdd init` offers the mode; the configuration reference's `dialogue.mode` row and the coding-agents guide describe it.

The approval word list was dropped, so nothing checks mechanically whether a bare "ok" was relayed as the reason; that is an accepted cost, and the risk of words the person never said stays with 20260924-232456-s-tac-f09.
