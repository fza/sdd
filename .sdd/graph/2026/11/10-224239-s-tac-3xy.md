The pre-flight calls take their own LLM params, closing 20261110-223542-d-tac-gej: commit b625c265, merged into `fza-fork` in bace6f29, adds `llm.preflight.params` and lays it key by key over `llm.params` for the purposes `preflight` and `preflight-extract` only.

Every other call keeps `llm.params`, and the configuration reference documents the key. `TestPreflightParamsTuneOnlyThePreflightCalls` in `internal/llm/factory/preflight_params_test.go` passes on `fza-fork`.

A live run confirms it: with `llm.preflight.params.reasoning_effort: medium` set in a local config, the stats log `.sdd/stats/llm.jsonl` records `reasoning_effort=medium` for the `preflight` call at 2026-11-10T19:21:44Z, while the writing-guide, gather-query and summarize calls before and after it keep `reasoning_effort=low`.
