A decision captured with a user present now requires the user's own words for its reason, and for a directive its intent, as directive 20261112-212433-d-tac-v7s commits: commit 7cdbbe1a on `pr/fix-capture-user-reason`, merged into `fza-fork` at 3a42b3a3.

- The capture procedure gains two optional fields, `reasonWords` and `intentWords`, collected at assemble and at a playback adjust, shown in the playback draft block and never written into the entry. `model.UserWordsOwed` says what a kind owes: every decision kind a reason, a directive also its intent, a signal nothing.
- A new predicate, `userWordsHeld`, holds the assemble step's transition to playback while the words are missing and names them, telling the agent to ask the user, end its turn and report the answer verbatim, never supplying it. It owes nothing in an autonomous session, so its placement cannot leak there.
- Outside autonomous mode the assemble intro asks for the words, the body and intent guidance point at the new fields, and playback's inference mark covers only elaboration the agent adds.
- `internal/proctest/capture_user_reason_test.go` shows a directive held without the reason and without the intent, advancing with both and playback showing the quoted words, an activity owing the reason only, a signal and an autonomous decision owing nothing; `TestUserWordsOwed` covers the kind table. Existing guided decision drafts in the proctests now carry the words.

Each guard was broken, seen to fail on its own assertion and restored: the predicate always passing, the transition without it, the autonomous exemption, the directive intent requirement and the signal exemption.

Open: how real agents respond is not yet shown; a Codex capture eval of a decision stated without its reason would show whether they now ask. An agent can still write words into `reasonWords` the user never said (20260924-232456-s-tac-f09).
