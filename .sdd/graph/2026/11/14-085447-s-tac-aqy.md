`abandon` now retries once when another attach of the same session appended to its log between abandon's load and its final append, instead of failing outright; built in b3a2ab2f on `pr/fix-abandon-retry` and merged into `fza-fork` at 9405b3c2.

## What changed

On that collision, abandon (`abandonSession` and `abandonOnce` in `pkg/application/workflow.go`) reloads the session log, replays it, abandons the running instances again and appends once more. A second collision returns the session conflict with "reorient with resume_session or start fresh" and leaves the session open.

Every other session append path already retried once after catching up with another attach's appends (a512ec23, the fix for 20261109-055313-s-tac-7j7); abandon was the one path left failing. The single retry follows the bound on automatic retries in 20260914-113737-d-cpt-qst.

## Evidence

- `TestAbandonFoldsAnAppendRacingIt` and `TestAbandonSurfacesASecondCollision` in `pkg/application/session_abandon_race_test.go` failed on the unchanged code and pass with the fix.
- Removing the retry turns the first test red; dropping the reorient suffix turns the second red. Both were restored byte-identically.
- In the full suite, `TestGraphRepoConnectedWriteFromTheCodeRepository` in `internal/cliapp` failed once under parallel load (serve EOF at the deadline) and passed when rerun alone; it does not touch abandon.
