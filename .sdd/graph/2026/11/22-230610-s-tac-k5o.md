The searches and judge timing of 20261121-221907-d-tac-4rg are built and merged on `fza-next` (adc53a80a, 2b73f5b2a, d1b639c39, 93fbfea44): every graph check searches with the draft's own text and the agent's phrase and terms on a graph of any size, candidates reach the judge in one fused order, and the judge's time limit and minimum are settings. The fix of adc53a80a also closes the gap 20261122-003705-s-tac-6bw.

## Built

- Gap 6bw (adc53a80a): `relevance.Halving` gives a call of more than one candidate at most half the time its caller's deadline leaves, so a timed-out call is halved within the pass; a single-candidate call keeps the whole time.
- Searches (2b73f5b2a): `gather` takes `query`, `terms` and `wait` over MCP and `--query`, `--term` and `--wait` on the CLI. Every check runs one vector search with the draft's text. The model-written query, its schema and the all-briefs path are gone.
- Order: `model.OrderGatherCandidates` fuses the searches' ranks by reciprocal rank fusion and orders entries only a listing found by similarity. On a tie a reference hop comes first and a done entry last; equal search scores break by ID. A done entry with a score ranks by it in the check and in the verifier's reading.
- Judge timing: `verify.judge_timeout` (1 minute, `0` for none, under 30 seconds read as 30) and `verify.judge_minimum` (50). At most 10 short of the minimum the check keeps judging; further short or failed, the agent gets the shortfall with one of four causes and the person chooses between waiting and the agent judging. A session without a person waits one further limit, then the agent judges. The session and server logs record a shortfall. Background calibration sends no judge call while a live check judges.
- Lines name a newer refining entry and no longer show which reads found an entry.

## Criteria met

All nineteen, under these tests: `TestGather_RunsTheDraftSearchTheAgentsSearchesAndTheListings`, `TestGather_SearchesWithTheDraftsTextAlone` and `TestGather_NeedsADraftASearchOrIDs` (searches, no model-written search, no all-briefs path); `TestOrderGatherCandidates_*`, `TestPlacePrefiltered_RanksDoneByScoreAndLastOnATie` and `TestPlaceVerifierReading_RanksDoneEntriesByScoreAndListsThem` (order, ties, done entries); `TestVerifyConfig_JudgeLimits` (settings); `TestJudgeLive_BoundsTheWaitByTheLimitAndTheMinimum`, `TestGather_AJudgeShortOfTheMinimumNamesItAndTheChoice`, `TestGather_AJudgeThatScoresTheWholeShortListIsNotShort`, `TestNewJudgeShortfall_NamesOneCause`, `TestJudgeShortfallText` and `TestCaptureGrounding_AnUnattendedCheckShortOfItsMinimumHasTheAgentJudge` (keep judging, shortfall, causes, unattended wait); `TestAutoCalibrate_PausesWhileACheckJudges` (calibration pause); `TestRefinedBy_*` and `TestRenderGather_LineNamesTheRefinementNotTheReads` (lines); `TestHalving_LeavesRoomForHalvesWithinTheCallersDeadline` (gap 6bw). No candidate link or entry date enters a score or rank.
