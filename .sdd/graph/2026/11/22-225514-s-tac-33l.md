The shorter repeats of 20261121-233256-d-tac-mm2 are built and merged on `fza-next` (4a259353a, 524624214, 295cd3e2e): sdd sends a framing block once per caller and again only when it changes, a short landing when a move ends, small collected values whole on `resume_session`, a deep verifier finding in full once, and after an adjust the playback text asks for the change only. The correction of fork point 37 for the framing and landing rules, 20261121-234844-d-tac-156, takes effect with it.

## Built

- Framing: sdd records the framing blocks it sent per caller and sends a block again only when its content changes. The mark that tells callers apart is the optional `caller` argument on every advancing tool; a call without it is the session's first caller. No block needs a proof to stay out.
- Landing: a move end and an abandon return the move's outcome and the choices for what comes next, and name the base step without its text.
- Resume: a collected value of up to 300 characters comes whole in `collected`; a larger one comes in `collected_large` by name with its size. The new `read_value` tool returns one value whole, paged.
- Findings: a settled finding is one line with its label, severity, axis, outcome and note. A proposal hunk carries the changed span with three words on each side. Open points go to the person by label. Settled findings ride one playback round, and the summary check carries them once.
- Playback after an adjust: the capture's playback text asks for the change only, in each mode, and for the full draft on request; guided mode also shows the full draft once before the final confirm when anything changed.

## Criteria met

All fourteen criteria, under these tests: `TestFramingServesEachCallerOncePerChange` (a block once per caller and change, every block in full to a second caller the session has not served, every block in full on `resume_session`), `TestServeChangedServesEachCallerOncePerChange`, `TestFramingHoldsNoEntry` (no proof); `TestAMoveEndsWithAShortLanding` (landing, and the base step in full on `resume_session`); `TestReadValueReturnsACollectedValueWhole` (resume values and `read_value`); `TestDeepSettledLine`, `TestBuildDeepProposal_HunkCarriesTheChangedSpanWithAFewWordsAround`, `TestCaptureDeep_SettledFindingsRideOnePlaybackRound`, `TestCaptureDeep_AnOpenPointGoesToTheUserByItsLabel` (findings); `TestCapture_PlaybackAfterAnAdjustShowsTheChange` (playback). Two criteria hold by the capture spec without a test of their own: a finding in full only at `deepAnswer`, and the summary check carrying the settled findings once (`20260703-094500-d-prc-cap.md`, changed in 524624214).
