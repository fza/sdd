Graph grounding, the draft verifier with its guide timing, configured hooks and multi-round delegate child sessions are built and merged into `fza-fork` at 58db706f (merge 7e31955b). Each feature sits on its own pull-request branch: `pr/feat-grounding` (cea22a90, with the event store from `pr/feat-event-store` at 032fa77d), `pr/feat-verifier` (801684cb), `pr/feat-hooks` (ac9b990c) and `pr/feat-child-rounds` (2e82e973). The full suite, vet and lint pass on `fza-fork`.

- **Grounding (20261109-004506-d-cpt-udz).** All fourteen slices of the attached design: `gather` and the reading with reason codes, the relevance ledger as committed segment files with compaction, the Jev prefilter, Cohere rerank and a calibrated cosine floor, calibration, blind audit and drift steps in `sdd grounding status`, `sdd.recheck` items with healing and the hand-edit scan, and `--session` on the CLI reads and `sdd new` with `sdd dismiss`.
- **Verifier and guide timing (20261109-004729-d-cpt-xs5, 20261109-005108-d-cpt-wcj).** `verifier.mode` light runs pre-flight over the reading; deep runs a verify task in a child session across rounds, launched by subagent, `claude`, `codex` or a custom driver, with `verifier.skip`. In guided captures the guide and the verifier run after playback approval, with a delta confirm when either changed the draft.
- **Child sessions (20261109-004845-d-cpt-ldx, 20261109-004924-d-cpt-cxw).** Interim deliveries, parent-filled gates, a round cap, relaunch with the same child handles, and sdd resuming a headless CLI by its stored session id. The parent handle stays out of every delegate prompt.
- **Hooks (20261109-005138-d-tac-i61).** Hooks from the personal config layers run through `sh -c` at every step event, `*` and `entry.written`, in parallel with a 600 s default timeout; findings join on the findings-channel steps. The 15 deep-verify steps share a new `stage` step key, so they raise one `capture.verify` event set, closed by a `deepDone` gate.

Named deviations, each left open as its own gap: continuing the same delegate through a harness has no per-harness launch configuration, so later rounds relaunch; `sdd config` shows only the top layer's hooks; two probes of the headless launch (whether `claude` keeps an MCP server under its safe mode, whether `codex exec` can skip AGENTS.md) are unverified. One commit subject (2572af13) is 101 characters, over the project limit.
