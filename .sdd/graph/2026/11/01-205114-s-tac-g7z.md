`view` has a public data form with one shared renderer, and the `show`, `search` and `view` text fields of `pkg/application` are deprecated with no reader left in the repository: commits `8cd1b315` and `63784feb` on `pr/feat-view-sections`, merged into `fza-fork` as `cf8229d8`.

- `ShowResult.Entries`, `SearchResult.Results` and `ViewResult.Sections` carry `Deprecated:` notes and are still filled. The MCP server and the engine's `entryChains` inject and view query render from `Items`, `Hits` and `ViewResult.ViewSections` instead. `SearchResult.Notices` is new, since MCP search text joins its notices with blank lines and `Notice` cannot rebuild that.
- `types.ViewSection` names its render, header name, brief flag, dependency repo and match count, and fills one of seven shape fields; entries in every shape are `types.ListedEntry`, the value search and view entry lines are formatted from. One conversion, `(*query.ViewResult).ViewSections`, feeds the CLI, the application and the presenter tests.
- Under `sessions.token_saving: off` MCP `view` returns `view_sections` (and `known_participants` when a participant filter matched nothing) with `sections` empty; with `on` its text is unchanged. The MCP field is `view_sections` because `sections` already names the text.
- The exported presenter entry-line functions are gone; nothing called them.

Output is byte-identical: whole-graph renderings, unbounded and budgeted, match before and after across all seven renders, brief, rank, `expand(refs)`, cuts and WIP markers. Breaking the WIP marker mapping fails the conversion test and a view render test.

Removing the three deprecated fields stays open.
