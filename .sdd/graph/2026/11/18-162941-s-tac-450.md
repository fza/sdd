`sdd grounding rescore` and `sdd grounding seed --from-refs` now finish past failing subjects and take `--judge <name>`, shipped on `fza-next` (d41c8f25, 3be4a206). A failed subject is recorded with its cause and the pass goes on; both commands list the failures at the end and exit non-zero, and the pairs left unscored are picked up by the next run. `--judge` scores with one judge, named as `sdd grounding status` names it, or by the alias `ranking` or `prefilter`.

This extends the rescore and seed commands of 20261114-003118-s-tac-0vl.
