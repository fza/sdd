The pre-flight quote guard of directive 20261110-212348-d-tac-z4m is built in 8dd61538 and merged into `fza-fork` in bace6f29. `internal/llmops/quote.go` adds `QuoteLocatesIn` and `flagUnlocated`: plain and prose pre-flight run each finding's quote against the call's user prompt, and a finding whose quote locates nothing stays in the verdict, its observation opens with the unverified note, and `high` drops to `medium`. The response schemas `preflight.json` and `preflight_light.json` require the `quote` field, and `verdict.tmpl` asks for it. `preflight_quote_test.go` covers the guard and `quote_locate_test.go` the matching.

This closes the gap 20261110-150803-s-tac-9u1 together with 20261110-211909-s-tac-n1p: under `deep` the pre-flight model no longer runs, and under `prose` or `off` an invented finding can no longer block the write. The gap's other items: `llm.debug` records the prompt and raw response of every checker call (n1p), and the stats log keeps recording in the `sdd`-owned clone after a graph-repository move. Two items stay unmet and are dropped with the closure. The gap asked to check a finding's category against a closed set once the rubric names one; the rubric still names none, so categories stay unchecked. The replay of the failing third pre-flight was not run; a deep capture no longer calls pre-flight after a deep exchange, so its question no longer bears on any path. Unparsed responses recorded as successes stay with their own open gap 20260831-093815-s-tac-k7d.

A prose-mode trial on 2026-11-10 with `glm-5.3-flash` at `reasoning_effort` `medium` returned three findings whose quotes all located; none was flagged.
