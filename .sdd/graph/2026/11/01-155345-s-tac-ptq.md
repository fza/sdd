Per-checkout records protecting the index versions other checkouts still read are implemented: commit `3018fbef` on `pr/feat-cache-prune`, merged into `fza-fork` as `f556666e`, plus `afae1561` on `pr/feat-cache-prune-fork`. It fulfills 20261101-153339-d-tac-pnl.

Every CLI fill and every search over the MCP runtime writes the checkout's current entry hashes to `<store>/used/checkouts/<hash of the graph directory>.json`. The record is rewritten when the hashes change, and otherwise only touched once a day. A fill writes it before it reads the manifest to plan. Each pruning lock session reads the records under the store lock and keeps the union of the versions named in records fresher than the retention, together with the launching checkout's own. It deletes records older than the retention.

Tests cover:
- a version kept for a second checkout;
- the same version deleted, together with its record, once the record is older than the retention;
- a record refreshed a day later;
- a record written by a fill and by a search.

Each new check was broken once to confirm its test fails. On the fork, where search hashes depend on the store's rune cap, the `afae1561` adaptation hashes the record under that cap. Breaking that adaptation is caught first by the existing check that a published entry's content matches its descriptor, which masks the new test's own assertion.

The race the decision accepted stays: a pruning session that reads the records just before a checkout's first record write can delete a version that checkout's fill then skips, until its next fill re-embeds it.
