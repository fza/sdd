Only directives and plans now owe a reason at capture, a directive also its intent, in guided, auto and assisted mode; other decisions store a reason when one is given (b62153a4, cd9032b3 on `fza-next`). Cause: an actor captured beside a role drew a reason question, because the served text demanded a reason whatever the decision's kind. `model.UserWordsOwed` is now the one rule every mode reads.

- **Guided:** the agent proposes a reason inferred from the dialogue and the graph through the host's question tool, as one option beside Other, and stores only what the person picked or wrote.
- **Assisted:** no separate reason question; the inferred reason and intent show in the one playback, and the approval adopts them.
- **Auto:** infers like assisted and keeps its agent mark and limit.

This narrows the rule of 20261112-212433-d-tac-v7s, built in 20261112-213854-s-tac-1lr, under which every decision captured with a user present owed the user's own reason. It also drops the reason question the assisted mode of 20261117-213336-s-tac-hlw put after a bare approval.
