No capture is refused or held for a missing reason on any kind, and a directive's intent is no longer demanded in the person's own words; a directive still needs an intent value, and a reason or an intent that is given is stored.

## The rule

- No kind owes a reason at capture. A directive, a plan and every other decision are captured with or without one. A signal carries none.
- A directive needs an intent value: `pending`, `guiding` or `settled`. This is the rule stock sdd has (`model.DirectiveIntentRequirement`), unchanged.
- The person's own words for a directive's intent (`intentWords`) are no longer demanded in guided mode.
- A reason and an intent are stored when given. The agent may propose both and shows them in the playback.
- The marks stay as they are wherever a reason or an intent is stored. `reason-by` names the person when the reason is in their own words and holds `agent` when the person approved a reason the agent proposed. `intent-by` names the person for an intent they chose in their own words and holds `agent` for a proposed intent they approved. Approving a proposal does not count as the person's own words.
- An entry without a reason carries no `reason-by`.

The rule holds in guided, assisted and auto mode alike.

## Why

The fork is this repository's own line of sdd, built on `fza-next` beside upstream (20261117-092355-d-prc-3vb). Stock sdd is upstream's build. Stock has no `reason` field, no `reasonWords` and no `intentWords`. It enforces only the intent value. An agent or a person who knows stock meets a hold in the fork that stock does not have. The fork stays compatible with stock sdd, and the reason requirement is one of the differences it gives up. The owner's instruction, verbatim: "remove the reason dor directive / plan enforcement".

Three other capture checks stay in every project: reading before writing (`verify.mode`, default `prose`), the hold until the agent's client matches an actor in auto mode, and the delegate check under `verify.mode: deep`.

Rejected: keeping the reason requirement for directives and plans. Rejected too: skipping all of the fork's capture checks in a project that sets none of the fork's keys.

## What this replaces

20261118-173349-d-tac-8jh narrowed the reason requirement to two kinds: a directive, with its intent, and a plan owed a reason at capture, and every other decision stored one when given. This entry removes the requirement for those two kinds as well, so the per-kind split has nothing left to separate. What 8jh said about storing a given reason and about showing an inferred reason and intent in the playback stands and is restated in the rule above.

20261118-162948-s-tac-kd7 built the per-kind rule. Three parts of that build change: `model.UserWordsOwed`, the `reason` field requirement on a directive or plan an agent decided in auto mode or a person approved in assisted mode, and the reason steps of the capture procedure in guided and assisted mode, with the `userWordsHeld` gate that holds assemble until the words arrive. In assisted mode the playback of 20261117-213336-s-tac-hlw keeps showing a proposed reason and intent. The agent marks of auto mode from 20261117-175601-s-tac-69f, `decided-by: agent` and the `agentLimit` step, stay as they are, except that a person taking on a draft parked at `agentLimit` is no longer asked for a reason in `reasonWords` or an intent in `intentWords`. The model check for the marks stays as it is: it already asks for `reason-by` only where a reason is stored.

## Also proposed upstream

The upstream proposal that carries the fork's capture checks drops the same two demands, so the proposal and the fork ask a person for the same things. The intent value stays required there as in stock.
