The deep verifier's grounding reading is now ranked and tiered so it fits a verifier's context on a large graph, shipped on `fza-next` (fb987165, 7dbfb547, dbd9059c, 05789a2f). Served whole, the reading on a large graph ran to 84 pages, about 980k characters, past any verifier's context.

The reading now serves:

- **In full, with no cap:** the declared targets (refs, closes, supersedes, approves), the entries the drafter read in full, and lifecycle neighbours: entries closing or superseding a target, or closed or superseded by one.
- **Briefs:** entries given a verdict.
- **Title and first summary sentence:** other entries one ref from a target, at most 50 by rank.
- **The ranked rest:** the top 5 in full, the next 100 by title and first summary sentence, then up to 300 by title, beyond that a count line. Once the prefilter is calibrated, its set-aside threshold replaces the count of 100. Done entries rank last and never serve in full.

Every line-listed entry sits in one closing block, "Listed, not served", which the verifier opens with `show`. Without a judge's score the top 5 were the first listed entries, base procedures among them, whose bodies overran the last page's bound; unscored entries now fill the top only while every ranked entry fits it (05789a2f).

This departs from 20261113-235104-d-tac-ypu, which kept every listed entry as a block until the recall bound held and rejected a fixed top K: on a large graph that uncalibrated reading is the one that overran. The calibrated threshold rule of 20261114-003118-s-tac-0vl still applies once the bound holds.

`sdd grounding seed --from-refs` labels each entry with what it refs, closes or supersedes and scores the pairs, so a judge calibrates from the graph's own refs instead of waiting for about two labels per live capture. `sdd grounding status` and `sdd serve` warn while the judge is uncalibrated.

Builds on the verifier fixes 20261117-192843-s-tac-19d and reshapes the reading of the verify procedure 20261109-010000-d-prc-vfy.
