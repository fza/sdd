Two capture defects are fixed: deep findings accepted without an edit now reach the drafter before playback, and a body re-staged at playback goes through the checks again before any confirm binds to it. Both showed in an assisted capture on a consumer graph, after the single playback of 20261123-111740-s-tac-bl3.

A deep round whose accepted findings carried no edit went straight to playback, because the proposal counted as empty when it had no hunk and nothing open for the user. The person was then asked to approve a draft still carrying the accepted gaps. The proposal now counts those findings too: `deepProposal` lists them as the drafter's to fix, without a diff when there is no hunk.

A body re-staged at playback refused every answer until the change was played back, the `adjust` reporting the change included, and the re-served playback carried a draft no check had judged; the person's next confirm then started the deep round after their approval. This narrows the first fix of 20261030-161232-s-tac-ij3, which refused any answer over an unserved change, and the confirm path of 20261113-222804-s-tac-k68, which played a re-staged file back at the same step. The refusal now covers only a confirm, and a refused confirm goes back through `assemble` and its checks, named by the new `redraft` step key on the playback; an adjust reporting the re-staged file stands. In an assisted session the returning draft is held for the person's approval afresh.

Commit `c5bd10186` on `fza-next` carries both fixes and the coding-agents guide update. The upstream branches take them apart: `0a313a074` on `up/verification` (findings without an edit), `e0a44bcf0` on `up/staged-capture-bodies` (re-staged body), both merged into `fza-integration` at `7e062e402`. Procedure tests reproduce both paths, a second deep round raising findings without hunks and a re-stage before an adjust and before a confirm; each failed before its fix. Five existing tests that expected the capture to skip `deepProposal` now take that step. Each new guard was broken and caught by its test.
