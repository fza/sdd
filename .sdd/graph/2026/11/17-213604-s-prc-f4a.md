The fork released v0.18.0-alpha.3.fza.49 on 2026-11-17 as a prerelease of `fza/sdd` (https://github.com/fza/sdd/releases/tag/v0.18.0-alpha.3.fza.49), built from `fza-next` at ca041823 on upstream v0.18.0-alpha.3 (20261107-123225-s-prc-vgd): binaries for macOS and Linux (amd64, arm64), checksums and `install.sh`, with the Homebrew cask `fza/tap/sdd` at the same version. It follows v0.18.0-alpha.3.fza.48 (20261117-094523-s-prc-d2t) on the branch layout of 20261117-092355-d-prc-3vb.

The release brings the agent decision marks (20261117-175601-s-tac-69f): auto-mode entries carry `decided-by: agent`, `run-by` and, on decisions, the agent's `reason`, with the MCP client's actor as participant; decisions past the agent limit park for a person; a mark follows agent-reasoned chains until a person's reason or `approves:` ends it, and `sdd new --approves` writes that field. It also brings the cross-graph capture fixes (20261117-141525-s-tac-dbu), the first-clone cooldown fix (20261117-172339-s-tac-mga) and the playback reopen after a mode switch (20261117-173207-s-tac-up6). Its notes list three breaking changes, all in auto mode: the agent limit, the required `reason` on decisions, and the agent named as participant.
