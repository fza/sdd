The required text question of 20261121-230157-d-tac-ym8, in the form revised by 20261122-234358-d-tac-lv2, is built and merged on `fza-next`: every response that starts, resumes or advances a session ends with questions about served text, the next advancing call must answer them, and a right answer serves repeated text short while `lost` or a wrong answer serves it in full. The anchor proofs are gone.

## Built

- 1402398a7 and eb2d417f4: `internal/model/question.go` builds and matches the questions; `anchor.go` and the tags in served text are gone. The engine records each served text and every question asked as session events, so a restarted server still checks an answer. `start_procedure`, `next`, `park` and `abandon` take a required `answer`; the `held` argument, the `unproved` and `proof_notice` result fields and the proof guide are removed. The coding-agents guide describes the question.
- 7924304ad, 66137e59b and 1c6ffe59b: the revised form. Four kinds, phrase and asked words as one run on one line without arrows, signs, code spans or entry IDs, at most 90 characters; two questions per response; no question about a text the agent or the person wrote; late catches counted and shown by `sdd sessions`.
- d57ca2f07 and aacf047b4: no asked run ends on a stop word, and the served answer example follows that rule.
- 37216f546: no question uses a phrase that a framing block or notice also holds, so an agent cannot answer from the wrong text.
- 5d86a7a30: no question shares its answer with an earlier one, so a stale answer from the call history cannot pass.

## Criteria met

The plan's nine criteria, as the revision reads them: questions end every advancing response (`TestTextQuestionEndsEveryAdvancingResponse`, `TestAResponseAsksTwoQuestionsAboutTwoTexts`); the answer is required and `lost` is valid (`TestTextQuestionAnswerIsRequired`, `TestAdvancingToolsRequireTheAnswer`, `TestIsLost`); short after a right answer and full after `lost` or a wrong one (`TestTextQuestionAnswerDecidesShortOrFull`, `TestARightAnswerServesRepeatsShort`, `TestBothAnswersMustBeRight`); a second agent on the handle (`TestServedMemoryIsPerAgentContext`); no question twice (`TestNoQuestionIsAskedTwice`, `TestNoAnswerAnswersTwoQuestions`); no tag in served text; no counting question (`TestQuestionKindsNeedNoSentenceBoundary`); the proof arguments gone. The revision's rules: `TestNoQuestionAsksAboutAnAuthoredText`, `TestTextQuestionAsksNothingTheAgentWrote`, `TestNoAskedRunEndsOnAStopWord`, `TestNoQuestionPhraseOccursInATextServedOncePerChange`, `TestALostAnswerRightAfterAShortServeCountsALateCatch`, `TestSessionsShowsCompactionsCaughtLate`.

## Runs

The hardened build, measured in the revision: 0 of 60 false rights after compaction for Opus and for Sonnet, in context Opus 92 of 93 responses right and Sonnet 89 of 93. On the first build, real Claude Code sessions on 396 entries of this graph answered on every call: Opus 15 of 19 right, Sonnet 15 of 16, every `lost` with the text still in context.
