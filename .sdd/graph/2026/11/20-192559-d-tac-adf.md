Accepting the deep verifier's proposal now merges the drafter's own fixes beside it, and a body staged from a file no longer blocks the accept.

sdd builds the proposal only from findings that carry an edit (`model.DeepChanges`), as the verify procedure (20261109-010000-d-prc-vfy) sets it up, and a finding may come without one. Since the serve token-cost plan (20261112-075604-d-tac-t4r), an accept that reported no draft field made sdd write the proposal into the draft. An accept that reported any field switched that write off, and sdd then refused a draft that differed from the proposal. So a drafter who agreed with a finding without an edit could not fix it through accept. Within the step, only contest and escalate remained: a contest spends one of the three verifier deliveries, and an escalation puts the point to the user. When the proposal rewrote the body and the body came from a staged file, sdd refused the accept until the drafter staged the proposal's text itself. Seen in a consumer project's captures.

The accept option of the capture procedure's `deepProposal` step (20260703-094500-d-prc-cap) now merges, in `applyDeepProposal` (`pkg/application/workflow_deep_proposal.go`):

- The fields the proposal changes take its values. The fields the accept reports stand beside them.
- sdd refuses, by name, a reported field set otherwise than the proposal sets it. When the proposal rewrites the body, it also refuses a full `body` that differs from the proposal's body, and any named `bodyHandle`.
- A reported `bodyPatch` applies over the proposal's body (`model.MergeDeepBody`). sdd refuses a pair that touches text a finding's edit changes, naming the pair and the finding.
- When the proposal rewrites a body bound to a staged file, sdd writes the body inline and clears the handle. The playback confirm reads a staged file again (20261113-222804-s-tac-k68), and without the handle it cannot write the file's text back over the proposal.
- The step lists the findings without an edit and says how to fix them through accept.

The check that refused a draft differing from the proposal is removed, because after the merge a draft differs by design. The refusals above take its place: text a finding's edit changes stays the verifier's, and changing it takes a contest.

Rejected:

- Requiring an edit for every finding from the verifier. A finding with no single right edit would then force repair rounds on the verifier.
- Leaving contest and escalate as the only routes. Each costs a delivery or a question to the user, and accepts over a staged body would keep failing.

Accepted cost: accept collects `bodyHandle`, so sdd can clear it. A new handle reported with a proposal that leaves the body alone therefore swaps the staged body, as staging already allows.

A model test covers the body merge, and the full test suite passes. Nothing follows: the behaviour is built and merged, and no point stays open.
