Only a directive, with its intent, and a plan owe a reason at capture; an aspiration, activity, role, focus, procedure or contract owes none and stores a reason when one is given, and a signal owes none.

Directives and plans weigh options, so their reason records why one route won. Role, focus and procedure describe; an activity dispatches work whose shape is known; an aspiration's why restates the aspiration. The rule was prompted by a person asked for a reason while capturing an actor and a role (20261118-162948-s-tac-kd7).

## How the reason is reached, per dialogue mode

- **Guided:** the agent proposes a reason inferred from the dialogue and the graph through the host's question tool, and stores only what the person picked or wrote.
- **Assisted:** no separate reason question; the inferred reason and intent show in the one playback, and the approval adopts them. This drops the reason question that the assisted mode of 20261117-213336-s-tac-hlw put after a bare approval.
- **Auto:** infers like assisted, and keeps the agent marks of 20261117-175601-s-tac-69f: `decided-by: agent` on the entry, and the `agentLimit` step that parks what an agent may not decide alone.

## What this narrows

The closed directive 20261112-212433-d-tac-v7s made every decision captured with a user present owe the user's own reason. Its rule now holds for directives and plans only. Guided mode also reverses 20261113-221325-s-tac-lt8, under which the agent asked openly and offered no reason of its own, not even as an answer option: it now puts its inferred reason to the person as one option beside their own words.

Settled: 20261118-162948-s-tac-kd7 delivered this rule, so no follow-up is owed.
