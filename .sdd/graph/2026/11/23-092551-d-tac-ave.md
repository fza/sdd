A check answer whose parts are one right answer and one wrong answer credits the right part: sdd serves short every text whose latest copy is at least as new as the text that part asked about, and the older texts in full. `lost` for any part, every part wrong, or a part count other than the round's still credits nothing and empties the held set. This revises the answer rule of the text question revision (20261122-234358-d-tac-lv2), which required both answers right, and refines the held-set rule of the linked-sessions plan (20261122-105026-d-tac-wl5), under which a wrong answer emptied the held set, as its build (20261122-231759-s-tac-qpo) does. It changes the build of the required question (20261122-234739-s-tac-vox), which served every text in full after a wrong part. The late-catch count of lv2 follows the same line: only an answer crediting no question right after a short serve counts as a compaction caught late, no longer a wrong part beside a right one.

A wrong answer beside a right one is a misread with the text in view: in the hardened re-run that lv2 records, every wrong part came with the text in context, and after compaction all 240 answer parts were `lost`, none wrong. The agent's context runs in one line from old to new, so a right answer proves the text it asked about and every newer text. Emptying the held set on such a misread resent every instruction lane and report schema; the texts of one capture step came to 20,641 characters in full. `lost` stays strict because a compaction summary can carry the answer to one pending question: with two questions per response, one came back right from a summary in 2 to 5 of 60 responses, both in none.

Rejected: crediting a right part beside `lost` too, since it reopens that summary route and lets sdd point at text the agent no longer holds; keeping the rule, since every in-view misread then resends every text once. Accepted cost: a wrong part may be a guess, and the right part beside it then holds texts the guess says nothing about.

Settled: commit `aae7e4089` on `fza-next` builds it (`model.RoundCredit`, `BeginCall` in `internal/engine/pieces.go`), with the served answer instructions and the coding-agents guide updated; nothing follows.
