Held gates now list only the conditions a step still owes, built on `pr/fix-gate-diagnostics` in a32eee39 and merged into `fza-fork` in 161484d0; this delivers the directive 20261110-093504-d-tac-4w0 and fixes the gap 20261110-015634-s-tac-e4c.

- `Predicate` carries a `Selector` flag, off by default. It is set on `autonomousDialogue`, `verifyBeforePlayback`, `guideDue` and `noHookFindings`, and on two pure config switches no shipped guard needs yet, `verifierOn` and `deepSkipWeighed`.
- `GuardExpr.Blocking` walks each clause with its polarity, pushing `not` through `and` and `or`. A conjunction failing on a selector is ruled out and lists nothing; a failing `or` lists the clauses of its disjuncts that are not ruled out.
- A predicate under `not` is listed only when it is true, with the message "predicate X holds", since a `FailMessage` reads for the false case.
- A guided capture refused at `assemble` before any reading now serves only the missing-reading line (`TestCaptureGrounding_RefusesUntilTheCaptureIsRead`).
- `internal/engine/gate_diagnostics_test.go` covers a selector-gated transition, a negated clause and step guards over synthetic specs.

The selector rule also applies to step guards: in `playbackConfirmed or verifyBeforePlayback` a guided capture sees only the playback line. When selectors rule out a step guard entirely, the engine lists its blocking clauses unfiltered, so a held step always names a reason and the stale-playback reopening still finds `playbackConfirmed`.

Each new guard was broken and caught on its own assertion, then restored. Only the engine test covers the selector skip.
