## The value

Measured over the recorded sessions of another project of the owner's, on the build of 20261122-231439-s-tac-it2: 8 captures with 14 draft versions, and 11 checks after a capture's first. Every check logged its 20 nearest entries, so the overlap needed no embedding call. The counts come from that project's session logs, which stay outside this repository, so no record is attached.

- Real edits: the 5 checks on a changed draft kept 19 or 20 of the 20, and the rule never fired. No draft changed its subject, so these logs cannot show whether a value catches a real move.
- A proxy for a move: the first checks of different captures compared with each other, 28 pairs. Pairs on different subjects kept 0 to 14 of 20. Half lets 8 of 25 such pairs pass, all between neighbouring subjects under one topic. The pairs on one subject kept 7, 9 and 10.

Fewer than 15 fires on all 25 pairs on different subjects and stays clear of every real edit seen. The value is built on `fza-next` in 2f83ee8da (`DraftMoved` in `internal/model/kept_judgements.go`).

Rejected:

- Keeping half, recorded as unproven on real moves. Per the proxy, a move between neighbouring subjects keeps stale low judgements.
- A scripted run with real subject changes before choosing. It spends model calls and time on a value the logs already bound from both sides.

Accepted cost: the value rests on a proxy, different captures rather than one draft that moved. A draft that drifts within a dense topic gets more judge calls.

## No rejudge call for the verifier

Decision 4 lets the drafter and the verifier each have sdd judge again. The drafter has it: `rejudge` on `gather`. The verifier gets none. It opens any entry by ID with `show` and runs its own `search`, and its reading already runs the capture's searches against the final draft, where a low judgement whose entry those searches rank among the first 20 is judged again, and an entry a stale low judgement still sets aside stays within its reach.

Rejected:

- A `rejudge` flag on `reread` that judges every candidate again and serves the reading afresh.
- A report of its own that judges again and reorders the lines in place.

Each adds judge calls and a change to the verify procedure for entries the verifier already reaches.

Accepted cost: set-aside entries stay counts in the verifier's reading, and it reaches them only through its own search.
