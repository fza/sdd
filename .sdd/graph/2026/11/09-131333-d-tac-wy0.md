Repeated sync rounds of a local-path connected repository are bounded by a refresh scope per engine call: `EnsureReposFresh` runs each dependency clone's sync round at most once within one workflow operation, while access is still asked on every resolve. The scope is planted in `WorkflowSession.setOperation` through `handlers.WithRefreshScope`; outside a scope the behaviour is unchanged.

This settles the choice the local-path gap (20261109-103623-s-tac-g82) left open between its two candidates, and closes that gap: one capture confirm against a local-path remote fetches once instead of seven times. It extends the per-machine git gate's intent of one sync round per cooldown (20261107-095427-d-tac-yux) to local targets within a call, without putting a network limit on a remote that needs none.

Rejected: a cooldown for local targets. It is a time limit on targets that need none, and it would hide an `inbound.accept` withdrawn a moment earlier until the cooldown ended, while the scope honours a withdrawal at once. Also rejected: remembering resolved projects per call, because access must be asked again on every resolve so a revoke within the call takes effect.

Settled: the scope is built and merged into `fza-fork` (9ffe62e9), recorded in the follow-up done (20261109-111708-s-tac-tuk), so nothing remains to build. Accepted cost: application calls outside a workflow operation get no scope; each of them resolves a dependency once today.
