The fork released v0.18.0-alpha.3.fza.50 on 2026-11-17 as a prerelease of `fza/sdd` (https://github.com/fza/sdd/releases/tag/v0.18.0-alpha.3.fza.50), built from `fza-next` at e118c7c7 on upstream v0.18.0-alpha.3: binaries for macOS and Linux (amd64, arm64), checksums and `install.sh` from a release workflow run that succeeded, with the Homebrew cask `fza/tap/sdd` at the same version, installed on the owner's machine. It follows v0.18.0-alpha.3.fza.49 (20261117-213604-s-prc-f4a).

The release brings `dialogue.mode: assisted` (20261117-213336-s-tac-hlw), in which the person approves each checked draft once and the entry is theirs, and every choice sdd puts to a person names the host's question tool, `AskUserQuestion` for every Claude host including Claude Desktop since e118c7c7. It also brings the deep verifier's reading of a whole draft across pages, with each ref's target class and admissible kinds, and one-line delegate replies (20261117-192843-s-tac-19d), and `sdd serve --output-schema` (20261117-213012-s-tac-vxh).

Its notes list three breaking changes, all in auto mode: a capture whose client matches no actor waits until an actor naming the client is recorded (20261117-212831-s-tac-8o2); a client that sends no name cannot capture; and a decision drafted in auto mode and confirmed in guided mode takes the person's own reason and becomes their entry (20261117-212559-s-tac-3lu). The participant of the client that started a capture, alias matches in graph health and a retry wait after a failed first clone ship as other changes.
