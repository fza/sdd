The credential scan keeps prose out of the vector index on false alarms, and nothing lets a reader clear one. `sdd index` logged "kept a chunk out of the vector index" for entry 20260420-163043-s-stg-ljq, reason `generic-api-key`. The chunk holds no credential. Its attachment opens with a markdown heading: the word "Key", a second word, a colon, then two lowercase words joined by a slash. The gitleaks `generic-api-key` rule reads the heading as a key name and a value. The slash-joined pair matches the value pattern and clears the rule's 3.5 entropy threshold.

Expected: plain prose stays searchable. The check in `internal/handlers/index_pipeline.go` (`secrets.IndexableChunk`) exists to keep real credentials out of the store, not words that look like an assignment.

The cost: the chunk is missing from vector search, while the entry and its other chunks are indexed. Entries are immutable, so the text can't be reworded, and `internal/secrets` has no way to mark a hit as reviewed: no inline allow marker, no fingerprint list. The same text is refused wherever the scan runs. A capture or a memory write that quotes the heading fails the same way, and so does printing it through a credential-redacting shell guard.

Candidate remedies (unsettled reasoning): narrow `generic-api-key` so a value with no digit and no mixed case passes; or let a graph keep a reviewed-hit list (gitleaks' fingerprint form), which the index and capture checks both consult.
