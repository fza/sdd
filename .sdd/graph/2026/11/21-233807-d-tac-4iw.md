In a capture's graph check, a judgement stays with the capture when the draft changes, the deep verifier's reading searches again against the final draft, and a judge without thresholds, a judge without scores and a project without an embedding model each have a rule.

Eight decisions state this. They refine two plans. The plan 20261121-221907-d-tac-4rg decides how a graph check finds, orders and judges its candidates. The plan 20261121-223938-d-tac-rqi decides what the agent and the deep verifier receive of them. Both stay in force. This plan answers points they leave open and changes two of their rules, named under decisions 1 and 8.

The terms are those of the two plans. A graph check is one `gather` call inside a capture. The judge scores each candidate against the draft, and the score places the candidate as likely bearing, in doubt or set aside. A judgement is that score. A low judgement places its entry as in doubt or set aside. The merged order is the order in which candidates go to the judge.

## 1. The verifier's reading searches again

When sdd builds the deep verifier's reading, it runs the capture's searches again against the final draft: the agent's search phrase and keyword terms, the vector search with the final draft's text, and the listings of the final draft's topics. The merged order over these reads places the verifier's candidates. The candidates stay the entries the capture's checks listed, joined by the entries the new searches find. A candidate the new searches do not find is ordered by its similarity to the draft, as 20261121-223938-d-tac-rqi orders a candidate no check placed.

This replaces the rule in decision 9 of that plan that the verifier's reading runs no search and takes each candidate's place from the capture's checks. Those checks ordered earlier versions of the draft. An order taken from them carries the drafter's view into the verifier's reading, and the verifier starts with as little bias as the reading can give it.

Today the verifier's reading is drawn from the union of every credited read of the capture and runs no search (`captureReadingOf` in `pkg/application/workflow_capture.go`, `planRanking` in `pkg/application/workflow_ranking.go`).

Rejected:

- The place the capture's earlier checks gave. It is the bias named above.
- Similarity to the final draft alone. It ignores the keyword hits, the reason 20261121-221907-d-tac-4rg rejects it for the check.

Accepted cost: one more round of searches per verifier's reading, and judge calls for the candidates those searches add. Decision 2 limits that judging to the candidates without a judgement.

## 2. A judgement belongs to the capture and the entry

sdd keeps a judgement per capture, judge and entry. It stays when the draft changes. A check and the verifier's reading send the judge only the candidates that hold no judgement in the capture, and the judge always scores against the latest draft.

Today a score is keyed by the judge and a hash of the draft text, which is the draft's kind, topics and body (`RelevanceSubjectHash` and `RelevanceText` in `internal/model/prefilter.go`). `Reading.Scores` returns no score given against an earlier draft, and `Reading.foldPrefilter` starts an empty set when the hash changes. The scores of a background ranking pass are read by the same hash (`rankedScores` in `pkg/application/workflow_ranking.go`). Every change to the draft therefore discards every score, and the next check judges every candidate again. With decision 1, the verifier's reading would do the same.

Rejected:

- Every judgement kept, whatever changes. A low judgement given before the draft changed its subject stays wrong.
- Every candidate judged again on any change, as today.

Accepted cost: a kept judgement answers an older text. An entry judged low before the draft changed may bear now. Decisions 3 to 5 limit that.

## 3. A second look at kept low judgements

A judgement that places its entry as likely bearing is kept as it is. An entry with a kept low judgement is judged again under two rules.

Per entry. The entry is judged again when the searches of a new check, or of the verifier's reading, rank it among their best 20, or when the draft gains a topic the entry stands under or a reference one reference step from the entry. The best 20 are counted in the merged order: each single search lists up to 20 hits (`gatherSearchLimit` in `pkg/application/gather.go`), so a count per search would take in every hit.

By the draft's meaning. sdd compares the 20 entries nearest to the draft as it was judged with the 20 nearest to the draft now. Both lists come from the vector search with the draft's text, which every check runs under 20261121-221907-d-tac-4rg. When fewer than half of the earlier 20 are among the current 20, the draft has moved. Every entry with a low judgement is then judged again, and the current draft becomes the draft as judged. The draft as judged is the draft of the capture's first check until this rule moves it.

"Half" is a first value. It is checked against recorded draft versions before the rule ships.

Rejected:

- A second look once more than a quarter of the draft's text changed. A share of text says how much was typed, not whether the subject moved, and a small entry reaches a quarter with one edit.
- A fixed vector distance between the draft as judged and the draft now. The value is tied to one embedding model.
- A draft that counts as moved once it lies farther from the draft as judged than from its nearest entry. One entry decides.
- No automatic rule. A stale low judgement then stays until the drafter or the verifier has it given again.

Accepted cost: "half" is a choice to test, the rule by meaning is jumpy where few entries lie near the draft, and some entries are judged twice.

## 4. A new judgement at any time

The drafter and the verifier can each have sdd judge again at any time, also after a small change. One changed sentence can change what bears on a draft, and neither rule of decision 3 sees every such change.

Accepted cost: the judge calls each such call spends.

## 5. The instruction names the new check

The instruction sdd serves with a check states: whenever a change gives the draft a fundamentally different shape, a new graph check must be run. The rules of decision 3 act only when a check or the verifier's reading runs, so the check after such a change is a duty of the agent.

## 6. Marks before the judge holds thresholds

A judge without valid thresholds marks up to five lines as likely bearing: the five highest-scored, and of them only those scored 0.5 or higher. Where fewer reach 0.5, fewer are marked. Once thresholds hold, the mark goes to every line whose score reaches the relevant threshold, as 20261121-223938-d-tac-rqi decides.

That plan names these marks as not part of it. The replays of 20261121-205340-d-tac-gyk, from which each graph learns its thresholds, put 3.2 to 5.6 candidates per check at or above the learned line for likely bearing. Five marks are the size of a learned marking.

Rejected:

- No mark until thresholds hold. The first checks on a machine, or after a change of judge, then carry no duty to answer.
- The five highest-scored whatever their score. On a draft with nothing related, five unrelated entries demand an answer.

Accepted cost: 0.5 is a raw score, and raw scores differ between judge models. The rule is a stopgap until thresholds hold.

## 7. Marks without scores

When the judge failed or is switched off (`verify.prefilter: false`), the first five unscored lines of the merged order carry the mark and owe an answer, and an entry the judge did score keeps its place. One rule covers every check without scores. This confirms decision 5 of 20261121-223938-d-tac-rqi for a judge that is off.

Rejected:

- No marks when the judge is off. An agent that opens nothing then passes every check of that project.
- Marks by vector similarity above a level. That is a second marking rule and one more value to tune.

Accepted cost: on a draft with nothing related, five lines demand an answer anyway.

## 8. A check without an embedding model

A check searches with what the project has. With an embedding model it runs the vector searches, the keyword searches and the topic listings. Without one it runs the keyword searches and the topic listings, and its result states that no search by meaning ran because no embedding model is set.

The setting `verify.brief_all_below` is retired. A config that still holds it loads without error, and sdd shows the key as ignored, as `sdd config` lists a retired key today. This follows the tolerant config reads of 20260921-174543-d-cpt-tv9.

This changes the consequence 20261121-221907-d-tac-4rg states, that a check on a small graph needs the embedding model, and answers the point that plan leaves open on the setting. It also narrows that plan's criterion that every check runs one vector search with the draft's own text: a check runs that search where an embedding model is set.

Today a capture's check on a graph of 100 or more live entries is refused without an embedding model (`checkEmbedder` in `pkg/application/gather.go`). A graph below 100 live entries gets every live brief and no search.

Without a model, the rule by the draft's meaning in decision 3 does not run, because it reads the vector search. The rule per entry, the call of decision 4 and the instruction of decision 5 remain.

Rejected:

- An embedding model required for every check. A fresh project could not capture before one is set.
- The all-entries list kept for small graphs without a model. The second code path that 20261121-221907-d-tac-4rg removes would stay.

Accepted cost: without a model, an entry worded differently from the agent's keywords is found only through its topic.

## Not measured

- The value "half" in decision 3, and how often each of its two rules fires.
- How many judge calls kept judgements save per capture.
- How many lines the rule of decision 6 marks per check, on any judge model.

## Not covered

- The form of the call in decision 4, a tool of its own or an argument of the check, and which entries one call covers.
- The verdicts of the far floor, the line of 20261121-205340-d-tac-gyk at or below which a check sets a candidate aside. `Reading.BelowFloor` reads them by the same hash today. Whether they stay when the draft changes is not decided here.
- The order of entries only a topic listing found when no embedding model gives their similarity to the draft.
- Which searches run again for the verifier's reading when the capture's checks passed different phrases and terms.

## Where the pieces live

- `internal/model`, pure: `Reading` keeps scores per judge and entry across draft versions, with the draft as judged and its 20 nearest entries. Functions beside it name the entries with a low judgement that are due a second look, from the new merged order, the topics and references the draft gained, and the two lists of 20. The marks of decisions 6 and 7 belong to the shared placement of 20261121-223938-d-tac-rqi. `verify.brief_all_below` leaves `VerifyConfig` and joins `model.RetiredConfigKeys` in `internal/model/config.go`, so a config that holds it reads without error, `sdd config` shows it as no longer used and `sdd config set` refuses it.
- `pkg/application`: `gatherOn` and `planRanking` send the judge the candidates without a judgement and those due a second look. `planRanking` runs the capture's searches for the verifier's reading. `gatherSearches` leaves out the vector searches where no embedding model is set, in place of the refusal of `checkEmbedder`. The call of decision 4 runs the judge for a capture outside a check.
- `internal/presenters`: the line that no search by meaning ran, and the instruction of decision 5 beside the instruction of decision 1 of 20261121-223938-d-tac-rqi.
- Shells, parsing only: `pkg/mcpapp` and `internal/cliapp` pass the call of decision 4 on.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The deep verifier's reading runs the capture's searches against the final draft, and the merged order over them places its candidates.
- [ ] A judgement given in a capture is used by every later check and by the verifier's reading of that capture, after any change to the draft.
- [ ] The judge receives only candidates without a judgement and entries due a second look, and scores them against the latest draft.
- [ ] An entry with a kept low judgement is judged again when the searches of a new check or of the verifier's reading rank it among the first 20 of the merged order.
- [ ] An entry with a kept low judgement is judged again when the draft gains a topic the entry stands under, or a reference one reference step from the entry.
- [ ] When fewer than half of the 20 entries nearest to the draft as judged are among the 20 nearest to the current draft, every entry with a low judgement is judged again and the current draft becomes the draft as judged.
- [ ] No rule of decision 3 judges an entry again whose judgement places it as likely bearing.
- [ ] The value "half" is checked against recorded draft versions before the change ships, and the done signal records the result.
- [ ] The drafter and the verifier can each have sdd judge again at any time.
- [ ] The instruction served with a check states that a change which gives the draft a fundamentally different shape requires a new graph check.
- [ ] A judge without valid thresholds marks at most five lines as likely bearing, each scored 0.5 or higher.
- [ ] When the judge failed or is off, the first five unscored lines of the merged order carry the mark, and an entry the judge did score keeps its place.
- [ ] A check without an embedding model runs the keyword searches and the topic listings on a graph of any size, and states that no search by meaning ran because no embedding model is set.
- [ ] A config that sets `verify.brief_all_below` loads without error, the setting has no effect, and sdd shows it as ignored.
