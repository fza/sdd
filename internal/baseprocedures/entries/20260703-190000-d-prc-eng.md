Engage is the universal entry move for any session anchored on a graph entry: explore it, implement it, evaluate it, supersede it, check other work against it. One flow for every intent. Establish the anchor, receive its chains in full, brief the user, put candidate moves to them at the junction. The engagement ends by dispatching: the picked move runs as its own procedure. Capture entered with `anchor`, `supersedes` or `closes` start inputs covers most acting-on moves. Or the engagement concludes with an explicit "nothing further".

## unit: anchor

Establish what this engagement is about. A resolved anchor advances this step. A seeded anchor (dispatched from another move, or passed as a known entry ID) auto-advances past this prompt. You see it only when the anchor still needs resolving.{{if .anchorHint}} The user's pointer: "{{.anchorHint}}".{{end}} Report `anchor`: one entry, the subject the brief and the junction key on. Report `targets` for connected entries engaged alongside; their chains are served too. Report `goal` when the user voiced what they want out of it.

- **Entry ID given**: report it directly.
- **Pointer or phrase given**: search the graph from several angles: different phrasings, plus any known term or ID. Dialogue with the user toward picking the centre. The candidates are theirs to choose from, not yours to assume.
- **Action verb only** ("keep going", "let's evaluate"): recent dialogue or an active WIP marker resolves to a clear entry? Propose it. Otherwise ask which entry. Recency ("the last thing we shipped") does not resolve an anchor; it breaks in a shared graph. Ambiguity resolves in dialogue with the user.

## unit: brief

Anchor and surroundings, served in full. Read them. Summaries elsewhere are pointers; this is the material:

{{.entryChains}}

**Widen before you brief.** Search the graph from several angles for what the chains above do not name: a concept phrase from the anchor, the goal phrase, a key term from the chain. Record searches and what they surfaced in `widenReport`. Inspect any promising result in full before it shapes the brief. Surroundings would overwhelm this dialogue's context? Dispatch the explore procedure to a disposable context (a sub-agent running against this server) with the targets and a goal. Fold its briefing in.

Then synthesize `brief`, shaped by the anchor's kind and the goal{{if .goal}} (the user's goal: {{.goal}}){{end}}:

- **Plan (or plan-shaped directive) + implementation intent**: an AC-status table: ✓ done (evidence: partial done signals in the chain), ◐ partial, ☐ remaining. The contract is the union of the entry's acceptance criteria and every open augmenting directive in its downstream chain. Fold each augment in as a row. Prior work paused at a slice boundary? The most recent partial done signal usually carries pickup notes; surface them.
- **Signal + orientation intent**: a short narrative. What this is about, current status, what happened since, what is around it. Close with the orienting question: what does this need?
- **Contract or aspiration + check intent**: a divergence list over the named target work: alignments, divergences, ambiguous items. Surface alignments as well as divergences. A divergence-only list is a hunting expedition, not a fair check.
- **Done signal + evaluate intent**: what landed (from the body and what it closes) against what was committed. Inner lens: did it meet the target? Outer lens: what did it surface?

These are the recognized shapes, not a closed set. Kind and intent pair differently? Adapt: name what the user engages on and what outcome they want; that pair selects the shape. Present the brief in the user's language.

## unit: moves

{{if eq .dialogueMode "autonomous"}}Decide what should happen with {{.anchor}}.{{else}}Put the junction to the user: what should happen with {{.anchor}}?{{end}} Surface candidate moves that fit the anchor's kind and current state: a short prioritized offer, not a menu dump. Two flavors:

- **Acting on it**: the outcome is a graph change against the entry. Capture a decision on a gap or question, implement a plan or directive, supersede, close via done, decompose, refine, augment mid-implementation.
- **Using it as a lens**: the outcome is findings about other work. Evaluate against a closed commitment, compliance-check against a contract, alignment-check against an aspiration, progress-check against a focus.

Closed entries stay engageable. Closure ends the open-status derivation, not the dialogue; a closed plan still anchors a retrospective. {{if eq .dialogueMode "autonomous"}}Pick the move the anchor's state calls for and answer with a one-line reason: **move**{{else}}Wait for the user's explicit pick and relay their answer verbatim: **move**{{end}} carries `selectedMove` (the move in their words). **conclude** ends the engagement with an explicit nothing-further. **abort** abandons a mis-anchored engagement. Picked move starts a follow-up procedure (a capture against the anchor, an explore over the neighborhood)? Start it with this instance as `parent`, so the session log records the lineage.
