Capture is the shared spine for recording signals and decisions through dialogue. Assemble a draft grounded in the graph. Play it back for the user's explicit confirmation. Judge the writing guide's findings on the approved content and fold them in. Write through the pre-flight gate (the prose draft verifier over the grounding reading, where `verify.mode` enables it); its override is reachable only through the user. Verify the generated summary for fidelity. The guide runs once on the content the user approved; a later adjust gets a delta check of its changed lines. In an autonomous dialogue the guide and the verifier run before playback, since no dialogue edits follow. Supersede, close and anchored captures are not separate moves. They are this procedure entered with start inputs (`supersedes`, `closes`, `anchor`, a pre-selected `kind`). The instruction units below are the authoritative capture guidance, served step by step against the running draft.

## unit: assemble

### lane: intro

Draft one entry. One idea, dialogue first. Report the fields below; one batched report is fine. The step re-serves, naming exactly what is missing, until the draft is complete and mechanically valid.{{if eq .dialogueMode "guided"}}

No decision owes a reason, and no directive owes the user's words for its intent. Words the user gives for a decision's reason go to `reasonWords`, and for a directive's intent to `intentWords`, quoted as the user said them; sdd writes them on the entry as theirs. The reason states why, never what: words stating the decision itself never fill `reasonWords`. You may propose a reason in `reason` and the intent value, inferred from the dialogue and the graph: the play-back shows them as your inference, the user's confirm adopts them as theirs, and their own words in `reasonWords` take the place of your reason. Never write words they did not pick or give. A wording change to the draft changes the body, never these quotes: report them again only with words the user gives for the reason or intent.{{else}}

{{if eq .dialogueMode "assisted"}}In assisted mode you draft and check the entry alone, as in auto, and the person approves it once at the end: after every check passes, sdd plays the draft back to them, and their approval writes it as theirs, `approved-by` them.{{else}}In auto mode you decide alone, and sdd marks the entry `decided-by: agent`.{{end}} A decision may state your own reason in `reason`, inferred from the dialogue and the graph; no kind owes one, and a signal carries none. A directive states its intent in `intent`, inferred the same way.{{if eq .dialogueMode "assisted"}} The person's approval adopts the reason and intent as theirs; to change them, they adjust the draft at the play-back. `participants` follow the guided rule: unreported, the entry names the local participant. No agent limit binds the draft, since the person approves it.{{else}} Leave `participants` unreported: sdd writes your client's actor. An agent alone writes every signal kind, and plans, activities and pending directives on the tactical and operational layers. It never approves, and never supersedes or closes an entry a person decided, except that a done closes anything. Any other draft parks for a person.{{end}}{{end}}
