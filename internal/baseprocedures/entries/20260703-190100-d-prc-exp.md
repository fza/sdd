Explore takes a set of entries that surfaced (a catch-up lane, an engage neighborhood, a topic cluster) and builds the picture: read wide, return narrow. Run it in a disposable context, a sub-agent connected to this server, so wide reading costs nothing. Caller passes targets and a goal. Only the compressed briefing travels back. No goal, no compression axis: the procedure does not start without one. Run inline, it still works; the caller then pays the context.

## unit: inspect

Mission: build the goal's picture from the graph. Targets and their chains, served in full:

{{.entryChains}}

The goal (verbatim): {{.goal}}

**Read the whole surface. Do not be shy.** Your context is disposable. Under-reading is the only real failure. Everything past the served depth renders as a one-liner. One-liners are pointers, not facts. Read in full (`show`) any chain node or search result that could bear on the goal before you judge its relevance.

**Iterate.** Search the graph from several angles: a concept phrase from the targets, the goal phrase, key terms from the chains, any known identifier. Read promising results in full. Full bodies name entries and concepts the first pass missed; each spawns the next angle. Stop when an iteration surfaces nothing new that bears on the goal.

Record per iteration what you searched and what it surfaced in `widenReport`. Record every entry you read in full in `inspectedIds`. The briefing stands on exactly those reads.

## unit: compress

Compress toward the goal. Select, do not editorialize. Per inspected entry: **keep** it (its content serves the goal), **drop** it (topically adjacent, does not serve the goal), or **mention it briefly** (not load-bearing, but flags a tension, blind spot or open question worth one line). Drop for goal-irrelevance, never for brevity. Unsure whether the outer dialogue needs an entry's context? Include it.

Structure `briefing` exactly:

```
## Goal
<the goal, verbatim; the caller checks its axis got applied>

## Targets
<per target: full-id · kind · layer · one-line status-aware summary>

## Picture
<how the kept entries connect: chains and relationships compressed
toward the goal, full IDs inline>

## Neighbors
<each kept search-surfaced entry: full-id, kind, layer, one-line summary,
one-line goal relevance>

## Mentioned briefly
<full-id and a one-line note each>

## Compression note
<one or two sentences: what was dropped and why, so the caller can ask>
```

Rules:

- Every kept or mentioned entry carries its full ID.
- Each kept entry carries enough context for the caller to dialogue and act on it without re-reading.
- No interpretation beyond goal-relevance: no move suggestions, no rankings, no proposed decisions. That is the caller's dialogue.
- Never omit an entry you judged load-bearing.
- Chains already saturate the goal and search added nothing? Say so in the compression note.
