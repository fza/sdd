Evaluate treats evaluation as real work, not a verdict appended to a done. Scope what this run judges, carry the judgment out with evidence, record the evaluation as work the graph can see. Two lenses, generic postures; the project gives their concrete meaning. **Inner is verification**: is the work sound, judged against the project's own guidelines? **Outer is validation**: is it the right thing, working in use, serving the people it's for? A run may cover one lens or both. Completeness is a coverage property computed across the graph; other runs and participants cover the rest. Anchor on the done signal, or on the closed commitment: its closing done and downstream arrive with the chains either way.

## unit: carryOut

Carry the scoped run out. Evaluation is work done now, part automated and part human, not a summary of impressions:

- **Inner: verification.** Judge whether the work is sound under the project's own guidelines. Check what the done signal claims against what its commitment demanded: acceptance criteria, augmenting directives in the chain, deviations named or omitted. Check the project's standards for this work type. Inspecting and running is host work: do it. What was inspected and run, and what came back, goes in `innerEvidence`. The judgment goes in `innerEvaluation`.
- **Outer: validation.** Judge whether it is the right thing: working in use, serving the people it's for. Run it end to end where that answers the question. Judgment needs a human (the user, the people the work serves)? Gather their read: a human attestation counts as evidence. What was observed and attested goes in `outerEvidence`. The judgment goes in `outerEvaluation`.

Report the lens(es) the plan scoped. At least one judgment must land, with evidence beside it: a judgment without evidence is an impression. Present the read to the user in their language: what held, what did not, what it taught.

2. Then capture each selected finding as its own entry, `surfaced-by` that evaluation done, anchored where the finding's subject lives.

Start every capture with this instance as `parent`. The answered dispatch seeds your `widenReport` into each child, so you draft without re-widening. **conclude** records nothing: use it for an evaluation that should not enter the graph at all. Work passed clean? Prefer record with no findings over conclude. Never invent findings to justify the run.
