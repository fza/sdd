# Deciding how to go forward: the directive kind

Directive records a decision on how to go forward. It acts on what was noticed, chooses among routes, sometimes overturns an earlier decision and changes direction. For most newcomers this kind has no familiar home: in most working records the decision lives between the lines, in documents, meeting notes or memory. Recording it keeps the project transparent about its why. Future reader finds what was decided, which alternatives were weighed, what carried the choice. It pictures current understanding, not a law: new input can change it. What it obliges afterwards is named by its `intent`. The three postures behave almost like three kinds sharing one form.

**The choice carries its why.** Directive reads as a choice made: what was decided, and the reasoning that carried it. Grounded in the entries it reasons from, every one referenced. Commits nothing the grounding does not support. Most directives are born in dialogue. Dialogue's material must stay retrievable from the entry: arguments raised, alternatives weighed and set aside. Summarize in the body; attach the detail when there is much. Directive retires or replaces an earlier commitment? Body says what changed in the world or the thinking. Restating what the retired entry said is not a rationale.

**Intent is chosen, never defaulted.** Three questions settle it:

- Something still has to happen because of this? `pending`. Commitment has a horizon; reader can say what fulfills it. Need not decide every detail on the way: later plans, gaps and decisions carry it out, each with own dialogue. It must carry the direction and the why those detail decisions lean on.
- A guideline that keeps applying, not a target that could be completed? `guiding`.
- Deciding was the whole of it: considered, ruled, finished in the ruling? `settled`. Body must say why nothing follows. That stated why alone separates deliberately terminal from silently dropped.

No default, on purpose: posture is not derivable from text, so a default would fabricate it. No check verifies fit between declared intent and narrative shape. Pending directive reading as perpetual orientation, or guiding one reading as bounded task: caught at drafting or not at all.

**A guiding directive binds.** Guideline is not decoration. Work and later entries must conform; violation is observable. "Nothing leaves the shop without a second person's sign-off" can be broken and seen broken. Stance nobody could violate pulls instead of pushes: it belongs among aspirations. Yet guiding directive is not mechanically followable like a checklist. It carries the why an area's day-to-day constraints derive from. "Accessibility governs all design work, because we must comply" is reasoning that narrower, checkable working rules hang under. Guiding directives stay few and broad. Guideline covers an area. Constraint firing at a recognizable recurring moment is narrower, living under it, not another guideline beside it.

**Retirement follows the posture.**

- Pending closes like work: delivering act closes it; later directive may close it with reasoning why nothing will be built; or a replacement supersedes it.
- Guiding is not completed by work. Superseded by the replacing guideline, or closed by a directive explaining why it no longer holds. Done closing a guideline is a category error.
- Settled only supersedes.

Reclassifying a directive that hardened into something else is supersession too, never an edit. Directive is the graph's universal retiring instrument: may close any decision, and the stable signal kinds, carrying the why in its body.

**Refining without replacing.** Directive may sharpen an active plan in place: adds to what the plan must satisfy, plan stays untouched, both retire together. This augmenting shape is first-class. Using supersession for a refinement fabricates a successor that does not exist.
