# Dispatching a piece of work: the activity kind

Activity records a commitment that a specific piece of work happens: a batch roasted, four bents raised, a review held. A decision: committed to, not noticed. Work lies ahead of the record. That it happened is a separate record: the done that closes it. Reader asks: what work is sent, when does it count as finished, what does it leave behind? Good activity answers all three, shortest.

**The choosing happened before the entry.** Dispatch presupposes a dialogue already held. Route was weighed and settled behind this record: in the plan it runs under, the decision it carries out, the exchange that shaped it. What remains is sending the work on its way, no design left to make while doing it. That is the kind's structural limit as much as its license. Activity has no place to keep a why. So a non-obvious choice turning up inside the dispatch keeps the entry a directive. Boundary is lossy in that direction: a why lost crossing it cannot be recovered later. But where truly no choice is left, activity is the right record, not a cautious fallback. Choice-shaped entry written to be safe documents a deliberation that never happened. Future reader will hunt for reasoning that was never there.

**The shape lives where the reader can reach it.** Activity works only because the work's form is already known: from the plan it runs under, from referenced entries, or from its own words when the work is self-evident. That keeps the body lean. References carry the context. Activity says what happens; chain behind it says why. It commits nothing those references do not support.

**Judge the form of the work, not of what comes out.** Emergent outputs do not disqualify an activity. Coffee roastery: "run Thursday's cupping with the new roaster" has a well-known form, though which lots make the cut is unknowable in advance. Session is the work; verdicts are its outputs. Measuring an activity by whether outcomes can be specified up front uses the wrong axis. It owes no slate of outcome criteria; it owes a completion condition.

**Say when it is finished.** Whole validation is one question: was it done? Stated completion condition makes that answerable. Coffee roastery: "finished when every sample lot has a verdict" hands the closer the exact line its record must meet. Work will wait its turn while the world moves? Condition says so, not a frozen snapshot. Scope knowable only at execution is stated as resolving then. A list written today would read as complete when it is not.

**Name what the work leaves behind.** Expected landing (artifact or state the work ends in) turns "did it happen?" into something the closing record confirms concretely. Purpose bounds the work? Say what it is for. Coffee roastery: "a trial batch roasted to learn whether the profile holds, not to commit to it" keeps deliberately non-committing work from being read as more.

**Sequence by prerequisite, not by calendar.** Work waits on something? Name it, not a date. Timber framing: "after the frame inspection passes". Named prerequisite stays true when the schedule slips. Date goes stale and says nothing about why work has not started.

**Research is dispatched, not enshrined.** Looking into something (research, evaluation, trial) is an activity like any other, not its own kind. Findings come back as own entries: facts, insights, gaps, open questions, each pointing where it belongs. Done closing the activity records only that the looking happened.

**How an activity resolves.** Closes like work: done recording the act closes it. That done covers the whole remit: each required part delivered, or its deviation named with reasoning. Silent omission is the failure. Work advancing it partway points at it without closing. Activity whose work will not be done: retired by whatever entry carries the reasoning why it stopped holding. Stated rationale qualifies the retirement, not the carrying kind. Replacement activity may supersede it: documented path, rarely taken. Other direction: activity may close a signal its dispatch plainly resolves, such as a deviation whose remedy needed only sending. But closing an entry means saying why it stopped holding, and activity carries no reasoning of its own. Retirement that needs arguing belongs to an entry built to carry the why.
