# Querying for input: the question kind

Question records a demand for input: something the project needs to know and does not. Put in the record so later work (session, meeting, dialogue) finds it, reasons toward answers, explores the space it opens. A signal: noticed, not committed to. Project is not ready to decide and says so by asking, because an unasked question gets answered anyway: silently, by whatever the work happens to do. Open questions sit beside open gaps on the working surface. They carry the demand into every future session until something meets it. Unknown becoming explicit is a consequence of asking, not the purpose. Reader wants: what input is asked for, why needed, what could settle it? Good question answers all three, shortest.

**As specific as the demand that raised it.** Input is asked for because concrete work needs it. Question's scope follows that demand's scope. Roastery shaping a marketing campaign gains nothing from "what is the life-goal of our customers?": interesting, attached to nothing. "What joy can our products bring into our customers' lives?" asks for exactly the input the campaign needs. Framing carries more than focus. Question's own words are the handle a later answer must take up: settler names it by what it asks, and a claimed resolution is checked against that framing. Unknown posed too broadly for any answer to demonstrably settle fails its purpose at writing.

**Name the demand and where it arose.** Questions come out of concrete work: review surfacing an ambiguity, work accepted with a doubt attached, customer's ask nobody could answer. Say which work needs the input. Point at the entry that raised the question. Candidate answers can be re-reasoned later; where the demand came from cannot be reconstructed once forgotten. Layer names depth of the unknown itself, not of the occasion. Doubt surfacing in day-to-day handovers may open a conceptual question.

**A question exists to be brought into dialogue.** Answer rarely waits somewhere to be looked up. It is reached in dialogue: later session picks the question up, people at the table work it out. So a question may seed that dialogue: candidate answers, directions worth weighing, a proposed move ("think about a parent survey"). Path to an answer already visible? Name the move that would settle it: a trial, a measurement, an unknown answerable only through a real attempt. All of it enters marked as unsettled. Question may not pre-empt the dialogue. Commits to a route (imperative with owner and horizon)? It is a decision in a signal's clothes. Committing to the survey, after that dialogue, belongs in its own decision. Question's job: make the dialogue happen.

**One demand, with its strands.** Unit is the unknown, not the theme. Two questions different resolutions would answer: two entries. One demand can have several angles that stand or fall with the same resolution. Those travel together, each stated. Line need not be perfect: question is temporary, and its answer can split what turned out to be two. Guard is against multiplying entries into ceremony, not against bundling. Question is one face of a larger unknown the graph already holds? Say so, point at its siblings, so answering work sees the whole shape.

**How a question resolves.** Stays open until something meets the demand. Most often a decision: answer is a choice, recorded with its why. Sometimes knowledge alone: a fact or insight dissolves it by answering, no choice required. Learning the timber yard stocks only one grade dissolves "which grades can we source?" without anyone deciding. Decision may also close a question that will not be pursued, carrying the why. Sharpening a still-open unknown: supersession by a refined question, not closure. Every path owes traceability, not certification: answering entry can be checked against the question's words; nothing adjudicates whether the answer is right. Question never closes anything itself: asking is not answering.
