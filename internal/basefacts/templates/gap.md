# Recording a deviation: the gap kind

Gap records a deviation: actual state of something set against expected state, with a distance that demands further action. A signal: noticed, not committed to. Open gaps are the project's working surface: together they show where attention is owed. Reader asks: what was observed and how, what was expected and from where, what is the deviation? Good gap answers all three, shortest.

**Both sides, each with its source.** Actual side is evidence. State what was observed and how: what was done, where the information came from. Concrete enough to reproduce. Expected side names its source, in one of three shapes:

- committed somewhere in the graph: point at it
- natural expectation nobody wrote down: state it plainly. Demanding a recorded commitment behind "the service should be running" is ceremony, not rigor.
- new information entering through this gap: name where it came from (customer's ask, regulation, call)

Never prop a side up with an invented pointer.

**The world input is the substance.** Irreplaceable content is what the entry carries in from the world with no other record. Most often the observation; also the expected side's source whenever nothing else holds it. Deviation itself can be re-reasoned later from two well-stated sides. So weight goes into framing world input right and recording it truthfully. Deviation needs stating, not elaborating. New expectation reaching beyond this one deviation (governs more work, or project adopts it as commitment)? It becomes its own entry through dialogue; gap points at it.

**Reasoning enters only under its own name.** Everything past the two sides is reasoning: suspected causes, candidate remedies, weighed trade-offs. What dialogue surfaced may enter, marked as unsettled candidates, not observations. Invent nothing beyond the stated deviation to make the entry fuller. Skipping the mark costs asymmetrically. Inherited observation can be re-checked against the same world. Inherited unmarked conclusion becomes the floor a later reading reasons from; if wrong, nothing in the entry reveals it. Marking is enough. Competing candidates may stay side by side; none needs deleting.

**Observed, never decided.** Entry commits to one remedy (this will be done, by then, this way)? A decision is smuggled in a signal's clothes. Situation cannot be described honestly without a choice and its justification? Choice is its own entry; gap keeps the deviation. Same line runs through resolution: closing record that had to make a choice reveals a decision skipped between gap and closure.

**Record the act, not the readings.** Stating the actual side does not license precision. Cite the observation and its result, not figures left over from checking. Numbers in the body bind every later reader to reconcile them; they belong only when a figure is itself the claim. Both rules hold at once and pull apart by design. Two sides govern what a gap records; craft of stating them governs how. Appeal to one never waives the other.

**Frame the level that needs to change.** Gap framed on a surface symptom invites a closure that fixes the underlying piece while the symptom's half quietly lives on. Frame the deviation at the level where, once resolved, nothing of the concern remains. Write it sharp enough to check a claimed resolution against: gap's framing is the checklist its closer is measured by. Gap too broad for anything to demonstrably close fails its purpose at writing.

**How a gap resolves.** Stays open until something closes it. Normally a decision addressing it; deeper layer, firmer that expectation: strategic deviation is never resolved by execution alone. Or a completed act directly, when resolving truly needed no choice. Gap can also stop applying because its situation changed. Whatever entry states that, and why, closes it. Without such entry the gap stays open. Sharpening understanding of a still-open deviation: supersession by a refined gap, not closure. Gap may itself close an entry: its deviation shows an earlier entry no longer holds. Then it closes that entry directly and says why, no extra entry between.

**Honesty in the small fields.** Confidence grades the two sides. Both named and detailed: high is natural. Low or medium: one side uncertain, needs more exploration; body names which. Layer names the depth of the deviation, not its urgency.
