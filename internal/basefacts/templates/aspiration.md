# Orienting the work: the aspiration kind

Aspiration records a direction the work is pulled toward. Not a target the project will reach: a quality it keeps moving toward over time. A decision: committed to, not noticed. Project chooses which way its work should lean; later work is read against that choice. Reader asks two things: which way does this pull, and how do I tell if a piece of work moves with it or against it? Good aspiration is the shortest record that lets someone not in the room run that check. Setting direction for the whole is strategic almost by nature. Most aspirations name the strategic layer.

**A pull that binds nothing.** Aspiration exercises pull: tells the work which way to go without binding any single piece. No task conforms to it; none can violate it. Village bakery: "every loaf we sell was baked that morning from grain whose farm we can name". It leans buying, baking and hiring one way without obliging any one delivery. No inspection could pass or fail a loaf against it. Direction carries no completion gate. It outlives every piece of work aligned with it. So it stays a live commitment, not an item to work off a list.

**Alignment is a degree, never a verdict.** Asking "does this comply?" of an aspiration is a category error. Question is "how well does this align?". Answer is a position on a gradient, not pass or fail. Two consequences:

- Detour is not a violation. Step moving obliquely, even away for a stretch, is coherent if explicable against the direction.
- Aspiration never gates work. Proposal strains against a pull? Tension is material for dialogue, never grounds to block.

**One pull among several.** Project holds several aspirations at once. Pulls straining against each other are the normal case, not a defect. Coffee roastery: growth against brand purity, reach against depth of relationship. Together they form a field of tensions, not one target. Work resolves each tension case by case. So a new aspiration positions itself against the pulls it lives beside. Reinforces one, or runs on an unrelated axis? Nothing to defend. Strains against an active pull? Entry says so and argues the coexistence. Writing the tension in is the craft; leaving it unspoken is the fault.

**The pull is argued, never derived.** No reading of the record produces an aspiration. It says what the people involved want the work to become; only they can settle that, between them. So the grounding rule bends here by design. Aspiration may synthesize direction the record does not yet hold. Demanding every strategic pull trace back to something written prevents strategic thinking. Ground what can be grounded; own the rest as the project's reach. In exchange the entry owes the argument itself: what understanding of the project the direction rests on, what was weighed on the way. Summarize in the body. Point at a fuller articulation where one exists; do not restate it. Aim nobody can say why the project wants is an aim nobody holds. Aspirations sit in tension by design. Pull whose reasoning was never had will not survive its first conflict with another. Tension is resolved by argument, not lookup. Entry with no argument brings nothing to that table.

**Concrete enough to score against.** Direction opens as a plain declarative sentence in present tense, stated as if already the project's nature. Rest of the body makes it checkable. Name what the pull pushes against: habits and counter-forces it exists to resist. Give a working test where one exists. Coffee roastery: "for any new offering: does it bring the drinker closer to the grower?". Framing toward something and away from something are equally valid. Sometimes the negative framing carries more energy. Whole entry's measure is one line: someone not there must be able to score a later decision against it.

**The check when the record runs out.** Active aspirations together are the surface the project's movement is checked against for direction. Also preparation for dialogue. Novel situation the record does not back? Weigh the proposal against them: which pulls it moves toward, which away from, where tension sits. A guideline could not hold this seat without collapsing the direction check into a compliance check. The role sets two demands. Pull too vague to score against contributes nothing when the check runs. Active set stays small: few enough that whoever runs the check holds every pull at once.

**Never completed; retired with its why.** Aspirations shift when understanding of what the project does shifts. Slowest-moving decisions, but not fixed doctrine. A pull is extended over time and changes when a misalignment is observed. Never completion: no act of work reaches a state where the pull is fulfilled. Nothing completes an aspiration. Retirement is its only exit: superseded by the evolved direction replacing it, or closed by whatever entry carries why the direction stopped holding. Stated rationale qualifies the retirement, not the carrying kind.

**Confidence tracks how far the pull has proved itself.** New direction may honestly start low. It rises as work repeatedly aligns with it and that alignment proves useful. High-confidence aspiration carries more force in dialogue. Work pulling against a well-proven direction signals something is off: examine it, do not wave it through.
