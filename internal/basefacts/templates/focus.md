# Declaring current attention: the focus kind

Focus records what the project is advancing right now and who carries it: an involvement declaration. A decision: committed to, not noticed. Project commits its present attention. Record makes visible what otherwise lives only in heads: current work, who is engaged on each piece, how long this holds. Reader asks exactly that: what is driven, who carries each piece and what waits for pickup, what ends this period? Good focus answers all three, shortest.

**A focus points at work; it does not contain it.** Targets are existing entries the project actually drives this period, most often plans and decisions. Focus names them; their content stays theirs. Engagement and dates live in the focus, never on the target. Target stays usable in every later period. Chain of foci over time records how attention moved. Focus stands on its involvements. References and topic labels are optional; where present they carry grounding, not the work.

**Who, what, and when live in the structure.** Each involvement joins one target with the people engaged on it and the window it runs in. People and window stated once for the whole focus are defaults every involvement inherits. Involvement carries its own only where it truly differs: own people when engagement splits across targets, own window when a piece runs on another clock. Never a restatement of the shared one. Engagement is plural by design. Several people can angle at one target; work actually distributes that way. Kind has no single-owner slot. Timber-framing shop: "through November the shop is attending to the barn-frame commission: Mara drives the joinery layout, and the client walkthrough runs from the 20th". That is the shape: one period, several pieces, each carried by whoever actually carries it.

**Open for pickup is a statement.** Involvement may deliberately name no one. Target is in scope this period, waiting for whoever takes it up. Same shop: "the timber-sourcing decision is open for whoever picks it up." Differs from saying nothing, which merely inherits the shared default. Deliberate blank is first-class. It beats dropping the target or guessing an owner. Focus made entirely of open-for-pickup targets reads two ways: deliberate pickup board, or declaration still missing its people. Body says which.

**What the period sets down is the half only dialogue produces.** Naming what the project attends to is easy, mostly uncontested: active work names itself. Agreeing what waits needs the people in the room. Setting something down costs someone: a commitment they carry stalls, a piece they wanted this period does not make it. That negotiation is where a focus's why comes from. Later reader cannot reconstruct it from the target list. Focus written without that conversation reads as a list of everything: no focus at all. So body names what was deliberately left out and why. Missing candidate then reads as a choice, not an oversight.

**The body carries why now.** Structure answers who, what, when. Body carries the period's narrative: what this stretch is about, why these targets over other open commitments, how they relate (what ships first, what unblocks what, what runs in parallel). It also states what would close the focus in project terms, functionally where it can: met when the thing works, not merely when it exists.

**Honest about the window.** Window with a start and no end says what most periods truly can: began now, no fixed finish. End date belongs on a truly bounded push: a stretch with a real finish line. Even the pace undetermined? Honest move is no window at all; body says work proceeds as time permits. Window is a claim like any other. State what is actually known about the period, not what the form seems to invite.

**Two ways out, and the drafter makes the call.** Focus is not work to be completed. It is a standing declaration of current attention, replaced when priorities change. That is why it is its own kind, not a dispatched piece of work. Ends one of two ways: superseded by the replacing focus when priorities shift mid-period, or closed by a done signal when the period ran its course. Like any entry, it can be retired by whatever entry carries the reasoning why it stopped holding. Nothing arbitrates between paths. Call belongs to whoever writes the ending record, deliberately. Period may close without its targets completing: attention moved on, legitimate. But closing record says so, not leaving the shortfall to inference.

**Replacement and coexistence each say so.** Focus superseding another names what changed: which finish line was overtaken, why the new shape displaced the old. Replacement sharing no targets with its predecessor is a wholesale priority shift. It reads as an oversight unless body names it as one. Parallel foci are legitimate: a project can attend to two strands at once. New focus not replacing an existing one says so and records the neighbor as related. Silence reads as displacement.

**The layer sets the reach, and foci can nest.** No layer is pinned. Higher-layer focus reaches further, less specific: broad direction of attention over many months. Lower one is shorter, more concrete: particular work, particular people. Most land tactical: arranging current work is tactical. Foci nest. Broader period holds narrower ones inside, each with own window. Narrower focus ending does not end the one containing it.
