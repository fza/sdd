# Recording a synthesis: the insight kind

Insight records a synthesis: understanding reached by connecting what is already recorded. Pattern across entries, cause read behind them, bound on an earlier claim. A signal: noticed, not committed to. Changes the reading of the project, not its obligations. Open insights together are the project's current understanding of itself. Reading that no longer holds but stays open makes the picture wrong. Reader asks: what is now understood, what does it rest on, does it change anything ahead? Good insight answers all three, shortest.

**The connection is the substance, and the reasoning is the part only this entry holds.** Entry adds the relation among the records it reasons over. They are its ground: each pointed at, none restated. Repeating a referenced entry is duplication debt: copies drift, later reader cannot tell which telling governs. References are load-bearing, not decoration: remove them and the entry visibly loses its ground. But material is only half the synthesis. Dialogue that reached it is kept nowhere else. So the entry is that reasoning's durable representation. Its standard is re-derivability: later session reaches the entry's claims again from its writing, attachments and references alone.

**One synthesis, one entry.** Drafting fuses two things? Split and cross-reference. Commonest fusion welds a deviation to the reading behind it. "The delivered stock keeps failing grade" is a gap demanding action. "The failing deliveries all come from the merchant with the fastest quoted turnaround" is the pattern read across them. Two entries pointing at each other: deviation can be resolved while understanding stands, and corrected without reopening the deviation. Same split for a reading and the question it raises: each retires on its own terms.

**Understanding, never commitment.** Insight may walk far into what-to-do territory: candidate direction, alternatives the dialogue surfaced. All of it may stand as exploration. It may not commit. Entry acquires an owner and a horizon (this will be done, by someone, by then)? A decision is smuggled in a signal's clothes. The choice belongs in its own entry with own dialogue. Insight keeps the understanding and the explored space.

**Captured because it changes something.** Connection earns its entry by making a difference: sharpens a coming choice, redirects work, unseats an assumption. Relation that alters nothing when read is trivia; trivia dilutes the record. Difference need not be claimed now. Nothing is owed on an insight, so recording it and deliberately not acting is a legitimate end state: hold the understanding until the work it bears on arrives. Saying so keeps the restraint readable as deliberate, not neglect.

**Narrowing an earlier reading.** Understanding sharpens by narrowing as often as by replacing. New material read against a recorded reading may yield no contradiction but a more specific claim: it holds, inside bounds nobody had found yet. "The sourdough proofs in four hours" does not become false when winter shows six. It becomes a claim about the summer kitchen. Narrowing entry owes that boundary, stated precisely. "That reading went too far" makes every later reader renegotiate the whole claim. "It holds in the summer kitchen" leaves the surviving part usable. Earlier entry stays open, still true where observed.

**What it closes.** Insight may close a question by answering it. Closing entry carries the answer's context: restates the question's framing, or says which unknown it settles. Close is then readable without opening the question. Carried context earns the close, not correctness. Record shows what answered and where the answer came from; whoever reasons from it later judges if right. Insight may also close an entry its reading overturns: a fact or earlier reading the synthesis shows no longer holds. Same context owed: body says why the target stopped holding.

**Nothing owed after the reading.** Open insight demands nothing. It sits beside the deviations and unknowns where attention is owed, not among them. Open because it still holds, not because anyone must act. So it is retired, not fulfilled: nothing intrinsic to resolve. Sharpened or corrected understanding supersedes it, pointing at the material that changed the picture and saying why the reasoning moved. Closing is for a reading that goes entirely. Being acted on is not a retirement. Commitment taking the reading up, or completed work running off it, points at the insight and leaves it standing: understanding is now load-bearing, not obsolete. Insight retires when the reading stopped holding. Any entry may carry that if it says why: material moved, or reasoning had a flaw. Restating the insight, or noting its age, is not a rationale.

**Honesty in the small fields.** Confidence grades the evidence behind the synthesis, not the conviction. Lower grade: connection needs more instances or a check; body names what would firm it. Layer names where reasoning landed, not where inputs sit. Reading drawn from several operational deviations and one survey result is strategic if the thinking arrived there.
